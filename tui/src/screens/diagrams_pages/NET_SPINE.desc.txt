The spine is the top tier of a leaf-spine fabric: every leaf switch has a fibre to every spine, so any rack reaches any other in two hops. One spine is a 2RU box holding a single 51.2 Tb/s switch ASIC (Broadcom Tomahawk 5, Marvell Teralynx, Cisco Silicon One), 64 OSFP 800G optics, fans and two power supplies; ECMP spreads traffic across all spines. Tickers: AVGO, MRVL, ANET, CSCO, NVDA, COHR, LITE, FN, CRDO, CLS.
