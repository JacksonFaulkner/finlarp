The server is the 8-GPU node every AI cluster is built from: an HGX baseboard with eight GPUs joined by NVLink through NVSwitch chips, a host board with two CPUs and PCIe switches, one 400-800 Gb/s NIC per GPU plus two DPUs, and cold plates that carry about a kilowatt per GPU into liquid. Traffic leaves through the NICs to the leaf switch. Tickers: NVDA, AVGO, ALAB, SMCI, DELL, VRT.
