# 6. Dennard scaling arithmetic (textbook)

  scale all dimensions and voltage by 1/k (k ~ 1.4 per node):
    gate delay          x 1/k          so clock can rise ~1.4x
    capacitance         x 1/k
    voltage             x 1/k
    power per gate      x 1/k^2        (C V^2 f, with f x k)
    gates per area      x k^2
    power density       x 1            the free lunch; ended mid-2000s when Vt and oxide could not keep scaling
  after: Ioff ~ exp(-Vt / SS-ish) so Vdd stalled near ~1 V; more transistors at same power = dark
  silicon, multicore

# 7. Risks and positioning

  * Mature-node overcapacity from China adds price pressure (UMC wafer shipments +12.3% in 2025,
  revenue +2.3%).
  * Tickers: TSM (28nm still a large line), INTC (first 22nm FinFET, 2011), UMC, GFS, SOI.PA, SMIC (0981.HK).
  * FD-SOI is a niche (IoT, RF, auto); the mass market moved to FinFET at 16/14nm and below.
