# 1. Xtacking: NAND on a separate logic wafer

YMTC's Xtacking bonds the NAND array onto a separate logic wafer, so the circuits under the array do not limit the stack. Reports put mass production at about 267-270 layers on Xtacking 4.0, with 300+ layers in development. Layer counts differ across sources.

# 2. Beyond 300 layers

The X5-9080 line is reported to pass 300 layers by bonding three decks, trading some throughput for more bits per wafer.

# 3. The DRAM pivot

Reports say the Phase 3 Wuhan fab splits output roughly evenly between NAND and DRAM. TweakTown reports a plan for 9,600 MT/s low-power DRAM on 4F2 cells, with no announced timeline. Treat the DRAM plan as early and unconfirmed.

# 4. Position

Counterpoint Research puts YMTC at about 14% of NAND bits, ahead of Kioxia and Micron. YMTC is private; no ticker here.
