# 1. HBF: flash in an HBM package

SanDisk co-developed high-bandwidth flash (HBF) with SK hynix: NAND stacked in an HBM-style package, so an AI inference chip gets far more capacity per GPU at a bandwidth below HBM.

# 2. The spec

The first HBF spec came out through the Open Compute Project in August 2026. It covers 8- and 16-high NAND stacks up to 512 GB, with three bandwidth tiers from about 0.4 to 3.0 TB/s, plus the xPU-HBF host interface. Google and Tenstorrent joined the workstream.

# 3. Timing

Samples of the first HBF inference devices are expected in early 2027. This is a spec and a roadmap, not a product you can buy.
