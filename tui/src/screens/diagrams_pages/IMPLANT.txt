 │   ↓ ↓ ↓ ↓ ↓                                 *****                                              │
 │  ┌──────────────┐ surface                 ***     ***                                          │
 │  │ . .          │  ◀ nuclear + electronic ***       ***        peak sits at projected range Rp │
 │  │   . ▒▒▒▒     │    stopping slows ions **           **       energy up ──▶ Rp deeper         │

  (the beam leaves vacancies and interstitials; ANNEAL puts dopants on lattice sites, heals crystal)

# 4. The physics

  dose            Q = (1/qA) * integral I(t) dt      ions per cm2, time integral of beam current [2]
  magnet          r = m v / (q B)                   only the right mass/charge passes the slit (textbook)
  energy          E = q V                           each charge gains q x accelerating volts (textbook)
  channeling      ions running along crystal planes go too deep; tilt or pre-amorphization limits it
  activation      spike anneal: ramp >= 75 C/s, ~1 s soak, high activation, little diffusion [4]
  laser anneal    10-200 us up to about 1350 C (vendor claim); +laser after spike raised activation [4]
  ms anneal       millisecond anneals remove implant damage poorly, leakage risk [4]

# 5. By the numbers: the three implanter classes

 ┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
 │ class          energy                       dose (ions/cm2)   beam current                          │
 │ high current   0.1 - 100 keV                1e14 - 1e17       up to ~25-30 mA                       │
 │ medium current 3 - 500 keV (some to ~600)   1e11 - 1e14       1 uA - 5 mA (Wikipedia: 10 uA - 2 mA) │
 │ high energy    100 keV - 5 MeV (to 10 MeV)  1e10 - 1e13       lower, deep wells                     │
 └─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
  (approximate ranges from patents, Wikipedia and Axcelis; the sources say not strict) [1][2][3]
  Highest dose with economical throughput: about 1e16 /cm2 [3]

# 7. Who, structure, chokepoints

  Applied Materials (AMAT)      owns Varian Semiconductor (bought 2011); the incumbent leader [7]
  Axcelis (ACLS)                Purion line; implant was 98.2% of FY2025 revenue (aggregator figure);
                                agreed to merge with Veeco (VECO) Oct 2025, pending in 2026 sources [5]
  SEN (Sumitomo Heavy 6302.T)   50/50 JV of Axcelis and Sumitomo; leader in Japan (2004 10-K) [6]
  SiC power devices             one analysis puts Axcelis at ~70-80% of SiC implant (could not verify)
  Chokepoints: a near-duopoly in the West; SiC and power-chip demand swings; and dopant gases (AsH3, PH3
  are toxic) under tight safety rules (textbook, unsourced).

# 8. Why implant still matters at the leading edge

  Every transistor needs wells, source/drain extensions, halos and contact doping (textbook). SiC and
  power devices lean on high-energy, hot implants, which is the niche Axcelis emphasises.

# 8. Where each class is used (textbook, unsourced) and the anneal that follows

 ┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
 │ high current   source/drain and extensions: shallow, heavy dose (B, As, P), low energy   │
 │ medium current wells, channel and threshold-voltage adjust, halos: moderate energy, dose │
 │ high energy    retrograde / deep wells, image-sensor and power devices: MeV depth        │
 └──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

  implant (damage)  ──▶  anneal (heal + activate)  ──▶  dopants electrically active

 ┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
 │   AFTER IMPLANT                       AFTER SPIKE / LASER ANNEAL                    │
 │   ┌──────────────────────┐            ┌──────────────────────┐                      │
 │   │ ▒▒▒▒ amorphous ▒▒▒▒  │   heat     │ crystal regrown      │                      │
 │   │ ▒▒ dopant on random  │  ≈≈≈≈≈▶    │ dopant on lattice    │                      │
 │   │ ▒▒ interstitials ▒▒  │  ramp      │ sites = ACTIVE       │                      │
 │   │ crystal below, intact│  >=75 C/s  │ little diffusion     │                      │
 │   └──────────────────────┘            └──────────────────────┘                      │
 │   risk: transient enhanced diffusion pushes dopant deeper if the anneal is slow [4] │
 └─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

  (implant also makes SOI wafers by oxygen implant (SIMOX), per Wikipedia [1])

  Trade-off in one line: more heat activates more dopant but also diffuses it, so anneals keep getting
  shorter and hotter (spike, then millisecond, then microsecond laser) while keeping damage low. [4]
