# 1. What happens: metallurgical Si to 9N+ polysilicon

 ┌────────┐  ┌───────────┐  ┌───────────┐  ┌────────────┐  ┌────────────┐  ┌───────────┐
 │ MG-Si  │  │ Si + HCl  │  │ DISTILL   │  │ BELL JAR   │  │ CRUSH      │  │ POLY      │
 │ 98-99% │─▶│ 300 C bed │─▶│ TCS clean │─▶│ Si on rods │─▶│ etch, pack │─▶│ 9N+ chunk │
 └────────┘  └───────────┘  └───────────┘  └────────────┘  └────────────┘  └───────────┘

  (MG-Si is the output of SAND; polysilicon is the input to MELT + PULL; the loop recycles H2, HCl, STC)

# 3. The chemistry

  TCS synthesis   Si + 3HCl ──▶ SiHCl3 + H2        (about 300 C, fluidised bed; Fraunhofer lists
                  Si + 3.1HCl ──▶ 0.9SiHCl3 + 0.1SiCl4 + 1.1H2   as the practical mix)
  Distillation    SiHCl3 and SiCl4 (STC) separated at 30-60 C in multi-stage columns
  Deposition      SiHCl3 + H2 ──▶ Si + 3HCl         on rods at ~1100 C (main reaction, gives Si)
  Side reaction   4SiHCl3 ──▶ Si + 3SiCl4 + 2H2    (textbook, no source fetched; makes STC)
  Recycle         H2 and HCl recovered and re-fed; STC is a byproduct (silicones, silica, or
                  hydrogenated back to TCS in modified Siemens routes)
  Finish          rods crushed, then acid etch (HNO3 + HF, room temp) or alkaline etch (70-80 C)

  Slim rods start at 20 mm; final rods are 150-180 mm; growth is up to 1.9 mm/h. Electronic grade
  uses slower, uniform growth (steady H2:TCS ratio, low HCl, even temperature) so the rod stays
  smooth and dense. Solar grade pushes speed and tolerates rough surfaces.

# 4. By the numbers

  deposition temp     about 1100 C rod surface; 1000-1150 C range in patents
  rod diameter        20 mm slim rod in, 150-180 mm out
  growth rate         up to 1.9 mm/h on the diameter
  electricity (SoG)   60-70 kWh/kg in 2023 (CPIA / Fraunhofer ISE); ~35-45 kWh/kg of it in CVD
                      (70-85% of CVD heat recoverable); TCS synthesis ~9; other (PEM H2, scrubbers) 16
  electricity (EG)    higher; company averages: Wacker 91.9 (2020, multi-product), REC 123 (2020),
                      Hemlock 126 (2019), Wacker EG-only 150 (2002), Tokuyama 274 all energy (2019)
  patents quote       a looser 65 to 200 kWh/kg range for Siemens reactors in background text
  purity              electronic grade: donors <= 1.5e12 atoms/cm3 (Special EG), base metals <= 0.1 ppbw
                      solar grade allows donors <= 2.5e14, i.e. about 100x more
  price pressure      electronics grade is only ~5% of volume yet needs purity near 11N
  note                Bernreuter says 12N purity claims are not achieved for EG polysilicon

# 7. Solar volume vs chip-grade: two different businesses

  Almost all tonnes are solar. Semiconductor grade is a small, high-margin niche where non-Chinese
  producers still matter. Shares of EG supply are NOT published openly; a paid-report aggregator
  lists Wacker ~22.6% and Hemlock ~19.2% (low reliability, unverified). Tokuyama, Mitsubishi
  Materials and REC Silicon are the other names usually cited in electronic grade.

# 8. Chokepoints and risks

  - 9N-11N supply sits with a handful of plants: Wacker (Burghausen, Germany), Hemlock (Michigan),
    Tokuyama (Japan). Wafer makers qualify chunk lots for years, so switching is slow.
  - China makes 93.5% of polysilicon but almost all of it is solar grade. A 2024 Chinese overcapacity
    glut hit Wacker and others; top four Chinese producers lost money in H1 2024 (Bernreuter).
  - Electricity is a large cost line, so power price decides who survives.
  - Wacker is investing to lift hyperpure cleaning capacity by more than 50% (2024 annual report).
