== HBM3 on 16 nm
# What it does
Reported 8-high HBM3 stack on a 16 nm process; samples went to Huawei in 2025.
# Why it matters

# Source
Tom's Hardware and Notebookcheck, 2025-2026 reports.

== Yield
# What it does
One secondary source puts HBM3 yield near 25%; mass production is targeted for late Q4 2026.
# Why it matters
Low yield caps how many stacks can ship, whatever the target date.
# Source
Notebookcheck (single source; verify before relying on it).
