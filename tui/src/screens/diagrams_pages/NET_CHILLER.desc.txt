The chiller makes the cold water a data hall runs on: a refrigeration machine that boils refrigerant in an evaporator to pull heat out of the water, squeezes the vapour hot in a compressor and dumps the heat outdoors through a condenser. Big units move 1-2 MW of heat; on cold days, or with warm-water liquid cooling, free cooling and dry coolers let the compressor rest. Tickers: TT, CARR, JCI, MOD, VRT.
