A substation's power transformer steps transmission voltage (115-345 kV) down to medium voltage (13.8-34.5 kV) for the campus. It is a steel core with copper windings in an oil-filled tank: the turns ratio sets the voltage ratio, and it wastes under 1% of the power. Large transformers are the grid's longest wait, averaging about 128 weeks in 2025. Tickers: GEV, ETN, HD Hyundai Electric.
