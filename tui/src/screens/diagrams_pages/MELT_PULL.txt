# 3. The physics and chemistry

  Growth       a seed touches the melt at the freezing point; atoms attach in the seed's orientation,
               so pulling up extends one crystal (melting point 1414 C in the COMSOL model; 1425 C on
               Wikipedia for semiconductor grade, sources differ by a few degrees)
  Mass flow    pull rate v x crystal cross-section x density = kg of silicon frozen per hour
               worked example (computed here, density 2.33 g/cm3 is textbook): 300 mm body,
               v = 1.0 mm/min ──▶ ~71 cm3/min ──▶ ~165 g/min ──▶ ~9.9 kg/h
               v = 1.6 mm/min ──▶ ~15.8 kg/h
  Oxygen       melt dissolves the quartz crucible: SiO2 + Si ──▶ 2SiO (textbook, unsourced); most SiO
               evaporates, some freezes in. Crystal ends up ~1e18 oxygen atoms/cm3 (Wikipedia).
               Crucible rotation, melt depth, pull speed and magnetic fields tune the oxygen level
  Dopants      boron / phosphorus added to the melt. Segregation: solute prefers the liquid, so the
               melt gets richer and the tail gets more doped than the head (textbook, unsourced)
  Dash neck    pull a 2-4 mm neck fast (up to ~6 mm/min); dislocations slide out the surface within
               ~100-125 mm of neck (US 6,866,713). Another patent: neck at 1.0-3.0 mm/min, e.g. 2.4
               mm/min, 200 mm long. Dash-necked crystals are limited to about 100 kg by neck strength

# 4. By the numbers

  melt temp          ~1414 C (COMSOL); contact temperature 1403-1407.5 C in the model
  axial gradient     500 down to 100 C per metre along the rod (model)
  atmosphere         argon, 100 L/min, furnace at 2500 Pa (model, not a vendor spec)
  heater power       310 kW graphite heater (model)
  rotation           crucible 5 rpm, crystal -5 rpm (model)
  diameter           300 mm wafers need ~300 mm+ body; 450 mm was planned for 2018, stopped in 2016
  length / mass      up to 2 m, several hundred kg (Wikipedia)
  pull rate (300mm)  1.6 mm/min simulated: interface shifts 33 mm, growth time -46.4%, power -4.97%
                     (2023 study aimed at photovoltaic silicon)
  steady state       model reaches steady contact temperature after ~400 minutes

# 7. Chokepoints and risks

  - Quartz crucibles: high-purity quartz mostly from Spruce Pine, NC (see SAND). Crucibles are consumed
    every pull; a Spruce Pine outage idles pullers.
  - Yield: a dislocation anywhere in the body means remelting. Big hot zones and long cycles make a
    ruined pull expensive. Cycle times are not sourced here.
  - Oxygen and dopant uniformity at 300 mm is the hard control problem (patents on crucible rotation).
  - Demand is cyclical: SEMI says 2023 silicon wafer revenue fell 10.9% to $12.3B; polished 300 mm
    shipments fell 13% and epi 300 mm 5%, total area 12,602 million sq in (-14.3%).
