Contacts are the tungsten plugs and silicide that connect transistor source, drain and gate up to the first metal layer. As holes shrink below the 19 nm electron mean free path of tungsten, liners and boundary scattering make contact resistance a scaling bottleneck, driving cobalt, ruthenium, molybdenum, selective tungsten and contact-over-active-gate. Applied Materials (AMAT) and Lam Research (LRCX) sell the deposition tools; TSMC (TSM) and Intel (INTC) design the schemes.
