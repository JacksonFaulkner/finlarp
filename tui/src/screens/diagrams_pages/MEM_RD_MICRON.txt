# 1. HBM4 now

Micron's HBM4 uses a 2048-bit interface on its 1-beta DRAM. Micron has said HBM4 (36 GB, 12-high) entered high-volume manufacturing in March 2026. Its base die is Micron's own.

# 2. HBM4E: custom base die

HBM4E (2027-28) moves the base die to foundry logic. The logic die for standard and custom HBM4E is expected from TSMC, and DRAM moves from 1-beta to 1-gamma.

# 3. vHBM: DRAM on the processor

Micron's vertical HBM (vHBM) places the DRAM stack directly on the processor rather than beside it. Both new lines target volume in 2028, with three generations on the roadmap through 2030.
