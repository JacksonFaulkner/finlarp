An HBM stack is several thinned DRAM dies on a base logic die, joined by TSVs and microbumps, placed beside the GPU on a silicon interposer (CoWoS). That closeness allows a 1024-bit (HBM3, 819 GB/s) or 2048-bit (HBM4, up to 2 TB/s) bus per stack. Height limits, heat, stack yield and CoWoS packaging capacity are the constraints.
