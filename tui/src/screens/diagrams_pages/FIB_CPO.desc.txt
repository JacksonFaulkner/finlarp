Co-packaged optics move the optical engines from pluggable modules at the front panel onto the switch package itself, next to the switch chip. The electrical path shrinks from tens of centimetres to millimetres, so the DSPs and most of the power go away; lasers stay out at the panel where they can be swapped. NVIDIA claims 3.5x lower power for its photonics switches. Tickers: NVDA, AVGO, TSM.
