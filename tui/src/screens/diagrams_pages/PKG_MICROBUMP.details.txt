== C4 BUMP
# What it does
C4 bumps are solder balls at about 150 um pitch that join the interposer (or a chip) to the package substrate.
# Why it matters
They carry power and signals into the package from below; their coarse pitch is set by the substrate's wiring.
# Source
Patent 8896118; SemiEngineering.

== MICROBUMP
# What it does
Microbumps join the logic and HBM dies to the interposer at about 40 um pitch: 20-25 um bumps with 15 um spacing.
# Why it matters
Their density sets how many connections fit under each die; they are expected to hit a wall near 10 um.
# Source
SemiEngineering, scaling bump pitches.

== CU PILLAR
# What it does
A copper pillar is a plated copper post with a solder cap, the usual form of a microbump, standing taller than a plain solder ball.
# Why it matters
Pillars allow tighter pitch than balls because the copper doesn't spread when the solder melts.
# Source
Patent 8896118 (40 um copper pillar array).

== HYBRID BOND
# What it does
Hybrid bonding joins polished copper pads directly while the surrounding oxide bonds too, with no solder and no gap, at 10 um pitch and below.
# Why it matters
It allows far denser connections and thinner stacks, but needs near-perfect flatness and cleanliness.
# Source
SemiEngineering, bumps vs hybrid bonding.

== BUMP PITCH
# What it does
Bump pitch is the centre-to-centre spacing of bumps; connection density rises with one over pitch squared.
# Why it matters
C4 about 150 um, microbump about 40 um, hybrid 10 um and below: each step multiplies the connections per mm2.
# Source
SemiEngineering; patent 8896118.

== UNDERFILL
# What it does
Underfill is epoxy wicked between die and interposer and between interposer and substrate, then cured.
# Why it matters
It shares thermal-expansion stress across the whole area instead of the outer joints; for C4 joints it gives over 10x thermal-cycle life.
# Source
UCLA Pb-free workshop abstract (Totta).

== ELECTROMIGRATION
# What it does
As bumps shrink, each carries more current density, and electromigration slowly moves metal atoms until joints fail.
# Why it matters
Power for the logic die enters through thousands of bumps, so more bumps share the current.
# Source
Textbook; see MID METAL electromigration sources.

== SoIC
# What it does
SoIC is TSMC's system-on-integrated-chips stacking, which bonds dies on top of each other with hybrid bonds.
# Why it matters
It is TSMC's route past microbump pitch limits for die-on-die stacking.
# Source
TSMC 3DFabric.
