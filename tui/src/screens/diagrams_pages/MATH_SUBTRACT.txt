# 1. Take away: 7 minus 4

   ●●●●●●●                  start with 7
   ●●●✕✕✕✕                   take away 4 (crossed out)
   ●●●                       left: 3

   7 - 4 = 3

# 2. The same move backwards on the number line

   0   1   2   3   4   5   6   7   8
   ┼───┼───┼───┼───┼───┼───┼───┼───┼
               ◀───────────────●
                 -4 steps left

# 3. Check it with addition

  4 + 3 = 7, so 7 - 4 = 3. Subtraction undoes addition.
  Step past zero and you reach negatives: 3 - 4 = -1.
