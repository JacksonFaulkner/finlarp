Medium copper levels M5-M9 route block to block. Each is built by dual damascene: etch a trench and via into porous low-k, line them with a barrier (TaN/Ta, now Ru), plate Cu, polish flat. Wire delay (R x C) pushes the industry to Ru liners, ultra-low-k and air gaps. Tools: Applied Materials (AMAT), Lam (LRCX), Tokyo Electron (8035.T); fabs TSMC (TSM), Intel (INTC); RC signoff by Synopsys (SNPS) and Cadence (CDNS).
