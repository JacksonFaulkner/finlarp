== Xtacking 4.0
# What it does
NAND array bonded onto a separate logic wafer; reported at about 267-270 layers in mass production.
# Why it matters
Keeps the circuits out of the way of the stack, so more layers can be added.
# Source
Capital & Compute and Digitimes-sourced reports (layer counts vary).

== Three-deck bonding
# What it does
X5-9080 is reported to pass 300 layers by bonding three decks.
# Why it matters
More bits per wafer, at some cost in throughput.
# Source
Digitimes via Domain-b (2026).

== DRAM pivot
# What it does
Wuhan Phase 3 reportedly splits output between NAND and DRAM; a 9,600 MT/s low-power DRAM on 4F2 cells is planned, no timeline.
# Why it matters
Would put YMTC in DRAM, not just flash; still unconfirmed.
# Source
TweakTown (reported plan).
