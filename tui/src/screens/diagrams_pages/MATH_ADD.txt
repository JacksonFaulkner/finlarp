# 1. Two amounts go in, one total comes out

     ●●●           ●●●●            ●●●●●●●
     3 apples  +   4 apples    =   7 apples

  (order does not change the answer: 4 + 3 is also 7)

# 2. On a number line: start at 3, move 4 steps right

   0   1   2   3   4   5   6   7   8
   ┼───┼───┼───┼───┼───┼───┼───┼───┼
               ●───────────────▶
                +4 steps right

  3 + 4 = 7. A positive number moves you right; the amount moved is the second number.

# 3. Carrying: 9 + 1 = 10

  Write 0 in the ones column and carry 1 to the tens column. Computers do the same
  in binary: a full adder takes two bits and a carry in, and gives a sum bit and a carry out.
