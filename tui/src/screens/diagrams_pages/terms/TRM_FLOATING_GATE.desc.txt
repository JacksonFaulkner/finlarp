A floating gate is a conductive layer fully surrounded by insulator, sitting between the control gate and the transistor channel. Electrons pushed through the tunnel oxide get trapped there and shift the transistor threshold, which is how the cell stores a bit. It is the cell behind EEPROM and most flash, including NAND. Related: Micron (MU), Kioxia (285A.T), SanDisk (SNDK), Samsung (005930.KS).
