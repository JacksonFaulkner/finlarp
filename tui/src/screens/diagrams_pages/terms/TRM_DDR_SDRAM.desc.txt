DDR SDRAM is dynamic random access memory that transfers data on both edges of its clock, which doubles throughput per pin over plain SDRAM. Each generation, such as DDR4 and DDR5, raises transfer rates and changes voltage and packaging, so modules and CPUs must match. Servers fill most of their RAM with DDR5 DIMMs, while AI accelerators put faster HBM beside the GPU. Related: Micron (MU), Samsung (005930.KS), SK Hynix (000660.KS), AMD (AMD).
