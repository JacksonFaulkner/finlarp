Neon is a noble gas that is a byproduct of air separation, and it is the buffer gas in the excimer lasers that expose wafers in deep-ultraviolet lithography. Before 2022, Ukraine supplied a large share of semiconductor-grade neon, and that supply was disrupted when the war began. Chip makers qualified new sources and recycled neon in their tools to reduce the risk. Lithography runs on neon-based lasers, so any shortage affects the output of advanced logic and memory fabs.
