Agentic AI refers to systems where a language model plans steps, calls tools or software, checks results and repeats until a task is done. Because one task can trigger dozens of model calls, agents increase inference demand. Enterprise software companies market agents for customer service, coding and workflows. Reliability, security and cost are cited as risks. Related: Microsoft (MSFT), Salesforce (CRM), ServiceNow (NOW), NVIDIA (NVDA).
