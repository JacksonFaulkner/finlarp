Aisle containment arranges racks in rows so that cool supply air and hot exhaust air are kept separate, with doors or roofs sealing the hot or cold aisle. Keeping the streams apart lets cooling equipment run at higher temperatures and lower fan power, improving PUE. In AI halls it is still used for the air-cooled share of the heat, such as networking and storage, alongside liquid cooling. Related: Vertiv (VRT), Schneider Electric, nVent (NVT).
