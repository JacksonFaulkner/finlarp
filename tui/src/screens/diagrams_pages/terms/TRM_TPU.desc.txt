A TPU is Google's custom AI accelerator, built around large systolic arrays of multiply-accumulate units. The seed also groups other hyperscaler in-house accelerators here, such as AWS Trainium and Inferentia and Meta MTIA. These chips are connected in large pods with custom interconnect and serve the owner's internal models and cloud customers. Related: Alphabet (GOOGL), Amazon (AMZN), Meta Platforms (META), Broadcom (AVGO), Marvell Technology (MRVL).
