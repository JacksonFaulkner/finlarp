Open RAN splits the radio access network into standard interfaces so that radios, distributed units and software can come from different vendors. The O-RAN Alliance defines the open fronthaul and control interfaces that make this possible. The goal is to reduce vendor lock-in and cost, but adoption has been slower than planned and integration is harder. Related: Nokia (NOK), Ericsson (ERIC), Samsung (005930.KS), Qualcomm (QCOM).
