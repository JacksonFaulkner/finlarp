Operational technology (OT) is the hardware and software that monitors and controls physical equipment such as pumps, breakers, valves and robots. It is kept apart from IT, which runs business applications and standard servers, and OT often uses older protocols on equipment with long service lives. As data centers and factories add automation, OT networks connect more often to IT, which widens the cyber attack surface. Related: Rockwell Automation (ROK), Emerson Electric (EMR), Honeywell (HON), Siemens (SIE.DE).
