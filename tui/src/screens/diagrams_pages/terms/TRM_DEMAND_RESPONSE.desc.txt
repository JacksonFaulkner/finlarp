Demand response is a utility or grid operator program that pays large electricity users to reduce load when the grid is stressed. Participants agree to a notice window and a maximum number of hours, and are paid for the capacity they make available. Bitcoin miners and data centers that can switch off quickly are increasingly enrolled in these programs. Related: Riot Platforms (RIOT), Marathon Digital (MARA), IREN (IREN), TeraWulf (WULF).
