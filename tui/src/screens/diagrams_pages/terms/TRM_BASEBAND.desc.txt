The baseband is the digital signal processor that turns radio samples into bits and back, handling modulation, coding and protocol stacks. In phones it is usually part of a system-on-chip with the modem, and in base stations it runs on a separate digital unit. It is compute-heavy, so newer designs increasingly use programmable and AI-assisted processing. Related: Qualcomm (QCOM), MediaTek (2454.TW), Marvell (MRVL), Ericsson (ERIC).
