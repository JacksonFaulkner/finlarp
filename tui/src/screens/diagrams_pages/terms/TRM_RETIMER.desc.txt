A retimer is a small chip placed in the middle of a long high-speed link that recovers the degraded signal, cleans up its timing, and retransmits it fresh. As PCIe and Ethernet lane speeds rise, signals can only travel a few inches over a circuit board before becoming unreadable, so AI servers add retimers between CPUs, GPUs, NICs and SSDs. They are a fast-growing attach product per accelerator. Related: Astera Labs (ALAB), Broadcom (AVGO), Marvell (MRVL), Credo (CRDO).
