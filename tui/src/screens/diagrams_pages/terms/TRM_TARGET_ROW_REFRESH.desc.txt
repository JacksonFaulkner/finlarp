Target row refresh is an in-DRAM mitigation in which the chip tracks frequently activated rows and refreshes their neighbours before bits can flip. It is one of the main defenses against row hammer in commodity DRAM. The extra refresh work takes a small share of bandwidth and adds some power, which matters when memory is the bottleneck for AI workloads. Related: Micron (MU), SK hynix (000660.KS), Rambus (RMBS).
