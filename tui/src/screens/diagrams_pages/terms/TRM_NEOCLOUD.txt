# 1. What it is: GPU rental clouds

   ┌───────────────┐     ┌──────────────┐     ┌──────────────┐
   │ NVIDIA GPUs   │     │ NEOCLOUD     │     │ CUSTOMERS    │
   │ bought with   │ ──▶ │ CoreWeave,   │ ──▶ │ AI labs,     │
   │ debt + equity │     │ Nebius, IREN │     │ hyperscalers │
   └───────────────┘     └──────────────┘     └──────────────┘

  (multi-year take-or-pay contracts back the debt)

# 2. Why AI cares

Neoclouds add GPU capacity quickly and are large Nvidia buyers. Their contract backlog and financing costs are key filing metrics.

# 3. Risks

High leverage, few big customers and GPU depreciation schedules are the usual risk factors.
