NERC is the North American Electric Reliability Corporation, a self-regulatory body that writes and enforces mandatory reliability standards for the bulk power system under FERC oversight. Its standards cover grid planning, operations, and the CIP cybersecurity rules for grid assets. For AI infrastructure it matters because large data center loads are changing grid planning, and NERC has treated them as a new reliability risk. Related: Vistra (VST), GE Vernova (GEV), Eaton (ETN).
