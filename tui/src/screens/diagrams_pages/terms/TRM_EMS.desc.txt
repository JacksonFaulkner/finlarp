EMS (electronics manufacturing services) is the contract manufacturing industry that builds circuit boards, servers and other hardware for brand owners that design but do not assemble their own products. Large EMS firms buy components, run surface-mount and test lines, and ship finished systems, often from plants in several countries. For AI infrastructure, the EMS and ODM builders set how fast racks of GPU servers can be built and how much of the margin the assembly step keeps. Related: Foxconn (2317.TW), Jabil (JBL), Flex (FLEX), Celestica (CLS), Sanmina (SANM).
