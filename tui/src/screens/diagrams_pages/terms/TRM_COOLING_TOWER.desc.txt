A cooling tower rejects heat from a water loop to the air by letting part of the water evaporate, which cools the rest. Warm water from chillers or heat exchangers is sprayed over fill inside the tower while fans pull air through it. Towers are cheap to run, but they consume water, so water availability can limit where a large data center is sited.
