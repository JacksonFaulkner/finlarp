A foundry manufactures chips designed by other companies. TSMC is the largest pure-play foundry and makes most leading-edge AI accelerators; Samsung, GlobalFoundries, UMC, SMIC and Intel Foundry also compete. The model lets fabless chip companies skip the cost of owning fabs. Foundry capacity at advanced nodes and in advanced packaging is a key bottleneck for AI chip supply. Related: Taiwan Semiconductor (TSM), GlobalFoundries (GFS), United Microelectronics (UMC), Intel (INTC).
