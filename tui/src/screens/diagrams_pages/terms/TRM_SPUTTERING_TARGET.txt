# 1. What it is: a metal plate that becomes a thin film

   ┌──────────────────┐
   │ target (Cu, Ta,  │
   │ Ti, Al, W)       │
   └────────┬─────────┘
            │ ions knock atoms off
            ▼
   ┌──────────────────┐
   │ vacuum chamber   │
   └────────┬─────────┘
            │ atoms land on wafer
            ▼
   ┌──────────────────┐
   │ thin metal film  │
   └──────────────────┘

  (purity and grain size of the target control film uniformity)

# 2. Why AI cares

Fabs use sputtered metal layers to wire GPUs, HBM stacks, and substrates. Target supply limits how fast those tools can run.
