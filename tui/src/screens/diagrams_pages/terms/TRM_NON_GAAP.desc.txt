Non-GAAP figures are company-defined profit measures that remove items such as stock-based compensation, amortization of acquired intangibles, and restructuring charges from GAAP results. SEC rules require a reconciliation to the closest GAAP measure whenever a company publishes one. For AI infrastructure names, stock-based compensation is often the largest add-back, so the gap between the two numbers can be wide. Related: NVIDIA (NVDA), Broadcom (AVGO), Marvell (MRVL), CoreWeave (CRWV).
