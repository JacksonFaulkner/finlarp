A take-or-pay or long-term supply agreement commits a buyer to purchase a minimum volume, or pay for it anyway. In AI, memory and GPU buyers sign them to secure scarce supply, sometimes with prepayments, while cloud providers sign multi-year compute commitments with neoclouds. They give the seller visibility and cash up front but create purchase obligations for the buyer. Filings list them under purchase commitments and contractual obligations. Related: Micron (MU), NVIDIA (NVDA), CoreWeave (CRWV), Microsoft (MSFT), Western Digital (WDC).
