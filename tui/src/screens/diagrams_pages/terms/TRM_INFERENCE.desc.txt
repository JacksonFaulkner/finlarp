Inference is running an already-trained AI model to produce outputs such as text, images or predictions. Unlike training, it happens every time a user sends a request, so its cost grows with usage. Companies cite inference demand as a growing driver of GPU, custom accelerator, memory and networking sales. Efficiency per token is the main competitive metric. Related: NVIDIA (NVDA), Advanced Micro Devices (AMD), Broadcom (AVGO), Marvell (MRVL).
