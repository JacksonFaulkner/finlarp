Helium is an inert noble gas used as a coolant for superconducting magnets in MRI scanners and research equipment, and as a carrier and leak-check gas in chip fabs. It is extracted from natural gas wells, and it cannot be made in a plant, so supply depends on a few producing countries and on by-product recovery. Chip makers need it in steady, high-purity volumes, so a shortage can slow tool use and raise fab operating costs. Helium supply is a small input cost for most AI data centers but a real constraint for fab expansion. Related: Linde (LIN), Air Products (APD), Air Liquide (AI.PA)
