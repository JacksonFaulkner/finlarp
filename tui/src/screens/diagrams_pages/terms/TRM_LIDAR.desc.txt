LiDAR (light detection and ranging) fires laser pulses and times the returns to build a 3D point cloud of the scene around a vehicle. Automakers and robotaxi builders use it because it measures distance directly and works in darkness, while rain, fog and dirt cut its range. Unit prices have fallen from tens of thousands of dollars toward a few hundred, and solid-state parts replaced many spinning heads. Related: Ouster (OUST), Hesai (HSAI), Mobileye (MBLY), Aeva (AEVA).
