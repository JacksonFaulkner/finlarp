A storage controller is the processor inside an SSD that turns host commands into reads and writes across many NAND dies. It runs the flash translation layer, error correction and wear leveling, and largely sets a drive's speed and endurance. Controllers are made in-house by large memory makers or bought from merchant vendors. Related: Marvell (MRVL), Silicon Motion (SIMO), Phison (8299.TWO), Samsung (005930.KS).
