Electronic design automation is software used to design, simulate and verify integrated circuits before they are manufactured. Tools cover logic synthesis, physical layout, timing analysis and verification. The market is concentrated in Synopsys and Cadence, which sell mostly through multi-year licenses. Growth in custom AI silicon raises demand for these tools. Related: Synopsys (SNPS), Cadence Design Systems (CDNS), Siemens, Arm Holdings (ARM).
