# 1. What it is: a giant next-word predictor

   │ TEXT IN │──▶│ TRANSFORMER │──▶│ TEXT OUT   │
   │ tokens  │   │ billions of │   │ next token │
   └─────────┘   │ parameters  │   └────────────┘
                 └─────────────┘                 

  (output token is fed back in, one step at a time)

# 2. Why it matters

LLMs are the models behind chat assistants and coding tools. Their size and usage drive most current AI infrastructure spending.

# 3. Business

Companies build, license or rent LLMs through cloud APIs, and many filings now disclose LLM risks and costs.
