Days inventory outstanding estimates how many days of cost of revenue are sitting in inventory, using ending inventory divided by daily cost of revenue. A rising figure can mean parts are piling up ahead of demand, or that products are not selling as planned. Hardware makers with long build cycles, such as AI server builders, watch it closely around platform ramps.
