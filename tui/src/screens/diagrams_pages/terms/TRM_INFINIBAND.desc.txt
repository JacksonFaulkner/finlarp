InfiniBand is a high-performance network standard with very low latency and built-in RDMA, long used in supercomputers. Nvidia owns the main supplier, Mellanox, and InfiniBand became the default scale-out network for early large AI training clusters. Ethernet with RoCE and Ultra Ethernet is now competing for the same role, so the InfiniBand vs Ethernet mix is a watched metric in Nvidia's networking revenue. Related: Nvidia (NVDA), Arista (ANET), Broadcom (AVGO).
