# 1. What it is: a byproduct metal that becomes chip compounds

   ┌──────────────────┐
   │ alumina refining │  gallium comes out as a byproduct
   └────────┬─────────┘
            ▼
   ┌──────────────────┐
   │ crude gallium    │  refined to 99.999%+ purity
   └────────┬─────────┘
            ▼
   ┌──────────────────┐
   │ GaAs / GaN wafers│  RF amplifiers, power switches, LEDs
   └──────────────────┘

  (ore is bauxite; gallium is not mined on its own in most places)

# 2. Why AI cares

AI servers need power-conversion and high-frequency parts made from GaN and GaAs. A supply cut or license delay for refined gallium can slow those parts and the power stages that feed accelerators.

# 3. Who controls it

Most refined gallium comes from China, so export rules there matter more than where the ore is found.
