Wi-Fi 7, the IEEE 802.11be standard, adds 320 MHz channels, 4096-QAM and multi-link operation across the 2.4, 5 and 6 GHz bands. It targets peak rates above 30 Gbps on paper and lower latency for enterprise and home networks. Chipmakers and access-point vendors sell the radios, so volume follows the upgrade cycle of routers and enterprise access points. Related: Qualcomm (QCOM), Broadcom (AVGO), Cisco (CSCO), Ubiquiti (UI).
