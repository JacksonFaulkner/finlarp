# 1. What it is: a license gate on chip shipments

   ┌─────────────┐    ┌───────────────────┐    ┌───────────────────┐
   │ US SUPPLIER │───▶│ BIS CHECK         │───▶│ SHIP / DENY       │
   │ GPU / tool  │    │ country, buyer,   │    │ license or        │
   │             │    │ performance limit │    │ Entity List block │
   └─────────────┘    └───────────────────┘    └───────────────────┘

  (rules key on chip performance density and end-user, not just country)

# 2. Business effect

A new rule can turn a product line into unsellable inventory overnight, as with NVIDIA's H20 charge in 2025. Vendors respond with cut-down China SKUs.

# 3. In filings

Look for 'export license', 'Entity List' and China revenue share in the risk factors and geographic revenue notes.
