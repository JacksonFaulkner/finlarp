# 1. What it is: malware that encrypts data and demands payment

   ┌───────────┐            ┌──────────┐            ┌───────────────┐
   │ attacker  │───────────▶│ foothold │───────────▶│ files locked  │
   └───────────┘            └──────────┘            └───────────────┘
                                  │
                                  ▼ steal data first, then encrypt

(double extortion: publish stolen data if the victim does not pay)

# 2. Why it matters

Ransomware outages can stop plants and hospitals for days. Backup, endpoint detection and exposure management are the main defenses sold against it.
