# 1. What it is: six-transistor memory on the logic die

   ┌──────────────┐   ┌────────────┐   ┌─────────────┐
   │ COMPUTE      │──▶│ SRAM       │──▶│ HBM / DRAM  │
   │ tensor cores │   │ ~1 ns      │   │ ~100 ns     │
   └──────────────┘   │ MBs on die │   │ GBs off die │
                      └────────────┘   └─────────────┘

  (SRAM bit cells shrink slowly at new nodes, so cache area grows as a share of die)

# 2. Why AI cares

Keeping weights and activations in SRAM avoids slow trips to HBM; chip designs trade SRAM area against compute area.
