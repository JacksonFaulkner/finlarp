# 2. Why AI cares

Data center campuses can draw hundreds of megawatts and can drop off the grid suddenly, which NERC has flagged as a reliability concern. Compliance adds cost and lead time for the utilities and generators that serve these sites. The CIP cybersecurity standards add another compliance layer on top.
