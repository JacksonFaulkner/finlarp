ARR, or annual recurring revenue, is the annualized value of subscription and other contracted recurring fees a company bills at a given point. Companies compute it as monthly recurring revenue times twelve, or from contract values, and definitions vary. Analysts use ARR as a forward signal because it reflects the current billing base rather than revenue booked over the past year. Usage and pilot revenue are often excluded or counted differently, so the filing's definition matters. Related: Datadog (DDOG), HubSpot (HUBS), MongoDB (MDB), Snowflake (SNOW).
