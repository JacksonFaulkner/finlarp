FERC is the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, the US agency that regulates interstate electricity transmission and wholesale power markets, plus interstate gas pipelines. It approves the tariffs and market rules that grid operators such as PJM and MISO run under. For AI infrastructure it matters because data center campuses connect to transmission systems FERC oversees, so its interconnection and co-location rules help set how fast a site can get power. Related: Vistra (VST), Constellation Energy (CEG), NRG Energy (NRG).
