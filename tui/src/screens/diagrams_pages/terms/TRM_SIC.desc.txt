Silicon carbide is a wide-bandgap semiconductor for high-voltage, high-temperature power devices. SiC MOSFETs and diodes handle 650V to 3.3kV with lower losses than silicon IGBTs. The main market is EV traction inverters, with growing use in solar, grid storage and data center power. Related: Wolfspeed (WOLF), onsemi (ON), STMicroelectronics (STM), Infineon (IFNNY), Coherent (COHR).
