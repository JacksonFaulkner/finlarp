An FPGA is a chip whose logic can be rewired after manufacturing by loading a configuration file. It sits between a CPU and an ASIC: slower and less dense than an ASIC but changeable in the field. FPGAs are used for networking, prototyping ASICs, low-latency trading, and some inference and signal processing. Related: Advanced Micro Devices (AMD), Intel (INTC), Altera, Lattice Semiconductor (LSCC), Microchip Technology (MCHP).
