A hosting agreement is a contract in which a site operator provides power, space, cooling, and connectivity while a customer owns and runs its own servers. The operator is typically paid a fixed fee per MW or kW per month, and the customer bears the hardware and GPU risk. For miners, these deals convert a volatile BTC revenue stream into contracted cash flow. Related: Core Scientific (CORZ), IREN (IREN), Cipher Mining (CIFR), Hut 8 (HUT).
