A building management system (BMS) is the software and controller network that monitors and runs a building's mechanical and electrical plant, such as chillers, air handlers, pumps and meters. It logs sensor data, schedules equipment and raises alarms when a reading drifts out of range. In a data center the BMS sits beside the power and cooling controls and gives operators one view of the site. Related: Johnson Controls (JCI), Schneider Electric (SU.PA), Siemens (SIE.DE), Honeywell (HON).
