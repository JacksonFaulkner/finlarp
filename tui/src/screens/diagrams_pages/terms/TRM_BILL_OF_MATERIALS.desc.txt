A bill of materials is the list of every part, quantity and supplier that goes into a product, and it drives purchasing and cost. In electronics, long-lead components on the BOM, such as memory, power parts and optics, can limit how many systems an EMS builder can finish. A change to the BOM can shift margins without any change to the headline product. Related: Jabil (JBL), Flex (FLEX), Super Micro (SMCI).
