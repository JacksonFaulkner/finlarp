An inventory write-down cuts the book value of unsold parts or products when their market value falls below cost, or when they are obsolete. It is booked as a charge against cost of revenue, so it hits gross margin in the quarter it is recorded. In AI hardware it tends to appear when a platform transition leaves older GPUs, boards or memory unsold. Related: NVIDIA (NVDA), Super Micro Computer (SMCI), Micron (MU), Intel (INTC).
