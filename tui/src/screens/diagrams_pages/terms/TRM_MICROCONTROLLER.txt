# 1. What it is: a whole small computer on one chip

   ┌─────────┐    ┌─────────────┐    ┌──────────────┐
   │ sensors │    │ MCU         │    │ motors, LEDs │
   │ buttons │───▶│ CPU + flash │───▶│ power stages │
   └─────────┘    │ RAM + I/O   │    └──────────────┘
                  └─────────────┘                    

  (typically Arm Cortex-M or similar, tens to hundreds of MHz)

# 2. Why it matters

MCUs are sticky: customers write code for one family and rarely switch. Inventory gluts in 2023-2024 hit the leaders' revenue hard.

# 3. AI angle

MCUs manage power supplies, fans and boards in servers; small 'edge AI' MCUs add neural accelerators.
