# 2. Why AI cares

A foundation model is trained once on broad data, then reused across many products through prompting, retrieval, or fine-tuning. Frontier training runs cost tens to hundreds of millions of dollars, which concentrates development among a few well-funded labs. After training, the recurring cost shifts to inference, which is where ongoing GPU demand comes from.
