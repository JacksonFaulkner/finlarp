# 1. What it is: long contract for a plant's output

   ┌───────────┐   ┌─────────────┐   ┌─────────────┐
   │ GENERATOR │──▶│ PPA         │──▶│ BUYER       │
   │ plant     │   │ fixed price │   │ hyperscaler │
   └───────────┘   │ 10-20 years │   └─────────────┘
                   └─────────────┘                  

  (physical PPA delivers power; virtual PPA settles the price difference in cash)

# 2. Why AI cares

Big AI buyers sign PPAs to secure power years ahead and to back new nuclear, gas or renewable builds.
