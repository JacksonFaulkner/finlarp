An active electrical cable (AEC) is a copper cable with small retimer/DSP chips built into each connector end that clean up the signal. That lets copper reach about 3-7 meters at 100G+ per lane, versus 1-2 m for passive copper, while using less power and costing less than optics. AECs are widely used for server-to-top-of-rack and in-rack links in AI clusters. Related: Credo (CRDO), Marvell (MRVL), Astera Labs (ALAB), Amphenol (APH).
