NIS2 is the EU Network and Information Security Directive 2, which sets cybersecurity and incident-reporting duties for essential and important entities across sectors such as energy, transport, and digital infrastructure. Cloud providers, data center operators, and managed service providers are in scope. For AI infrastructure it matters because covered operators must report significant incidents quickly and manage supply-chain security, with fines that can reach EUR 10 million or 2 percent of global turnover. Related: Equinix (EQIX), Digital Realty (DLR), Cisco (CSCO).
