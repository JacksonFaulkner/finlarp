CUDA is NVIDIA's programming platform for running general computation on its GPUs, including the compiler, runtime and libraries such as cuDNN and cuBLAS. Most AI frameworks are built and tuned on CUDA first, so code and developer skills are tied to NVIDIA hardware. This software lock-in is a key reason competitors' chips are harder to adopt. Related: NVIDIA (NVDA), Advanced Micro Devices (AMD), Intel (INTC).
