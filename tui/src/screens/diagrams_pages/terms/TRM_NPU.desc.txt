An NPU is a small AI accelerator block built into a phone, laptop or other device chip, tuned to run neural networks at low power. NPUs let devices run speech, vision and small language models locally instead of in the cloud. Their TOPS rating is a selling point for AI PCs and smartphones. Related: Qualcomm (QCOM), Apple (AAPL), Intel (INTC), Advanced Micro Devices (AMD), MediaTek, Arm Holdings (ARM).
