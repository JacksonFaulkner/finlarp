# 1. What it is: cellular radio plus a cloud core

   ┌──────────┐   radio   ┌──────────────┐   fiber   ┌──────────────┐
   │ PHONE    │──────────▶│ 5G RADIO     │──────────▶│ 5G CORE      │
   │ UE       │◀──────────│ gNodeB       │◀──────────│ cloud network│
   └──────────┘           └──────────────┘           └──────────────┘

  (NR radio: sub-6 GHz for coverage, mmWave for capacity)

# 2. Why it matters

5G is the air interface that connects phones and devices to the network. Its capex sits in radios, backhaul and core software, and the edge sites it creates are a possible home for inference.
