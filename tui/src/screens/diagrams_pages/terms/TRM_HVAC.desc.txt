HVAC (heating, ventilation and air conditioning) is the equipment that controls temperature, humidity and air quality in a building. In a data center, HVAC is the room-level layer that sits beside the chip-level liquid loops, and it is a large share of non-IT power draw. Cooling load grows with rack density, so HVAC capacity often sets how many megawatts a site can host. Related: Trane Technologies (TT), Carrier Global (CARR), Johnson Controls (JCI), Lennox (LII).
