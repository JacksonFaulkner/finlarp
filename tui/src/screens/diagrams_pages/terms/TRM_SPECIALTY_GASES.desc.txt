Specialty gases are high-purity process gases, such as nitrogen trifluoride, tungsten hexafluoride, silane, and etch gases, that chip fabs use in deposition, etching, and chamber cleaning. They are made in a small number of plants and delivered in cylinders or by on-site generators, so supply is concentrated. Purity demands are strict, and a bad lot can scrap wafers. Demand for these gases grows with the number of deposition and etch steps in advanced logic and memory. Related: Linde (LIN), Air Products (APD), Entegris (ENTG)
