# 1. What it is: fuel to firm power

   ┌─────────────┐   ┌─────────────┐   ┌─────────────┐   ┌───────────┐
   │ NATURAL GAS │──▶│ GAS TURBINE │──▶│ STEAM CYCLE │──▶│ GENERATOR │
   │ pipeline    │   │ compressor  │   │ waste heat  │   │ AC power  │
   └─────────────┘   │ combustor   │   │ (combined)  │   └───────────┘
                     └─────────────┘   └─────────────┘                

  (simple cycle ~35-40% efficient; combined cycle ~60%)

# 2. Why AI cares

Gas plants run around the clock, unlike wind or solar. Hyperscalers and developers are reserving turbines to power campuses that cannot wait for grid hookups.

# 3. Bottleneck

Large frame turbines are made by only a few firms, so lead times and pricing have climbed.
