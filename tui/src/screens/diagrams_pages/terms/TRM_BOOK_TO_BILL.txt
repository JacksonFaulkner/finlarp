# 1. What it is: orders compared with shipments

 ┌───────────────┐
 │ BOOKINGS      │  $1.2B new orders
 └───────┬───────┘
         │ divided by
 ┌───────┴───────┐
 │ BILLINGS      │  $1.0B shipped
 └───────┬───────┘
         │ above 1.0 = backlog grows
 ┌───────┴───────┐
 │ RATIO 1.2x    │
 └───────────────┘

# 2. Why it matters

It is a rough, quarterly gauge of demand momentum. Companies define bookings differently, so compare a company with its own history rather than with peers.
