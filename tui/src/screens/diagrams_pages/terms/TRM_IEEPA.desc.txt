IEEPA, the International Emergency Economic Powers Act of 1977, lets the US president regulate trade and financial transactions after declaring a national emergency. It is the legal basis for many sanctions programs and for the broad tariffs imposed in 2025 on many trading partners. For AI infrastructure it matters because those tariffs raise the cost of imported servers, GPUs, and components, and the legal challenges to them affect hardware margins.
