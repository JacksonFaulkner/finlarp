Charge trap flash stores electrons in defects inside a silicon nitride layer instead of in a conductive floating gate. Because the charge stays local, one defect does not drain the whole cell, which helps reliability and allows tighter cell spacing. Many 3D NAND products use this structure for high layer counts. Related: Samsung (005930.KS), SK Hynix (000660.KS), Kioxia (285A.T), Lam Research (LRCX).
