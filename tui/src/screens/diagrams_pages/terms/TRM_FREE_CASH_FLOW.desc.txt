Free cash flow is operating cash flow minus capital expenditures, and it shows how much cash is left after a company pays to maintain and grow its asset base. Companies define it slightly differently, for example whether they also deduct leases or capitalized software. Heavy AI data center spending can push free cash flow well below operating cash flow, or negative, even when the business is profitable. Related: Microsoft (MSFT), Alphabet (GOOGL), Meta Platforms (META), Oracle (ORCL).
