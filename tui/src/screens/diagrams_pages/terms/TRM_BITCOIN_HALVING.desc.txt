A bitcoin halving cuts the block subsidy paid to miners in half, which happens roughly every 210,000 blocks or about four years. The most recent halving was in April 2024, which reduced the subsidy from 6.25 BTC to 3.125 BTC per block. After a halving, miners must either grow hashrate, cut power cost, or earn revenue from other uses to keep margins, which is one reason public miners are adding AI hosting. Related: Marathon Digital (MARA), Riot Platforms (RIOT), Hut 8 (HUT), TeraWulf (WULF).
