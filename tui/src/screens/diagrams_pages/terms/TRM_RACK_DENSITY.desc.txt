Rack density is the power drawn by one server rack, measured in kilowatts per rack. Traditional enterprise racks ran 5-15 kW, while Nvidia GB200 NVL72 racks draw about 120-130 kW and planned systems go higher. Higher density forces changes in power distribution, liquid cooling and floor loading, so older facilities often cannot host AI gear without retrofits. Related: Nvidia (NVDA), Vertiv (VRT), Super Micro (SMCI), Equinix (EQIX).
