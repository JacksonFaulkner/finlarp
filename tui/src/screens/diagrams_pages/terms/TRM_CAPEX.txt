# 1. What it is: cash spent on buildings and machines

   ┌──────────────┐    ┌────────────────┐    ┌───────────────┐
   │ HYPERSCALER  │───▶│ SUPPLIERS      │───▶│ BALANCE SHEET │
   │ capex budget │    │ GPUs, memory,  │    │ PP&E, then    │
   │              │    │ network, power │    │ depreciation  │
   └──────────────┘    └────────────────┘    └───────────────┘

  (shows as 'purchases of property and equipment' in cash flow from investing)

# 2. Why it matters

One company's capex is another company's revenue. Rising hyperscaler capex guidance usually lifts the AI supply chain; cuts do the opposite.

# 3. Cash vs profit

Heavy capex lowers free cash flow now but only lowers earnings later as assets depreciate.
