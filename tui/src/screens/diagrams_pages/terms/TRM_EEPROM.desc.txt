EEPROM is non-volatile memory that can be erased and rewritten electrically, one byte at a time, without a UV eraser. Each cell is a floating-gate transistor, like flash, so data survives power loss. Its erase is slow and its density is low, so it holds configuration and calibration rather than bulk data. Related: Microchip Technology (MCHP), STMicroelectronics (STM), Infineon (IFX.DE).
