# 2. Why AI cares

Owning the chip lets a cloud set its own cost per unit of compute and avoid paying a GPU vendor margin.

# 3. Business angle

Volume of TPU/Trainium orders shows up in Broadcom, Marvell, HBM and foundry revenue.
