Ransomware is malware that encrypts a victim's files or systems and demands payment for the decryption key. Modern gangs add double extortion, stealing data first and threatening to publish it if the ransom is not paid. Attacks on hospitals, manufacturers and cloud backups can cause outages lasting days, which keeps backup, endpoint and detection spending high. Related: CrowdStrike (CRWD), Palo Alto Networks (PANW), Tenable (TENB), Rapid7 (RPD).
