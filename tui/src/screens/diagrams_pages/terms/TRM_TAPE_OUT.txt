# 1. What it is: design done, sent to the fab

   ┌─────────────┐   ┌────────────┐   ┌───────────────┐
   │ DESIGN      │   │ TAPE-OUT   │   │ FIRST SILICON │
   │ RTL, layout │──▶│ GDS to fab │──▶│ ~3 months     │
   │ verify      │   │ masks made │   │ bring-up      │
   └─────────────┘   └────────────┘   └───────────────┘

  (a respin (fixing bugs and taping out again) adds months and mask cost)

# 2. Why it matters

Tape-out dates anchor product timelines. Slips push revenue out.
