# 1. What it is: the full time to reopen a row in one bank

   ACT ──────────────────── PRE ─────────────── ACT
   │◀──────────── tRC ─────────────────────────▶│
   │◀──── tRAS (row open) ───▶│◀── tRP ───▶│
                              PRE done

  (tRC is roughly tRAS plus tRP: the minimum gap between two ACTs to one bank)

# 2. Why AI cares

tRC caps how often one bank can serve a new row. Parallel banks and subarrays are how memory hides this limit at system level.
