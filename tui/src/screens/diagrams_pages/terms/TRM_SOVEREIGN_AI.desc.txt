Sovereign AI is a government or national champion building AI compute, data centers and models inside its own borders under local control. Countries cite data residency, language coverage and strategic independence. For suppliers it is a new customer class beyond US hyperscalers, often funded by state budgets or national telecoms. Deals tend to be lumpy and tied to export-license approvals. Related: NVIDIA (NVDA), Oracle (ORCL), Cisco (CSCO), Dell (DELL).
