The RF front end is the set of analog parts between the antenna and the baseband, including power amplifiers, low-noise amplifiers, filters, switches and duplexers. Each band a phone or base station supports adds more filters and switches, so the content per device has grown with 5G band counts. Power amplifiers and filters are the parts most exposed to the RF bill of materials. Related: Skyworks (SWKS), Qorvo (QRVO), Broadcom (AVGO), Murata (6981.T).
