Gallium is a soft metal that is recovered mainly as a byproduct of alumina (aluminum oxide) refining, and it is the base for gallium arsenide (GaAs) and gallium nitride (GaN) semiconductors. GaAs goes into RF and power amplifier chips, and GaN is used in fast-switching power devices and some lasers and LEDs. China supplies most of the world's refined gallium, and it placed export licensing on gallium in 2023 and later tightened exports to the US. Supply disruptions there raise the cost and lead time of compound-semiconductor parts used in wireless and power electronics. Related: AXT Inc (AXTI), Navitas Semiconductor (NVTS), Coherent (COHR)
