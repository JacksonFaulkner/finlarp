# 1. What it is: a timer on every pixel gives depth

   ┌──────────┐   ┌───────┐   ┌───────────────┐   ┌───────────┐
   │ IR PULSE │──▶│ SCENE │──▶│ PHOTON RETURN │──▶│ DEPTH MAP │
   └──────────┘   └───────┘   └───────────────┘   └───────────┘

  (depth = light speed x round-trip time / 2, computed per pixel)

# 2. Why it matters

ToF gives robots and car cabins a cheap depth channel next to the camera. It is a smaller market than LiDAR but ships in far higher volume in phones and interiors.
