Power cost per kWh is the all-in price a miner or data center pays for each kilowatt-hour, including energy, transmission, capacity, and demand charges. For a bitcoin miner it is the largest input to cost per BTC mined, and a few cents per kWh can decide whether a fleet is profitable. Sites with long-term power contracts or their own generation report it to show a cost advantage. Related: IREN (IREN), TeraWulf (WULF), Riot Platforms (RIOT), Marathon Digital (MARA).
