SOCAMM is a compact, replaceable memory module built from LPDDR DRAM chips, pushed by Nvidia for its Grace and Vera CPUs. It gives the low power of LPDDR without soldering the memory to the board. Memory makers sell it as a new product line for AI servers alongside HBM. Related: Micron (MU), Samsung (005930.KS), SK hynix (000660.KS), Nvidia (NVDA).
