  (design cycle ~18-24 months; payback needs large volume)

# 2. Why AI cares

A chip tuned to one company's workloads can beat a GPU on cost per token. Google TPU, AWS Trainium and Meta MTIA are examples.

# 3. Business angle

Custom silicon revenue goes to design-service firms like Broadcom and Marvell and to the foundry, not to GPU vendors.
