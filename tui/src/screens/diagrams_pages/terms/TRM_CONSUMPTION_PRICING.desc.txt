Consumption pricing charges customers for what they use, such as API calls, tokens, compute hours, or stored data, rather than a fixed subscription. It is often combined with a committed minimum spend that customers pay regardless of usage. Revenue moves with customer activity, so it grows quickly in busy periods and falls when usage drops. AI model APIs and data platforms commonly use this model because inference and storage costs scale with use. Related: Snowflake (SNOW), Datadog (DDOG), MongoDB (MDB), Confluent (CFLT).
