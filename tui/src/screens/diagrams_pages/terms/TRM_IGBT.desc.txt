An insulated gate bipolar transistor is a high-voltage power switch that combines a MOSFET gate with a bipolar output stage. It handles hundreds to thousands of volts in motor drives, trains, wind and solar inverters and older EV inverters. SiC MOSFETs are taking share at the high end, but IGBTs stay cheaper for many industrial uses. Related: Infineon (IFNNY), Mitsubishi Electric, onsemi (ON), STMicroelectronics (STM), Fuji Electric.
