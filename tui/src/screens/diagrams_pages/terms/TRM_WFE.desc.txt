WFE (wafer fab equipment) is the market for the tools that process wafers inside a fab: lithography, deposition, etch, cleaning, implant and process control. It is the bulk of chipmaker capital spending, roughly $100B a year. Demand follows foundry and memory capex, so AI-driven leading-edge logic and HBM buildouts lift orders. A few suppliers dominate each tool category. Related: ASML (ASML), Applied Materials (AMAT), Lam Research (LRCX), KLA (KLAC), Tokyo Electron.
