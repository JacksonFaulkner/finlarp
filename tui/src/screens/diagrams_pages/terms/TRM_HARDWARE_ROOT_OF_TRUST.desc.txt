A hardware root of trust is a chip-level component that stores keys and measures firmware before anything else runs, so the system starts from a known state. Trusted Platform Modules (TPMs), secure elements and on-die security processors are typical forms. In AI servers and accelerators it anchors secure boot and remote attestation, which lets a buyer confirm the hardware and firmware before sending data or model weights. Related: Infineon (IFX.DE), NXP (NXPI), NVIDIA (NVDA), Microchip (MCHP).
