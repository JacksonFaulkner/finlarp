Foveros is Intel's 3D packaging technology that stacks logic chiplets on top of a base die, connected by microbumps, so compute and I/O can be built on separate process nodes and combined in one package. Foveros Direct replaces microbumps with copper-to-copper direct bonding to shrink the vertical gap between dies. For AI, it is one route to more compute and memory in a single package when a flat die would be too large or too slow. Related: Intel (INTC), TSMC (TSM), Amkor (AMKR).
