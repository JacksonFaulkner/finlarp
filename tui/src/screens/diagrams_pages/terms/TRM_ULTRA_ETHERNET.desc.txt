Ultra Ethernet is an open specification from the Ultra Ethernet Consortium that updates Ethernet for AI and HPC clusters. It adds a new transport with packet spraying across paths, better congestion control and faster loss recovery, aiming to match InfiniBand on performance while keeping Ethernet's multi-vendor supply. Version 1.0 was published in 2025, and switch and NIC vendors are shipping compliant parts. Related: Broadcom (AVGO), Arista (ANET), AMD (AMD), Cisco (CSCO), Marvell (MRVL), Nvidia (NVDA).
