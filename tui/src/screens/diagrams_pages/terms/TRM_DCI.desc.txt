Data center interconnect (DCI) is the network linking separate data centers, from buildings on one campus to sites hundreds of kilometers apart. It typically runs DWDM over leased or owned fiber using coherent optics, either in dedicated optical systems or as ZR pluggables in routers. Cloud providers are the largest buyers, and AI raises demand because large training and inference fleets are spread over multiple sites. DCI sales show up in the optical segments of networking equipment companies. Related: Ciena (CIEN), Cisco (CSCO), Arista (ANET), Nokia (NOK), Marvell (MRVL).
