Millimeter wave refers to radio spectrum roughly between 24 and 100 GHz, where wavelengths are a few millimeters. It offers very wide channels and high peak throughput but travels only short distances and is blocked by walls and foliage. Carriers use it in dense urban hot spots and fixed wireless access, which means many small sites and more phased-array front ends. Related: Qualcomm (QCOM), Skyworks (SWKS), Qorvo (QRVO), Verizon (VZ).
