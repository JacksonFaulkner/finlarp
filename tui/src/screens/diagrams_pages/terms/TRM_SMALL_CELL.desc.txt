A small cell is a low-power base station, often mounted on a streetlight or building, that adds capacity in dense areas where macro towers cannot cover demand. Small cells are needed for mmWave coverage because high-band signals travel only short distances. Operators and tower companies deploy them in large numbers, so the count of sites drives radio, fiber and power purchases. Related: American Tower (AMT), Corning (GLW), Ericsson (ERIC), Nokia (NOK).
