# 2. Why it matters

WFE vendor revenue tracks chipmaker capex with a lag. Guidance like 'WFE up mid-single digits' is how the industry frames the cycle.

# 3. Export controls

Restrictions on tool sales to China shift the mix and affect vendor growth.
