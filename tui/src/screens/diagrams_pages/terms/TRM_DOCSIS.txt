# 1. What it is: internet over the cable TV plant

   ┌──────────────┐  fiber  ┌──────────┐  coax  ┌──────────────┐
   │ HEADEND      │────────▶│ NODE     │───────▶│ CABLE MODEM  │
   │ CMTS         │◀────────│ HFC      │◀───────│ in the home  │
   └──────────────┘         └──────────┘        └──────────────┘

  (CMTS = cable modem termination system; HFC = hybrid fiber-coax)

# 2. Why it matters

DOCSIS upgrades are capex for cable operators and a market for headend and node gear. Upstream speed limits set how far they can compete with fiber.
