A modular data center is a data hall assembled from factory-built modules, such as power skids, white-space pods and cooling units, that are shipped to site and joined. Much of the wiring, piping and testing happens in the factory, which shortens field work and cuts weather exposure. Operators use modular units to add capacity faster than a conventional build, although each module has a size limit.
