Tape-out is the point when a chip design is finished and sent to the foundry to make masks. It marks the end of design and the start of a months-long build and test cycle. Companies announce tape-outs to show a product is on schedule, and mask sets at leading nodes cost tens of millions of dollars. Related: NVIDIA (NVDA), Broadcom (AVGO), Marvell (MRVL), Synopsys (SNPS), Cadence (CDNS).
