# 2. Why AI cares

Hyperscalers and chipmakers that book profits in low-tax countries can owe top-up tax, which raises their effective tax rate. The rules apply country by country, so the impact depends on where a company runs its operations and data centers. Incentives that push a country's rate below 15 percent are partly reversed by the top-up.
