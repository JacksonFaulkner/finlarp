Rack integration is the work of cabling, configuring, testing and validating a full rack of servers, switches, power and cooling gear before it ships to a data center. Integrators such as ODMs and system makers cut deployment time by delivering a rack that is already burned in and tested. For AI, rack-scale systems are heavy and power-dense, so integration capacity can become a bottleneck. Related: Super Micro (SMCI), Dell (DELL), Foxconn (2317.TW).
