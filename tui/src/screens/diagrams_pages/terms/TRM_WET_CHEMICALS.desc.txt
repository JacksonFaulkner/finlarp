Wet chemicals are ultra-pure acids, bases, and solvents, such as hydrofluoric acid, sulfuric acid, and ammonia-based cleans, used to etch and clean wafers between process steps. Electronic grade means metal and particle impurities are held to parts-per-trillion levels, because a single contaminant can ruin a transistor. Suppliers purify and deliver these chemicals in bulk, and chip fabs consume them every day at high volume. Demand rises with the number of cleaning and etch steps in advanced logic and memory.
