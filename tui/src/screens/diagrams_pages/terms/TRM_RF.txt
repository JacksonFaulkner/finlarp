# 1. What it is: the chips between the antenna and the modem

   ┌─────────┐    ┌────────────────┐    ┌─────────────┐    ┌───────┐
   │ antenna │    │ RF FRONT-END   │    │ transceiver │    │ modem │
   └─────────┘◀──▶│ switch, filter │◀──▶└─────────────┘◀──▶└───────┘
                  │ PA, LNA        │                                
                  └────────────────┘                                

  (PA = power amplifier on transmit; LNA = low-noise amplifier on receive)

# 2. Why it matters

Each new cellular band adds filters and switches, so content per phone rises with 5G. Customer concentration (Apple, Samsung) is high.

# 3. Materials

PAs often use GaAs or GaN; filters use BAW/SAW acoustic resonators.
