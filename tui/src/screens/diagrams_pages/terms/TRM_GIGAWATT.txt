# 1. What it is: the unit of AI capacity

   ┌──────────────┐   ┌──────────┐   ┌─────────────────┐
   │ 1 MW         │──▶│ 100 MW   │──▶│ 1 GW            │
   │ ~1,000 homes │   │ large DC │   │ AI campus       │
   │ ~10 racks    │   └──────────┘   │ ~1 nuclear unit │
   └──────────────┘                  └─────────────────┘

  (capacity = MW; energy over time = MWh; a GB200 NVL72 rack draws ~120-130 kW)

# 2. Why AI cares

Companies now report contracted and planned capacity in MW or GW, so the number tracks AI build-out directly.
