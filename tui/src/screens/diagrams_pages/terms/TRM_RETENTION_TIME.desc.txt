Retention time is how long a DRAM cell keeps enough charge to be read correctly after it is written. Standard DDR specifications require every row to be refreshed within a window of about 64 ms, which sets the refresh schedule. Shorter retention, which rises with heat, forces more refresh and costs bandwidth and power in AI servers. Related: Micron (MU), SK hynix (000660.KS), Samsung (005930.KS).
