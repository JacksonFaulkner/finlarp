# 1. What it is: a grid where data pulses cell to cell

   weights ▼        ▼        ▼
        ┌─────┐  ┌─────┐  ┌─────┐
 data ─▶│ MAC │─▶│ MAC │─▶│ MAC │
        └──┬──┘  └──┬──┘  └──┬──┘
        ┌──▼──┐  ┌──▼──┐  ┌──▼──┐
 data ─▶│ MAC │─▶│ MAC │─▶│ MAC │
        └──┬──┘  └──┬──┘  └──┬──┘
           ▼        ▼        ▼  partial sums out

  (each cell multiplies, adds, and passes data right and down)

# 2. Why AI cares

Reusing operands inside the grid cuts memory traffic, which is the main cost in matrix math.

# 3. Business angle

Explains why custom ASICs can match GPUs on narrow workloads at lower power.
