Fire suppression systems detect a fire and put it out with water mist, inert gas or a clean agent, which avoids the damage that sprinkler water does to electronics. Smoke or heat sensors, and in some rooms very early warning sensing, trigger the release in a zone. In data centers the system has to act quickly, stay safe for people and suit each room. Related: Carrier Global (CARR), Johnson Controls (JCI), Halma (HLMA.L), Honeywell (HON).
