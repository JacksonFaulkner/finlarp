An MRDIMM (multiplexed rank DIMM) is a server memory module that reads two ranks of DRAM at once and combines them through a mux buffer chip, doubling data rate to the CPU. It raises bandwidth per channel to 8800 MT/s and beyond using standard DDR5 chips. It targets memory-bound server workloads, including AI inference on CPUs. Related: Rambus (RMBS), Montage Technology (688008.SS), Micron (MU), SK hynix (000660.KS).
