The EU AI Act, Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, is the first broad legal framework for artificial intelligence, and it sorts AI uses into risk tiers with different duties. It bans some practices outright, sets requirements for high-risk systems, and adds transparency and documentation duties for general-purpose AI models. For AI infrastructure it matters because model developers and deployers selling into the EU must meet those duties, with compliance cost and dates that the 2025 proposals may shift.
