Bitcoin mining is the process of securing the Bitcoin network by running SHA-256 hash computations on specialized machines and being paid in newly issued BTC plus transaction fees when a block is found. Revenue depends on the BTC price, network difficulty, and the power cost per kWh, so power access is the main lever for margins. Miners with cheap, large-scale power are now using their sites to host AI and HPC workloads. Related: IREN (IREN), Hut 8 (HUT), Cipher Mining (CIFR), Bitdeer (BTDR), Core Scientific (CORZ), TeraWulf (WULF).
