# 2. Why AI cares

Nuclear is one of the few firm, carbon-free power sources that AI campuses can contract for, and new reactors cannot be built without NRC licensing. Licensing timelines decide when small modular reactor power can actually reach a site. Plant restarts and power uprates also depend on NRC approvals.
