An optical circuit switch (OCS) redirects light from one fiber to another with tiny tilting mirrors or other optics, without converting the signal to electrical form. Because it never reads packets, it is protocol- and speed-agnostic and uses far less power per port than an electrical packet switch. Google uses OCS to reconfigure the topology of its TPU pods and data center spine. Connections change in milliseconds, so OCS suits slowly changing topologies, not per-packet routing. Related: Alphabet (GOOGL), Lumentum (LITE), Coherent (COHR), Ciena (CIEN).
