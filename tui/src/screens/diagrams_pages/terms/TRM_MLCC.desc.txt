A multilayer ceramic capacitor is a tiny stacked capacitor that stores charge and smooths voltage near chips. A phone holds about a thousand; an AI server board holds many thousands around the GPU and its power stages. Demand rises with power per board, and supply is concentrated in a few Japanese, Korean and Taiwanese makers. Related: Murata (MRAAY), Samsung Electro-Mechanics, TDK (TTDKY), Yageo, Vishay (VSH).
