# 2. Why AI cares

The AI Act sets obligations by risk tier, and high-risk system duties were scheduled to apply from August 2026, though 2025 proposals would push some dates back. Providers of general-purpose models face documentation and copyright-policy duties. Compliance cost falls on model developers and on companies that deploy AI products in the EU.
