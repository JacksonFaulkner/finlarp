Net revenue retention, or NRR, measures how much revenue a set of customers generates now compared with the same customers twelve months earlier. It includes expansion, contraction, and churn but excludes revenue from new customers. Values above 100% mean the installed base grows even without new logos. Companies that sell usage-based AI products can see NRR move sharply because spend tracks consumption. Related: Datadog (DDOG), Snowflake (SNOW), MongoDB (MDB), Zscaler (ZS).
