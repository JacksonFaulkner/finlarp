Customer concentration is the share of revenue that comes from a few large buyers. Filers must disclose any customer that accounts for 10% or more of revenue, and the risk factors usually name the largest buyers. For AI chip and server suppliers, a few hyperscalers often make up a large share, so one order change can move the whole company. Related: NVIDIA (NVDA), Broadcom (AVGO), Marvell (MRVL), Super Micro Computer (SMCI).
