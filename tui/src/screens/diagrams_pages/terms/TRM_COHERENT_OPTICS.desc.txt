Coherent optics encode data in the phase, amplitude and polarization of light, not just its on/off intensity, and recover it with a local laser and a powerful DSP. This packs more bits per symbol and corrects fiber impairments electronically, enabling hundreds of kilometers of reach at 400G-1.6T per wavelength. Coherent was once limited to large line-card systems, but 400ZR and 800ZR pluggables now put it in switch ports for data center interconnect. Demand rises as AI campuses link multiple buildings and sites. Related: Ciena (CIEN), Marvell (MRVL), Cisco (CSCO), Coherent (COHR), Lumentum (LITE).
