NAND flash is non-volatile memory that keeps data with the power off, built today as 3D NAND with 200-300+ stacked layers of cells. Each cell holds 1 to 4 bits (SLC, MLC, TLC, QLC); more bits per cell means cheaper storage but slower writes and lower endurance. It is the storage medium inside every SSD, and AI data centers buy it for training datasets, checkpoints and inference caches.
