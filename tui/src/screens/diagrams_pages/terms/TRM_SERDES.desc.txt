A SerDes (serializer/deserializer) is the circuit at the edge of a chip that turns wide, slow parallel data into one very fast serial lane and back again. Every GPU, switch ASIC, NIC and optical DSP is ringed with SerDes lanes, and their speed per lane (56G, 112G, now 224G) sets how much bandwidth leaves a chip. Designing fast, low-power SerDes is hard, so it is a key differentiator and licensable IP. Related: Broadcom (AVGO), Marvell (MRVL), Credo (CRDO), Alphawave (AWE), Synopsys (SNPS), Cadence (CDNS).
