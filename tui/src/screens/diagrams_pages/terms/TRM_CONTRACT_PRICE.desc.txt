A contract price is the negotiated price large buyers pay memory makers for DRAM or NAND, usually set monthly or quarterly. It differs from the spot price, which is the daily price for small lots and tends to move first. Contract price trends drive memory makers' revenue and margins, so they are watched closely in earnings calls. Related: Micron (MU), Samsung (005930.KS), SK hynix (000660.KS), Sandisk (SNDK).
