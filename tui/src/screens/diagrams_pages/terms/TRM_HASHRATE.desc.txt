Hashrate is the number of hash computations per second that the Bitcoin network, or a single miner fleet, performs, usually quoted in EH/s (exahashes per second). A higher network hashrate raises mining difficulty, which spreads block rewards across more machines and lowers each miner's share. Fleet hashrate times efficiency (joules per terahash) sets how much power a miner draws per unit of output. Related: IREN (IREN), Marathon Digital (MARA), Riot Platforms (RIOT), Bitdeer (BTDR).
