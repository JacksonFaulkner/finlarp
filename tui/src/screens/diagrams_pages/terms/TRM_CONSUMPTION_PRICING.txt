# 1. What it is: billed on metered usage

┌──────────────────────────────────┐
│ customer usage                   │
│ API calls, tokens, GPU-hours     │
└──────────────────────────────────┘
          │ x unit price
          ▼
┌──────────────────────────────────┐
│ monthly invoice                  │
│ usage x rate, plus minimums      │
└──────────────────────────────────┘

(often paired with a committed minimum spend the customer pays regardless of usage)

# 2. Why AI cares

Fees track what a customer actually consumes, such as tokens, GPU-hours, or stored gigabytes, instead of a fixed seat count. Revenue rises when usage rises, which can make growth fast in a boom and reverse quickly when customers cut back. For AI vendors, revenue follows inference volume, so forecasting is harder than with subscriptions.
