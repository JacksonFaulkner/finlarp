A CRAH (computer room air handler) is a floor-standing unit that draws warm air from a data hall, cools it across a chilled-water coil and blows the cold air back toward the racks. It is the room-level cooler in many older and air-cooled halls, usually paired with raised floors or overhead ducting. CRAH units are giving way to in-row units and liquid loops as rack heat rises.
