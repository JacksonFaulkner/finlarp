Massive MIMO is a radio design that puts dozens or hundreds of antenna elements on one base station and serves many users at once using beamforming. Each extra antenna raises the number of spatial streams, which raises capacity without needing more spectrum. The approach is a core part of 5G mid-band radios, so it drives antenna, RF and baseband content per site. Related: Ericsson (ERIC), Nokia (NOK), Qualcomm (QCOM), Skyworks (SWKS).
