# 1. What it is: power wires on the back, signals on the front

   ┌───────────────┐   ┌──────────────┐   ┌─────────────┐
   │ FRONT METAL   │   │ TRANSISTORS  │   │ BACK METAL  │
   │ signal wiring │──▶│ thin silicon │──▶│ power rails │
   │ only          │   │ layer        │   │ to bumps    │
   └───────────────┘   └──────────────┘   └─────────────┘

  (old way: power and signal share the front stack and fight for space)

# 2. Why it matters

Shorter, thicker power paths mean less IR drop, so chips hold voltage under heavy AI load. Freed front-side tracks let designers pack cells tighter.

# 3. Cost

The wafer is bonded to a carrier and thinned to a few microns, adding bonding, thinning and alignment steps.
