# 1. What it is: a country buys its own AI cloud

   ┌────────────────┐    ┌───────────────────┐    ┌───────────────────┐    ┌────────────────────┐
   │ GOVERNMENT     │───▶│ NATIONAL OPERATOR │───▶│ LOCAL DATA CENTER │───▶│ LOCAL MODELS       │
   │ budget, policy │    │ telco / champion  │    │ GPUs in-country   │    │ own language, data │
   └────────────────┘    └───────────────────┘    └───────────────────┘    └────────────────────┘

  (examples cited in filings: Saudi Arabia, UAE, Japan, France, India)

# 2. Why it matters

Sovereign buyers add demand that is not tied to the big US cloud capex budgets. NVIDIA has called out sovereign AI as a revenue driver in earnings calls.

# 3. Risks

Shipments to many of these countries need US export licenses, so timing depends on policy as much as on the customer.
