A CMOS image sensor is a grid of light-sensitive pixels on a silicon chip that turns light into digital values, and it is the camera chip in phones, cars and robots. Automotive versions must hold high dynamic range at tunnel exits and headlights, and they must suppress LED flicker. Stacked designs put the pixel and logic layers on separate wafers, which lets the sensor carry more processing. Related: Sony (6758.T), onsemi (ON), Samsung (005930.KS), STMicroelectronics (STM).
