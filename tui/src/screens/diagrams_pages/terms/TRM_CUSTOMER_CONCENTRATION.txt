# 1. What it is: how much revenue comes from a few buyers

 ┌───────────────┐
 │ TOTAL REVENUE │  $10.0B
 └───────┬───────┘
         │ split by customer
 ┌───────┴───────┐
 │ CUSTOMER A    │  $4.0B = 40%
 │ CUSTOMER B    │  $2.5B = 25%
 │ all others    │  $3.5B = 35%
 └───────────────┘

# 2. Why it matters

Under ASC 280, any customer at 10% or more of revenue must be named or flagged in the filing. Losing or cutting one of those buyers can hit revenue faster than most other risks.
