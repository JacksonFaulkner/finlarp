MEMS (micro-electromechanical systems) are tiny silicon machines, such as moving mirrors, cantilevers and membranes, built with chip-style lithography and etching. They sit inside accelerometers, gyroscopes, microphones, pressure sensors and the beam-steering mirrors of some LiDAR units. Their value is low cost and small size at high volume, which is why they appear in cars, phones and robots. Related: STMicroelectronics (STM), Infineon (IFNNY), Knowles (KN), TDK (6762.T).
