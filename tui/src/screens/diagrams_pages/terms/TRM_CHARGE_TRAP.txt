# 1. What it is: flash that stores charge in a nitride film

┌────────────────────────────────────────┐
│        CONTROL GATE (word line)        │  gate voltage reads and programs the cell
├────────────────────────────────────────┤
│             blocking oxide             │  keeps charge from leaking to the gate
├────────────────────────────────────────┤
│       silicon nitride trap layer       │  charge sits in traps, not a metal island
├────────────────────────────────────────┤
│          tunnel oxide (thin)           │  electrons tunnel through on write/erase
├────────────────────────────────────────┤
│    n+ source   p channel   n+ drain    │  silicon substrate
└────────────────────────────────────────┘

(charge sits in traps inside silicon nitride, not in a conductive island)

# 2. Why it matters

Local charge storage lets cells sit closer together and tolerates single defects, which is why many 3D NAND stacks use it.
