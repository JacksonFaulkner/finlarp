CSRD, the Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive, is the EU rule that requires large companies to publish audited sustainability disclosures under the European Sustainability Reporting Standards, or ESRS. The disclosures cover emissions, energy, water, and workforce topics. For AI infrastructure it matters because energy use and data center emissions become audited figures that investors and customers compare across build-out plans. Related: Schneider Electric (SU.PA), Siemens (SIE.DE), Vertiv (VRT).
