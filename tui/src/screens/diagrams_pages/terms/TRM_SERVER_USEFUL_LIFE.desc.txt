Servers and network gear are depreciated over an estimated useful life, which hyperscalers raised from about 3 to 5-6 years between 2020 and 2023. A longer life spreads the cost over more years and raises reported earnings. AI GPUs improve fast, so critics argue their economic life may be shorter; Amazon cut some server lives back to 5 years in 2025. Changes are disclosed as accounting estimate changes with the dollar effect on operating income. Related: Microsoft (MSFT), Alphabet (GOOGL), Amazon (AMZN), Meta (META), CoreWeave (CRWV).
