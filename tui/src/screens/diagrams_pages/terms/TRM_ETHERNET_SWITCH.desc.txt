An Ethernet switch forwards packets between servers; its core is a switch ASIC with many high-speed SerDes ports. In AI data centers, Ethernet switches built on chips like Broadcom Tomahawk or Nvidia Spectrum-X connect thousands of GPU servers in the back-end network. Switch bandwidth doubles roughly every two years (51.2T, then 102.4T per chip) and AI back-end networks are a large, growing market. Related: Arista (ANET), Broadcom (AVGO), Nvidia (NVDA), Cisco (CSCO), Celestica (CLS), Marvell (MRVL).
