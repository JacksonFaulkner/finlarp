# 1. What it is: how many years a server's cost is spread over

   ┌─────────────┐    ┌─────────────────┐    ┌────────────────┐
   │ SERVER COST │───▶│ USEFUL LIFE     │───▶│ DEPRECIATION   │
   │ $ capex     │    │ 3 / 5 / 6 years │    │ yearly expense │
   └─────────────┘    └─────────────────┘    └────────────────┘

  (cost / life = yearly expense; longer life = lower expense, higher profit)

# 2. Why it matters

Depreciation is becoming the largest cost line for AI clouds. A one-year change in useful life can move operating income by billions.
