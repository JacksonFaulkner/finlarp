# 1. What it is: a high-voltage power switch

   ┌───────────┐    ┌────────────────┐    ┌──────────────┐
   │ DC bus    │    │ IGBT module    │    │ motor / grid │
   │ 600-6500V │───▶│ switch + diode │───▶│ AC out       │
   └───────────┘    └────────────────┘    └──────────────┘

  (slower switching than SiC, but lower cost per amp)

# 2. Why it matters

IGBT modules sit in industrial drives, grid equipment and UPS systems that back up data centers. Demand tracks industrial capex and renewables.
