# 1. What it is: a network card that does its own processing

   ┌───────────┐   ┌────────────────┐   ┌────────┐
   │ GPU / CPU │──▶│ SMARTNIC       │──▶│ SWITCH │
   └───────────┘   │ RDMA, crypto,  │   └────────┘
                   │ offload engine │
                   └────────────────┘

  (AI servers: ~1 NIC per GPU at 400G-800G)

# 2. Why AI cares

GPU-to-GPU traffic across servers runs through NICs; their speed and congestion handling affect training time.

# 3. Business angle

NIC units grow with GPU count, adding networking revenue per accelerator sold.
