A power purchase agreement is a long-term contract, often 10-20 years, to buy electricity from a specific generator at a set price. Hyperscalers use PPAs to lock in supply and meet clean-energy goals. For generators, a PPA gives revenue certainty that helps finance new plants. Related: NextEra Energy (NEE), Constellation Energy (CEG), Brookfield Renewable (BEP), Talen Energy (TLN).
