# 1. What it is: sunlight to AC power

   ┌───────────┐   ┌──────────┐   ┌─────────┐
   │ PV PANELS │──▶│ INVERTER │──▶│ GRID    │
   │ DC power  │   │ DC to AC │   │ daytime │
   └───────────┘   └──────────┘   └─────────┘

  (capacity factor ~20-30%, so 1 GW of solar averages 200-300 MW)

# 2. Why AI cares

Solar is quick to build and cheap per MWh, but AI loads run 24x7, so it usually needs storage or other firm power alongside.
