SASE (Secure Access Service Edge) is a cloud architecture that combines SD-WAN networking with security services such as secure web gateways, cloud access security brokers, zero trust access and firewalls. Traffic from branches, laptops and devices goes to nearby cloud points of presence, where one policy is applied everywhere. The term comes from a Gartner report, and it replaces separate appliances at each site. Related: Zscaler (ZS), Netskope (NTSK), Cloudflare (NET), Palo Alto Networks (PANW).
