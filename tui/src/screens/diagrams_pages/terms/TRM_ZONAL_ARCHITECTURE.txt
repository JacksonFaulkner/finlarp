# 1. What it is: zones report to a few central computers

   ┌─────────────────────┐   ┌─────────────────┐   ┌──────────┐   ┌─────────────────┐
   │ SENSORS + ACTUATORS │──▶│ ZONE CONTROLLER │──▶│ ETHERNET │──▶│ CENTRAL COMPUTE │
   └─────────────────────┘   └─────────────────┘   └──────────┘   └─────────────────┘

  (fewer, smarter boxes replace dozens of single-purpose ECUs)

# 2. Why it matters

Fewer, smarter boxes mean less wire and simpler software. The central computer becomes a high-value automotive chip, and zone controllers become a new silicon category.
