LNG is natural gas cooled to about -162 C so that it becomes a liquid roughly 600 times denser than gas, which makes it practical to ship by sea. Liquefaction plants, ships, and regasification terminals form a long supply chain that links gas fields to power plants and industry in other countries. Data centers that run gas-fired power depend on this chain for fuel, and grid buildouts for AI load often depend on gas supply. Price and capacity swings in LNG pass through to power costs in importing regions. Related: Cheniere Energy (LNG), Venture Global (VG), Sempra (SRE)
