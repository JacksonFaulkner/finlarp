Pillar Two is the OECD global minimum tax framework, part of the BEPS 2.0 project, which sets a 15 percent minimum effective tax rate for large multinational groups in each country where they operate. If a jurisdiction's effective rate is below that level, a top-up tax applies. For AI infrastructure it matters because hyperscalers and chipmakers that used low-tax locations for data centers, IP, or manufacturing can face higher effective rates.
