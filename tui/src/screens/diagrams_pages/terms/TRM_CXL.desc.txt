CXL (Compute Express Link) is a protocol that runs over PCIe wiring and lets a CPU or GPU share memory with attached devices as if it were local. Its main use in AI servers is memory expansion and pooling: extra DRAM sits on a CXL card or in a shared box, so a server can reach more memory than its own DIMM slots hold, at higher latency than local DRAM. Related: Astera Labs (ALAB), Marvell (MRVL), Micron (MU), Samsung (005930.KS).
