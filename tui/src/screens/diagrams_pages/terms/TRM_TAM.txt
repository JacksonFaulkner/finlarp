# 1. What it is: the ceiling on a market

 ┌───────────────┐
 │ TAM           │  all spend on the category
 └───────┬───────┘
         │ cut to products the company sells
 ┌───────┴───────┐
 │ SAM           │  serviceable market
 └───────┬───────┘
         │ cut to a realistic share
 ┌───────┴───────┐
 │ SOM           │  obtainable share
 └───────────────┘

# 2. Why it matters

TAM is an estimate, so the source and year matter. A large TAM does not mean revenue follows; compare it with the revenue the company actually reports.
