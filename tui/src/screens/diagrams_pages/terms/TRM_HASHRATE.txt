# 1. What it is: hash attempts per second

   ┌──────────────┐
   │ hash attempts│
   │ per second   │
   └──────────────┘
          ▼
   ┌──────────────┐
   │ EH/s fleet   │
   └──────────────┘

  (1 EH/s = 10^18 hashes per second)

# 2. Why it matters

Hashrate in EH/s and efficiency in J/TH together set power draw. A fleet's share of network hashrate determines its share of block rewards.
