A process node is a named generation of chip manufacturing technology, such as TSMC N3 or Intel 18A. The names no longer match any physical dimension; they mark steps in transistor density, power and speed. AI accelerators, CPUs and phone chips race to the newest node because each step gives roughly 10-30% better power or performance. Only a few companies can afford leading-edge nodes, which concentrates supply. Related: Taiwan Semiconductor (TSM), Intel (INTC), Samsung, NVIDIA (NVDA), Apple (AAPL).
