A robotaxi is a self-driving car run as a paid ride-hailing service with no human driver on board. The operator owns the vehicle, the autonomy stack, the remote assistance team and the depot, so unit economics depend on utilization, vehicle cost and sensor cost. Commercial service is still limited to a small number of cities, and local permits gate each new one. Related: Alphabet (GOOGL), Tesla (TSLA), Uber (UBER), Pony.ai (PONY).
