An HDD (hard disk drive) stores data magnetically on spinning platters read by a moving head. It is far slower than an SSD but costs several times less per terabyte, so it still holds most of the world's cloud bulk data. AI raises demand for HDDs because training data, logs and generated content pile up in cheap capacity tiers. Related: Seagate (STX), Western Digital (WDC), Toshiba (6502.T).
