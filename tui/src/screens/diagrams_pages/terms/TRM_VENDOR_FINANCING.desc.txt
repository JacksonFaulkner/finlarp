Vendor financing is when a supplier lends money, guarantees debt, or invests in a customer that then buys the supplier's products. In AI infrastructure, a chip or cloud vendor may fund a neocloud or startup whose spending returns to the vendor as revenue. The revenue is real, but it depends on the customer's ability to repay, so the arrangements are scrutinized in the commitments and guarantees notes. Related: NVIDIA (NVDA), CoreWeave (CRWV), Oracle (ORCL), Advanced Micro Devices (AMD).
