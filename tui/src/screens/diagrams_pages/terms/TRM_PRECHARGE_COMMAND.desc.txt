The PRECHARGE command closes the open row in a DRAM bank, writing the row buffer back and resetting the bitlines so the bank can open another row. It is the second half of a row switch, paired with ACTIVATE. Random access that keeps switching rows pays this cost repeatedly, which is why memory controllers schedule requests to reuse open rows. Related: Micron (MU), SK hynix (000660.KS), Rambus (RMBS).
