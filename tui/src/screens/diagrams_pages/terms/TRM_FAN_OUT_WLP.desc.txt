Fan-out wafer-level packaging places known-good dies on a reconstituted carrier, molds them in epoxy, and builds a redistribution layer that fans the I/O out beyond the die edge. It lets a package carry more I/O than the die itself would allow, without a silicon interposer for many mobile and some AI parts. TSMC's InFO line and Amkor's fan-out lines are the main production examples. Related: TSMC (TSM), Amkor (AMKR), ASE (ASX).
