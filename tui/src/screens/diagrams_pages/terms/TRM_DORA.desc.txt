DORA, the Digital Operational Resilience Act, is an EU regulation that sets ICT risk, incident reporting, and resilience-testing rules for banks, insurers, and investment firms. It also lets EU supervisors oversee the cloud and software providers those firms rely on, which it calls critical ICT third-party providers. For AI infrastructure it matters because financial customers must audit and govern their cloud and AI vendors, which adds contract terms such as audit rights and exit plans.
