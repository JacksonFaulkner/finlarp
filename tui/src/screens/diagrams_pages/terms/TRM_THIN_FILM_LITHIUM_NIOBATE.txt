# 1. What it is: a fast modulator crystal, thinned onto a wafer

   ┌──────────────────────────────────────┐
   │ electrodes (RF data signal)          │
   ├──────────────────────────────────────┤
   │ LiNbO3 film, < 1 um  ◀ light guide   │
   ├──────────────────────────────────────┤
   │ oxide (buried insulator)             │
   ├──────────────────────────────────────┤
   │ silicon handle wafer                 │
   └──────────────────────────────────────┘

  (Pockels effect: index changes linearly with voltage, very fast)

# 2. Why it matters

Moving to 200G and 400G per lane strains silicon modulators. TFLN offers more bandwidth at lower voltage, so it is a candidate for next-gen transceivers.
