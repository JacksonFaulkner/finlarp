An ASIC is a chip designed for one job, with fixed logic rather than general-purpose cores. In AI, hyperscalers co-design custom accelerators (often called XPUs) with chip partners to run their own models at lower cost and power than merchant GPUs. ASICs trade flexibility and a large up-front design cost for efficiency at high volume. Related: Broadcom (AVGO), Marvell Technology (MRVL), Alphabet (GOOGL), Amazon (AMZN), Alchip, Taiwan Semiconductor (TSM).
