Curtailment is a grid operator's instruction to a large load, such as a bitcoin miner, to reduce or stop power draw during peak demand or congestion. Miners that agree to curtail earn credits or lower rates, but they lose mining revenue during the shutdown. The practice is a source of income for some public miners and a risk to others that depend on continuous power. Related: IREN (IREN), Riot Platforms (RIOT), Marathon Digital (MARA), Bitdeer (BTDR).
