Fiber to the home runs optical fiber all the way from the central office to the customer premise, usually using a passive optical network that splits one fiber among 32 or 64 homes with passive splitters. It offers symmetric multi-gigabit speeds and is the main alternative to cable broadband. Build-out is capex-heavy and drives demand for fiber, optical line terminals and drop cable. Related: Corning (GLW), Calix (CALX), Frontier (FYBR), Verizon (VZ).
