A zonal architecture groups a car's sensors and actuators by physical zone, with local zone controllers that connect to a few central computers over Ethernet. It replaces the older design of many separate ECUs wired point to point, which cuts harness weight and copper and allows software updates across the car. The shift matters to chip suppliers because it moves compute toward central high-performance SoCs. Related: NXP Semiconductors (NXPI), Infineon (IFNNY), Renesas (6723.T), Marvell (MRVL).
