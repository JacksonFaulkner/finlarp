# 1. What it is: copper cable with a chip in each end

   ┌──────────┐     ┌──────────────┐                 ┌──────────────┐     ┌────────┐
   │ SERVER   │────▶│ AEC END      │──copper 3-7 m──▶│ AEC END      │────▶│ SWITCH │
   │ NIC port │     │ retimer chip │                 │ retimer chip │     │ port   │
   └──────────┘     └──────────────┘                 └──────────────┘     └────────┘

  (passive copper ~2 m, AEC ~7 m, optics 100 m+)

# 2. Why AI cares

Racks hold more GPUs and lane speeds keep rising; AECs fill the reach gap between passive copper and optics at lower cost and power.
