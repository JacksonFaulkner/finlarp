Total addressable market, or TAM, is the total annual spending on a product category if the company captured all of it. Companies narrow it to a serviceable market (SAM), the part their products can reach, and then to an obtainable share (SOM). Management and analysts usually publish these figures on earnings calls, and the methods are rarely disclosed in filings. Related: NVIDIA (NVDA), Broadcom (AVGO), Micron (MU), Arista Networks (ANET).
