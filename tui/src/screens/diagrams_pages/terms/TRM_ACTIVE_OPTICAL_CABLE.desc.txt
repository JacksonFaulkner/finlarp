An active optical cable (AOC) is a fiber cable with transceivers permanently attached at both ends, plugging into ports like a copper cable. Because the optics are sealed in, there are no separate connectors to clean and the vendor can tune the link as one unit. AOCs reach farther than copper (tens of meters versus a few meters) and are common for GPU-to-switch links inside AI rows. They compete with active electrical cables (AECs) at short reach and with separate transceivers plus fiber at longer reach. Related: Coherent (COHR), Nvidia (NVDA), Amphenol (APH), Credo (CRDO).
