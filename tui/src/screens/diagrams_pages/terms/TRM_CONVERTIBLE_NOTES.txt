# 1. What it is: debt that can turn into shares

 ┌───────────────┐
 │ BOND ISSUED   │  $1.0B, low coupon
 │ to investors  │
 └───────┬───────┘
         │ holder may convert if stock > conversion price
 ┌───────┴───────┐
 │ SHARES        │  conversion price $100
 │ (if converted)│  = ~10M new shares
 └───────────────┘

# 2. Why it matters

Cheap debt helps fund buildouts, but the conversion feature can dilute shareholders later. Check the diluted share count and the capped-call terms in the notes to the filing.
