Utilization is the share of a fab's capacity that is running. Fabs have high fixed costs, so utilization drives gross margin: a full fab spreads depreciation over more wafers. Memory makers and IDMs cite underutilization charges in downturns. AI demand keeps leading-edge and advanced packaging lines full while mature-node fabs can sit underused. Related: Taiwan Semiconductor (TSM), Micron (MU), Texas Instruments (TXN), GlobalFoundries (GFS).
