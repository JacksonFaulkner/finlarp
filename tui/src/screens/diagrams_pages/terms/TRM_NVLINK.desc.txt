NVLink is Nvidia's proprietary high-bandwidth link that connects GPUs directly to each other, bypassing PCIe. With NVSwitch chips it forms a single fast domain across many GPUs, such as 72 GPUs in a GB200 NVL72 rack, letting them act like one large accelerator. This scale-up fabric is a major part of Nvidia's system advantage; NVLink Fusion licenses it to third-party CPUs and accelerators. Related: Nvidia (NVDA), Astera Labs (ALAB), Marvell (MRVL), AMD (AMD) with UALink as an alternative.
