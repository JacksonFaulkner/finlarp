Energized capacity is the megawatts of a site that are connected to the grid and can actually deliver power to equipment, as opposed to megawatts that are only contracted or planned. Companies report it separately because a site can be fully contracted but not yet energized, which means no revenue until the substation and switchgear are complete. Investors use energized MW to judge how fast a miner can turn power rights into AI revenue. Related: IREN (IREN), Hut 8 (HUT), Core Scientific (CORZ), TeraWulf (WULF).
