Enrichment raises the share of U-235 in uranium to around 3-5% for current reactors, or up to ~20% (HALEU) for many advanced designs. Capacity is concentrated in a few firms, and Russian supply is being phased out of US markets. More reactors for AI power mean more demand for enrichment services. Related: Centrus Energy (LEU), Cameco (CCJ), Urenco (private).
