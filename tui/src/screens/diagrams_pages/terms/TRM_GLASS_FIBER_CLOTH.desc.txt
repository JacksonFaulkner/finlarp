Glass fiber cloth is a woven fabric of fine glass yarns that reinforces the resin in copper clad laminate and in some package substrates. Standard E-glass works for common boards, while AI-grade substrates need low-dielectric or low-expansion glass such as T-glass or quartz-based yarns. Only a few makers, mainly in Japan and Taiwan, produce these specialty grades at volume. Shortages of these grades in 2024 and 2025 limited laminate and substrate output as AI demand grew.
