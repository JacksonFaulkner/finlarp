Backlog is signed customer orders not yet delivered or recognized as revenue. Under US GAAP companies report remaining performance obligations (RPO), which covers contracted revenue still to be earned. For AI it shows how much future demand is locked in: Oracle's and CoreWeave's RPO jumped on multi-year AI compute contracts. Backlog can be cancelled or delayed, so its quality matters as much as its size. Related: Oracle (ORCL), CoreWeave (CRWV), Microsoft (MSFT), Vertiv (VRT), GE Vernova (GEV).
