HAMR (heat-assisted magnetic recording) is a hard-disk technique that uses a tiny laser on the write head to heat a spot of the platter so it can be written at a much smaller size. It lets each platter hold more data, pushing drive capacity past 30 TB. It is the main path for HDD density growth this decade, with Seagate shipping first and others following. Related: Seagate (STX), Western Digital (WDC), Toshiba (6502.T).
