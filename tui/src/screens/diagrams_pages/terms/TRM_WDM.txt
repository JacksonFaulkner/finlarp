# 1. What it is: many colors of light on one fiber

   ┌──────────┐
   │ laser λ1 │──┐
   └──────────┘  │   ┌──────────┐            ┌──────────┐   ──▶ λ1
   ┌──────────┐  ├──▶│ MUX      │═══ fiber ═▶│ DEMUX    │   ──▶ λ2
   │ laser λ2 │──┤   │ combine  │            │ split    │   ──▶ λ3
   └──────────┘  │   └──────────┘            └──────────┘   ──▶ λ4
   ┌──────────┐  │
   │ laser λ3 │──┤   (CWDM: ~4-18 channels, 20 nm apart)
   └──────────┘  │   (DWDM: 40-100+ channels, ~0.4-0.8 nm apart)
   ┌──────────┐  │
   │ laser λ4 │──┘
   └──────────┘

# 2. Why it matters

Fiber is expensive to lay, so WDM is how carriers and cloud companies grow capacity. Inside data centers, 4-lane CWDM lets one fiber pair carry a 400G or 800G link.
