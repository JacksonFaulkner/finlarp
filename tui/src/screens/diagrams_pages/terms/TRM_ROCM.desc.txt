ROCm is AMD's open-source software platform for programming its GPUs, including the Instinct data center accelerators. It provides compilers, math and communication libraries, and the HIP layer for porting CUDA code. Its maturity affects how easily customers can move AI workloads from NVIDIA to AMD. AMD cites ROCm improvements as key to its data center GPU growth. Related: Advanced Micro Devices (AMD), NVIDIA (NVDA), Microsoft (MSFT), Meta (META).
