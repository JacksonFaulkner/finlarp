# 1. What it is: power and cooling wrapped around servers

   ┌───────────────────┐     ┌──────────────────┐     ┌──────────────────┐     ┌──────────────────┐
   │ GRID / SUBSTATION │ ──▶ │ UPS + SWITCHGEAR │ ──▶ │ IT HALL          │ ──▶ │ COOLING          │
   │ utility power     │     │ backup gensets   │     │ racks of servers │     │ chillers, towers │
   └───────────────────┘     └──────────────────┘     └──────────────────┘     └──────────────────┘

  (sized in MW of IT load; AI campuses now plan 300 MW - 1 GW+)

# 2. Why AI cares

Every GPU needs power, cooling and a rack slot. Data center capacity, mostly limited by grid connections, gates how fast AI compute can be deployed.

# 3. Who builds them

Hyperscalers self-build; colocation REITs and neoclouds lease or build for others. Suppliers include electrical, cooling and construction firms.
