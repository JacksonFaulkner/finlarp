Time-of-flight (ToF) sensors emit a short infrared pulse or modulated light and time how long it takes to return at each pixel, which gives a depth map from one chip. They are used for face unlock, gesture input, in-cabin monitoring and obstacle detection on mobile robots. Range is short compared with LiDAR and sunlight adds noise, but the parts are small and cheap. Related: STMicroelectronics (STM), Sony (6758.T), ams OSRAM (AMS.SW), Infineon (IFNNY).
