   charge

   │ ██
   │ ██╲
   │    ╲

   │       ╲

   write     refresh                 lost
             ◀──── retention time ────▶

  (cell charge leaks away; each row must be refreshed before it drops)

# 2. Why AI cares

Retention sets how often DRAM must be refreshed. Refresh takes time and power, so retention and refresh overhead show up in effective bandwidth and energy per bit.
