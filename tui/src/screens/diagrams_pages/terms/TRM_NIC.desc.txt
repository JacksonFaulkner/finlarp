A network interface card (NIC) connects a server to the network, converting data in memory into packets on the wire. AI servers often have one high-speed NIC (400G-800G) per GPU for the back-end network, plus SmartNICs or DPUs that offload networking, storage and security tasks from the CPU. NIC attach rate rises with GPU count, making it a meaningful product line. Related: Nvidia (NVDA), Broadcom (AVGO), AMD (AMD), Marvell (MRVL), Intel (INTC).
