A data center is a building that houses servers, storage and networking with the power, cooling and fiber needed to keep them running. Capacity is measured in megawatts of IT load rather than floor space. AI training and inference have pushed new campuses to hundreds of megawatts to gigawatts, making grid power the main constraint on growth. Related: Equinix (EQIX), Digital Realty (DLR), Vertiv (VRT), Nvidia (NVDA), Microsoft (MSFT).
