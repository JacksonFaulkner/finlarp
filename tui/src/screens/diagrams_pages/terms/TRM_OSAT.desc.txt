OSATs are contract companies that package and test chips after wafer fabrication. ASE and Amkor are the largest, with JCET and others in China. AI chips need advanced packaging such as 2.5D and chiplet integration, and OSATs are adding capacity alongside TSMC CoWoS. Testing and packaging cost rise with die size and HBM stacks. Related: Amkor (AMKR), ASE Technology (ASX), Taiwan Semiconductor (TSM).
