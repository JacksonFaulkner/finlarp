5G is the fifth generation of cellular wireless standards, defined by 3GPP, that uses new radio (NR) air interfaces across low, mid and millimeter-wave spectrum. It delivers higher peak speeds, lower latency and more connections per cell than 4G LTE, and standalone 5G adds a cloud-native core. Carriers spend heavily on 5G radios, backhaul and spectrum, and the traffic it carries feeds demand for edge compute.
