Wavelength division multiplexing (WDM) sends several signals down one fiber at once, each on its own color of laser light. A multiplexer combines the wavelengths at one end and a demultiplexer splits them at the other. Coarse WDM (CWDM) uses a few widely spaced colors for short links such as FR4 transceivers; dense WDM (DWDM) packs dozens of closely spaced channels for long-haul and metro networks. WDM multiplies fiber capacity without laying new fiber. Related: Ciena (CIEN), Coherent (COHR), Lumentum (LITE), Infinera, Nokia (NOK).
