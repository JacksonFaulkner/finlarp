# 1. What it is: orders signed but not yet delivered

   ┌────────────┐    ┌──────────────────┐    ┌──────────────┐
   │ NEW ORDERS │───▶│ BACKLOG / RPO    │───▶│ REVENUE      │
   │ bookings   │    │ signed, unearned │    │ as delivered │
   └────────────┘    └──────────────────┘    └──────────────┘

  (book-to-bill above 1.0 = backlog growing)

# 2. Reading it

Compare backlog growth with revenue growth. Note how much converts within 12 months, which companies usually disclose next to RPO.
