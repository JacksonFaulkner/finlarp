An at-the-market offering lets a company sell new shares gradually into the open market through a broker, instead of in one marketed deal. It is set up under a shelf registration and a sales agreement, with a dollar cap approved by the board. The company raises cash over time and the share count grows, which dilutes existing holders. Related: CoreWeave (CRWV), Nebius (NBIS), Super Micro Computer (SMCI), Applied Digital (APLD).
