# 1. What it is: sensors feed one computer that acts

   ┌─────────┐   ┌──────────┐   ┌───────────────┐
   │ SENSORS │──▶│ ADAS SOC │──▶│ BRAKE / STEER │
   └─────────┘   └──────────┘   └───────────────┘

  (levels: L0 warnings only, L2 hands-on steering assist, L3+ eyes-off)

# 2. Why it matters

Every new car ships with some ADAS, so camera, radar and chip content per vehicle is a volume market. Higher levels need more compute and redundant sensing, and that is where the spend per car grows.
