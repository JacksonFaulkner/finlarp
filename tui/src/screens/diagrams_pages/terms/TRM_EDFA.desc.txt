An erbium-doped fiber amplifier (EDFA) boosts light directly in the fiber, with no conversion to electrical form. A pump laser excites erbium ions in a short coil of special fiber, and passing signal light stimulates them to add photons at the same wavelength. One EDFA amplifies all DWDM channels in the C-band at once, which made long-haul WDM practical. Amplifiers sit every 60-100 km on long routes and inside ROADM nodes. Related: Lumentum (LITE), Coherent (COHR), Ciena (CIEN).
