Linear pluggable optics (LPO) are optical transceivers that drop the DSP chip and drive the laser and receiver with linear analog electronics, leaning on the switch or GPU SerDes to clean up the signal. Removing the DSP cuts module power by roughly 30-50% and lowers latency and cost. The tradeoff is shorter reach and tighter interoperability limits, so LPO targets short in-rack and in-row links in AI clusters. Linear receive optics (LRO) keep a DSP on the transmit side only, a middle ground. Related: Broadcom (AVGO), Marvell (MRVL), Arista (ANET), Coherent (COHR), Lumentum (LITE).
