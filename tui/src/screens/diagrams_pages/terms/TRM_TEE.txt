# 1. What it is: an isolated region of a processor

   ┌─────────────────────┐
   │ normal world (OS)   │
   │   app ──▶ call      │
   └──────────┬──────────┘
              ▼
   ┌─────────────────────┐
   │ secure world (TEE)  │
   │  keys + secrets     │
   └─────────────────────┘

(the OS can request work from the TEE but cannot read its memory)

# 2. Why AI cares

TEEs are smaller than full confidential VMs, so they suit narrow jobs such as key release for a model or verifying a device before it loads weights.
