Post-quantum cryptography is public-key cryptography designed to resist attacks from large quantum computers, which could break RSA and elliptic-curve schemes with Shor's algorithm. NIST finalized ML-KEM (FIPS 203) and ML-DSA (FIPS 204) in 2024 as replacements for key exchange and signatures. For infrastructure the cost is a multi-year upgrade of certificates, chips, HSMs and cloud services, and data recorded today can be decrypted later if it is not migrated.
