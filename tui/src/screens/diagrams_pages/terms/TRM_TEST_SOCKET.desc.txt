A test socket is a fixture that holds a packaged chip and presses its balls or pins against the tester's contacts during final test and burn-in. Sockets wear out and need frequent replacement, and high-speed sockets must keep signal integrity at very high data rates. Demand for sockets rises with the number of AI chips tested each year. Related: Advantest (6857.T), Teradyne (TER), Cohu (COHU).
