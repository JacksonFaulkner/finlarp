Analog chips handle continuous real-world signals: voltage, current, sound, temperature and radio. They include amplifiers, data converters, power regulators and interface chips, often built on older, depreciated fabs. The business has long product lives, many customers and high margins, and is tied to industrial and auto cycles. Related: Texas Instruments (TXN), Analog Devices (ADI), Microchip (MCHP), NXP (NXPI), onsemi (ON).
