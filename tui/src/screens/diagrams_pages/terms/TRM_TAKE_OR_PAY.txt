# 1. What it is: buyer commits to buy, or pays anyway

   ┌────────────────┐    ┌───────────────────┐    ┌────────────────┐
   │ BUYER          │───▶│ SELLER            │───▶│ SHORTFALL      │
   │ minimum volume │    │ reserved capacity │    │ buyer pays     │
   │ commitment     │    │ prepayment        │    │ the difference │
   └────────────────┘    └───────────────────┘    └────────────────┘

  (look for 'purchase obligations' and 'prepayments' in the notes)

# 2. Why it matters

These contracts move supply risk from seller to buyer. In a downturn the buyer may owe for chips it no longer needs, as NVIDIA's inventory and commitment charges have shown.
