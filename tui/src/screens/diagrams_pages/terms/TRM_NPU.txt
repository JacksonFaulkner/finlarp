# 1. What it is: an AI block inside a device chip

   ┌───────────┐   ┌─────────────────┐
   │ SoC       │   │ ON-DEVICE       │
   │ CPU cores │   │ speech, vision, │
   │ GPU       │──▶│ small LLMs      │
   │ NPU  ◀ AI │   └─────────────────┘
   └───────────┘

  (rated in TOPS at a few watts)

# 2. Why AI cares

Running models on the device cuts cloud cost and latency and keeps data local.

# 3. Business angle

NPU performance is used to market AI PCs and phones and can drive upgrade cycles.
