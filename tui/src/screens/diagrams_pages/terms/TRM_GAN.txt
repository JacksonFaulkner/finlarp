# 1. What it is: a faster, lower-loss power switch material

   ┌─────────────┐    ┌───────────────┐    ┌─────────────────┐    ┌─────────────┐
   │ AC / 48V in │    │ GaN FETs      │    │ small magnetics │    │ server rail │

  (higher switching frequency -> smaller inductors and capacitors per watt)

# 2. Why AI cares

AI racks push past 100 kW, and every point of conversion efficiency is heat that must be removed. GaN power supplies reach higher power density in the same rack slot.

# 3. Where it sells

Phone chargers were the first volume market; data center PSUs, EV onboard chargers and RF base stations follow.
