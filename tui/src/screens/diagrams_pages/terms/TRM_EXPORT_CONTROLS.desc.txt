Export controls are US rules, run by the Commerce Department's Bureau of Industry and Security (BIS), that limit which chips, tools and software can ship to which countries and companies. Since 2022 they have capped advanced AI GPU and chipmaking equipment sales to China and named firms on the Entity List. Companies disclose them as a risk factor and sometimes take inventory charges when a product is suddenly barred. They shape which SKUs get built and where revenue comes from.
