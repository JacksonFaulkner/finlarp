Capital expenditures are cash spent on long-lived assets such as data centers, servers, fabs and tools. Hyperscaler capex is the main funding source for the AI hardware boom: what Microsoft, Alphabet, Amazon and Meta spend shows up as revenue for chip, memory, networking and power suppliers. Capex hits the cash flow statement right away but reaches the income statement slowly through depreciation. Guidance for next year's capex is one of the most watched numbers on earnings calls. Related: Microsoft (MSFT), Alphabet (GOOGL), Amazon (AMZN), Meta (META), NVIDIA (NVDA).
