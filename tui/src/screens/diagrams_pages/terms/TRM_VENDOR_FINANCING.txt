# 1. What it is: the supplier funds the customer

 ┌───────────────┐
 │ CHIP VENDOR   │  lends or invests $1B
 └───────┬───────┘
         │ cash or guarantee to
 ┌───────┴───────┐
 │ CUSTOMER      │  often a neocloud
 └───────┬───────┘
         │ customer buys chips with the money
 ┌───────┴───────┐
 │ VENDOR REVENUE│  $1B back to the vendor
 └───────────────┘

# 2. Why it matters

The vendor books sales, but part of the cash comes from its own balance sheet. Look for these terms in the commitments, guarantees and related-party notes.
