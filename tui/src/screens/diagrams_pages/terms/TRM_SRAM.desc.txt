SRAM (static random access memory) is fast memory built from six transistors per bit, used for caches and buffers on the same die as the logic. It needs no refresh and answers in about a nanosecond, but it takes far more area per bit than DRAM. AI accelerators carry tens to hundreds of MB of SRAM, and some inference chips rely on it in place of HBM. Related: Nvidia (NVDA), TSMC (TSM), Groq (private), Cerebras (CBRS).
