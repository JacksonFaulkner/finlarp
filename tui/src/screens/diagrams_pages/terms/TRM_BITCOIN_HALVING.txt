# 1. What it is: block subsidy cut in half

   ┌────────┐      ┌────────┐      ┌─────────┐
   │ 6.25   │─────▶│ 3.125  │─────▶│ 1.5625  │
   │ BTC    │ 2024 │ BTC    │ 2028 │ BTC     │
   └────────┘      └────────┘      └─────────┘

  (each halving: roughly every 210,000 blocks, about 4 years)

# 2. Why it matters

Revenue per block falls by half at each halving, so miners need cheaper power or higher hashrate to keep margins. Fees and the BTC price offset some of the drop.
