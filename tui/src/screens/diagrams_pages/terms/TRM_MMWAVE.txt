# 1. What it is: fast radio with short reach

   ┌────────────┐  24-100 GHz  ┌──────────────┐
   │ mmWave     │─────────────▶│ PHASED ARRAY │
   │ small cell │◀─────────────│ front end    │
   └────────────┘              └──────────────┘
          ▲                            ▲
          │   blocked by walls,        │
          │   range ~hundreds of m     │

  (wide channels, high peak rate, short range)

# 2. Why it matters

mmWave needs many small sites and phased-array modules, so it adds RF content per site even though its coverage is limited.
