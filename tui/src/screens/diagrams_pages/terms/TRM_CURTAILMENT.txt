# 1. What it is: the grid asks a large load to cut power

   ┌────────────┐     ┌────────────┐     ┌────────────┐
   │ grid peak  │────▶│ ISO signal │────▶│ miner goes │
   │ demand     │     │ curtail    │     │ idle       │
   └────────────┘     └────────────┘     └────────────┘

  (the miner is paid for the power it did not use, or sells it back)

# 2. Why it matters

Curtailment income can offset part of the lost mining revenue. Frequent or long curtailment, though, cuts uptime for hosting customers who need continuous power.
