ADAS (advanced driver-assistance systems) is the set of sensors and software that warns a driver or steps in to brake, steer or hold a lane. It runs on cameras and radar, sometimes with LiDAR, feeding a central compute chip, and it is now standard equipment on new cars. For chip suppliers it is the largest high-volume automotive market for AI silicon, and moving from L2 assist to hands-off driving raises compute needs sharply. Related: Mobileye (MBLY), NVIDIA (NVDA), Qualcomm (QCOM), Ambarella (AMBA).
