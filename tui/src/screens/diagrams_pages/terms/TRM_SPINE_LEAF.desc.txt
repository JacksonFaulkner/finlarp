Spine-leaf is the standard data-center network layout: every server rack connects to a leaf switch, and every leaf connects to every spine switch. Any two servers are then the same small number of hops apart, giving predictable latency and bandwidth. AI back-end clusters use two or three tiers of this design, and the number of tiers drives how many switches and optical links a cluster needs. Related: Arista (ANET), Cisco (CSCO), Broadcom (AVGO), Nvidia (NVDA), Coherent (COHR).
