# 1. What it is: a generation of manufacturing tech

  (each step: more transistors per mm2, lower power at same speed, higher wafer price)

# 2. Why AI cares

Datacenter GPUs and custom accelerators move to the newest node to fit more compute per watt. Node access is a constraint on supply.

# 3. Economics

Leading-edge wafers cost far more per unit than mature ones, and fab construction runs tens of billions of dollars.
