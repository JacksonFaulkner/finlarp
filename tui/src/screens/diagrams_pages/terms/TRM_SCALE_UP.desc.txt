Scale-up networking ties a group of accelerators together with very fast, memory-like links (NVLink, UALink) so they behave as one big machine, usually within a rack. Scale-out networking connects many such groups across the data center with Ethernet or InfiniBand at lower bandwidth per GPU. Companies report these as separate opportunities, and the scale-up market is newer and contested between proprietary and open standards. Related: Nvidia (NVDA), Broadcom (AVGO), Astera Labs (ALAB), Arista (ANET), Marvell (MRVL).
