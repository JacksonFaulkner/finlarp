Beamforming steers radio energy toward a receiver by adjusting the phase and amplitude of signals across an antenna array. It raises signal strength and reduces interference, which lets a base station serve more users on the same spectrum. Phased-array beamforming chips are central to mmWave phones and small cells. Related: Qualcomm (QCOM), Skyworks (SWKS), Qorvo (QRVO), Ericsson (ERIC).
