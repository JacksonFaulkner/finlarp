GPU-as-a-service is renting GPU compute by the hour or under multi-year contracts instead of buying hardware. Neoclouds such as CoreWeave and Nebius, plus the hyperscalers, buy GPUs, install them in data centers and lease capacity to AI labs and enterprises. The business is capital heavy and often debt funded, with returns depending on utilization, contract length and how long the GPUs stay useful. Related: CoreWeave (CRWV), Nebius (NBIS), Oracle (ORCL), Amazon (AMZN), NVIDIA (NVDA).
