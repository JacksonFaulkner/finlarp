# 1. What it is: the thin insulator electrons tunnel through

┌──────────────────────────────┐
│        FLOATING GATE         │  electrons stored here
├──────────────────────────────┤
│     tunnel oxide, ~8 nm      │  electrons pass by tunneling
├──────────────────────────────┤
│       silicon channel        │  source and drain sit beside it
└──────────────────────────────┘

(a voltage across the oxide pulls electrons through; the layer must stay intact or the cell wears out)

# 2. Why it matters

Tunnel oxide sets the write and erase voltage and the endurance of a flash cell. Each program and erase cycle stresses it, so its thickness and quality are central to flash yield.
