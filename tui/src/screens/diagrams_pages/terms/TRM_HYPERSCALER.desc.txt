A hyperscaler is a cloud provider that runs computing at very large scale across many owned data centers, mainly Amazon (AWS), Microsoft (Azure), Google (GCP) and Meta for its own use. Their combined capital spending, now several hundred billion dollars a year, is the largest source of demand for AI chips, networking and data center equipment. Supplier filings often name hyperscalers as top customers, so capex guidance from them moves the whole AI supply chain. Related: Amazon (AMZN), Microsoft (MSFT), Alphabet (GOOGL), Meta (META), Oracle (ORCL).
