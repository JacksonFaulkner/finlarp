# 1. What it is: chip that rewrites one byte at a time

┌────────────────────────┐
│ EEPROM                 │
│ electrically erasable  │
│ programmable read-only │
│ memory                 │
└────────────────────────┘

(keeps data with power off; erased by electrical charge, not UV light)

# 2. Why it matters

EEPROM holds settings, calibration data, serial numbers and small firmware parameters on boards and modules. Erase is per byte, so it is smaller and slower than NAND or NOR flash.
