Edge AI runs model inference on the device, such as a car, camera, PC, phone or robot, instead of in a data center. It keeps latency low, saves bandwidth and works offline, but it is limited by power, memory and thermal headroom on the chip. Chips for it are sized in TOPS per watt and often pair an NPU with a CPU and GPU. Related: Qualcomm (QCOM), Ambarella (AMBA), NXP Semiconductors (NXPI), Intel (INTC).
