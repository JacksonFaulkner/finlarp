Fine-tuning is further training of an already-trained model on a smaller, specific dataset to adapt it to a task or domain. It needs much less compute than pretraining. Enterprises and cloud providers use it to build customized models, and cloud platforms sell it as a service. It adds a middle tier of compute demand between training and inference. Related: Microsoft (MSFT), Amazon (AMZN), Alphabet (GOOGL), Snowflake (SNOW).
