A humanoid robot has a human-like body with two arms, two legs and a head with cameras, built to work in spaces designed for people. It combines actuators and reducers, batteries, cameras and an onboard AI model that maps sensing to motion. Most units are still pilot builds in factories and warehouses, and the cost of actuators and hands is the main barrier to volume.
