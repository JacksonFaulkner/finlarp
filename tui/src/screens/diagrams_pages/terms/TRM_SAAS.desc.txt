SaaS, or software as a service, is software a vendor hosts and runs for customers, sold by subscription and accessed over the internet. The vendor carries hosting, upgrades, and security, so customers pay for access rather than installing software. Revenue recurs each billing period while customers stay subscribed, which is why investors value these companies on recurring revenue. AI features add inference cost to a product whose main cost was hosting. Related: Salesforce (CRM), ServiceNow (NOW), Workday (WDAY), Snowflake (SNOW).
