# 1. What it is: annualized value of recurring contracts

┌────────────────────────────────────┐
│ ARR = MRR x 12                     │
│ MRR = monthly recurring revenue    │
│ contracted, excludes one-time fees │
└────────────────────────────────────┘

(run-rate: the current billing period, annualized, not revenue booked over the year)

# 2. Why AI cares

ARR annualizes the recurring fees a company bills today, so it works as a forward signal of the revenue base. Companies define it differently: some include usage fees, pilots, or contracts that have not started billing. Check the filing's definition before comparing ARR across companies.
