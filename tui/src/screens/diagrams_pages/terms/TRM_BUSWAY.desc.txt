Busway is a factory-built run of copper or aluminum bars inside a steel housing, used to carry high current through a building. Plug-in taps attach at set points along the run, so racks or PDUs can be fed without pulling new cable. In data centers it is a common alternative to conduit and cable for moving power from switchgear to rows of racks. Related: Eaton (ETN), Schneider Electric (SU.PA), Siemens (SIE.DE), nVent Electric (NVT).
