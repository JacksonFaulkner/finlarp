Low earth orbit satellites fly roughly 300 to 1,200 km up, so they cut latency to tens of milliseconds compared with geostationary links at about 600 ms round trip. A broadband service needs a constellation of hundreds or thousands of satellites, which means frequent launches and inter-satellite links. Operators use LEO for consumer internet, backhaul for mobile networks and government links. Related: AST SpaceMobile (ASTS), EchoStar (SATS), Viasat (VSAT), SpaceX (private).
