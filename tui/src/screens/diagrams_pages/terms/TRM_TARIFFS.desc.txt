Tariffs are import taxes charged at the border, paid by the importer. Recent US tariffs under Section 301 (China) and Section 232 (national security, including semiconductor investigations) affect servers, components and equipment. They raise input costs, push companies to move assembly to Mexico, Taiwan or Southeast Asia, and can pull orders forward ahead of deadlines. Filings discuss them under risk factors and gross margin. Related: Dell (DELL), Super Micro (SMCI), Apple (AAPL), Applied Materials (AMAT), Celestica (CLS).
