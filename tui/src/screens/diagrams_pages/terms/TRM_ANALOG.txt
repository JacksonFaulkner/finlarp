# 1. What it is: chips for real-world, continuous signals

   ┌────────────┐    ┌──────────────┐    ┌───────────┐    ┌──────────────┐
   │ sensor     │    │ amp + ADC    │    │ digital   │    │ DAC + driver │
   │ real world │───▶│ analog chips │───▶│ CPU / MCU │───▶│ analog chips │
   └────────────┘    └──────────────┘    └───────────┘    └──────────────┘

  (analog sits at both edges of every digital system)

# 2. Why it matters

Analog parts sell for years with little price pressure, so gross margins run 60%+ at leaders. Inventory cycles in industrial and auto drive the swings.

# 3. AI angle

Data center power management and high-speed interface chips are the main AI-linked analog lines.
