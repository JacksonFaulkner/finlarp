# 1. What it is: licensed radio frequency bands

   ┌──────────────┬──────────────┬──────────────┐
   │ LOW BAND     │ MID BAND     │ mmWAVE       │
   │ < 1 GHz      │ 1-7 GHz      │ 24-100 GHz   │
   │ coverage     │ capacity mix │ peak speed   │
   └──────────────┴──────────────┴──────────────┘

  (licensed via FCC auctions; the cash paid is a balance-sheet asset)

# 2. Why it matters

Spectrum is the input that caps what a carrier can sell. Auction spend and license holdings show up in the carrier's capex and assets.
