# 1. What it is: a tax on goods crossing the border

   ┌───────────────┐    ┌───────────────┐    ┌─────────────────┐
   │ FOREIGN MAKER │───▶│ US CUSTOMS    │───▶│ US BUYER        │
   │ server / chip │    │ tariff % paid │    │ higher cost     │
   │               │    │ by importer   │    │ or lower margin │
   └───────────────┘    └───────────────┘    └─────────────────┘

  (exemptions and country-of-origin rules decide who actually pays)

# 2. Business effect

Companies either pass tariffs on in price or absorb them in margin. Watch for pull-ahead demand before a tariff date and a dip after.
