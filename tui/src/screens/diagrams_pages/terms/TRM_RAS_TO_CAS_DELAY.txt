# 1. What it is: the wait between ACT and READ or WRITE

   ACT row 42           READ col 7          data
   ───────┬─────────────────┬────────────────────▶
          │◀── tRCD ───────▶│
          │  row sensed     │  column can be read
          │  into row buf   │

  (tRCD is the ACT-to-READ or ACT-to-WRITE gap in the same bank)

# 2. Why AI cares

tRCD adds to the time before the first byte of a new row can move. Controllers hide it by keeping rows open and pipelining requests across banks.
