A DRAM subarray is a block of memory cells made of rows and columns, with its own sense amplifiers. A bank is built from many subarrays, and the way they are wired controls how fast memory can serve several requests at once. This matters to AI infrastructure because accelerators pull huge volumes of weights and activations from memory, so bank-level parallelism drives effective bandwidth.
