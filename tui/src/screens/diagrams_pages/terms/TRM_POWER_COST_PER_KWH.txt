# 1. What it is: all-in cost of one kilowatt-hour

   ┌────────────┐    ┌────────────┐    ┌────────────┐    ┌────────────┐
   │ energy     │───▶│ transmis.  │───▶│ capacity / │───▶│ all-in     │
   │ $/kWh      │    │ $/kWh      │    │ demand fees│    │ $/kWh      │
   └────────────┘    └────────────┘    └────────────┘    └────────────┘

  (a site's margin = BTC revenue per kWh minus this number)

# 2. Why it matters

Miners compare their all-in $/kWh against the revenue per kWh they earn from mining or hosting. Lower power cost is what makes a site competitive after a halving.
