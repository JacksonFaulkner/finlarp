CMMC, the Cybersecurity Maturity Model Certification, is the Department of Defense program that requires defense contractors to meet set cybersecurity levels before they win or keep certain contracts. It has three levels, and the higher levels usually need a third-party assessment. For AI and semiconductor vendors it matters because any supplier that handles controlled unclassified information for the DoD must certify, or lose eligibility for that work. Related: Palantir (PLTR), Leidos (LDOS), CrowdStrike (CRWD), Booz Allen Hamilton (BAH).
