Book-to-bill is new orders (bookings) divided by units shipped and invoiced (billings) over the same period. A ratio above 1.0 means orders are running ahead of shipments and the backlog is growing, while a ratio below 1.0 means the backlog is shrinking. Semiconductor equipment and chip makers have long reported it as a leading read on demand. Related: Applied Materials (AMAT), ASML (ASML), Lam Research (LRCX), Teradyne (TER).
