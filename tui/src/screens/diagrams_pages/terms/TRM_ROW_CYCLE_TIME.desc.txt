Row cycle time, written tRC, is the minimum time between two ACTIVATE commands to the same DRAM bank. It equals the row-active time plus the precharge time, and it caps how fast one bank can serve different rows. Memory systems hide it by spreading accesses across many banks, which is why bank count matters for AI memory throughput. Related: Micron (MU), SK hynix (000660.KS), Rambus (RMBS).
