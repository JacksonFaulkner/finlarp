Quantum error correction encodes one logical qubit across many physical qubits, then uses ancilla measurements to detect and correct errors without reading the encoded data. The surface code is the leading scheme because it needs only nearest-neighbour connections. Error correction only helps when physical error rates sit below a threshold, and the overhead of hundreds to thousands of physical qubits per logical qubit is the main cost of fault-tolerant machines.
