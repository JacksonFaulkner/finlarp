# 1. What it is: stacking chiplets on a base die

  ┌──────────────────────────────┐
  │ chiplets (compute, cache)    │
  └───────────────┬──────────────┘
                  ▼
  ┌──────────────────────────────┐
  │ microbumps or Cu-Cu bonds    │
  └───────────────┬──────────────┘
                  ▼
  ┌──────────────────────────────┐
  │ base die (I/O, power, cache) │
  └───────────────┬──────────────┘
                  ▼
  ┌──────────────────────────────┐
  │ package substrate            │
  └───────────────┬──────────────┘

# 2. Why AI cares

Stacking puts logic next to memory and links over very short wires, which cuts power per bit moved. Yield and thermal limits on stacked dies are the main hurdles.

# 3. Versus EMIB

EMIB embeds a small silicon bridge in the substrate to join dies side by side. Foveros stacks dies vertically on a base die.
