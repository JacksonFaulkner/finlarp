A systolic array is a grid of multiply-accumulate cells where data flows from cell to neighbor each clock, so each value is reused many times without going back to memory. It is the core of Google TPUs and many other AI accelerators. The design gives high math throughput per watt for matrix multiplies but is less flexible than general cores. Related: Alphabet (GOOGL), Amazon (AMZN), Broadcom (AVGO), NVIDIA (NVDA).
