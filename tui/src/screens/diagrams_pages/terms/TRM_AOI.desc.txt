Automated optical inspection uses cameras and image software to check circuit boards after printing, placement and reflow, looking for missing parts, misplaced parts and bad solder joints. It catches defects early, before a board reaches functional test, which lowers rework cost. Dense boards for AI servers leave little margin for error, so AOI and X-ray checks matter for yield. Related: Jabil (JBL), Flex (FLEX), Celestica (CLS).
