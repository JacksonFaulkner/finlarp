An HPC colocation lease is a multi-year agreement in which a tenant rents powered, cooled data center space for its own high-performance computing or AI hardware. The tenant usually pays a monthly rate per kW of critical IT load, plus a pass-through or fixed charge for power. Lease terms, escalators, and any power cost pass-through determine how much of the rent is locked in. Related: Core Scientific (CORZ), Applied Digital (APLD), Hut 8 (HUT), Cipher Mining (CIFR).
