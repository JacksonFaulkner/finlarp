DOCSIS is the standard that lets cable operators carry internet data over their hybrid fiber-coax plant using cable modems and a cable modem termination system at the headend. The current version, DOCSIS 4.0, adds full-duplex and extended spectrum to raise upstream speeds. Cable is a major US broadband provider, so DOCSIS capex and cable-modem and CMTS equipment purchases track its network upgrades. Related: Comcast (CMCSA), Charter Communications (CHTR), Harmonic (HLIT), Cisco (CSCO).
