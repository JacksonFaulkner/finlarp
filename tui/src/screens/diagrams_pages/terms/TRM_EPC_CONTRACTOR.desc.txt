An EPC contractor takes responsibility for the engineering, procurement and construction of a project, often on a fixed price or a guaranteed completion date. It buys long-lead equipment such as transformers, switchgear and chillers, and runs the site crews that install them. On data center builds the EPC firm often delivers the whole power and cooling block, so its schedule and cost overruns move the date capacity comes online. Related: Quanta Services (PWR), EMCOR Group (EME), Comfort Systems USA (FIX), Sterling Infrastructure (STRL).
