Yield is the share of chips on a wafer that work. It depends on defect density and die size: big AI dies lose more to each defect, so their yield is lower. Yield sets the real cost per good chip and is a common reason new nodes ramp slowly. Chipmakers often cite yield improvement as a driver of gross margin. Related: Taiwan Semiconductor (TSM), Intel (INTC), KLA (KLAC), NVIDIA (NVDA).
