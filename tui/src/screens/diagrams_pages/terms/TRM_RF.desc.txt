RF covers chips that send and receive radio signals: amplifiers, filters, switches and transceivers. The RF front-end sits between the antenna and the modem in phones, base stations and Wi-Fi gear. Revenue tracks handset cycles and band counts more than AI, though RF parts also go into satellite and defense links. Related: Qorvo (QRVO), Skyworks (SWKS), Broadcom (AVGO), Qualcomm (QCOM), Analog Devices (ADI).
