Retrieval-augmented generation, or RAG, supplies a language model with passages retrieved from an external document store at question time, so answers can draw on current or private material. The system splits documents into chunks, embeds them, searches an index for the closest matches, and places those matches in the prompt. Retrieval quality usually limits answer quality more than the model does. RAG is widely used in enterprise search, support, and research assistants. Related: Snowflake (SNOW), MongoDB (MDB), Elastic (ESTC), Palantir (PLTR).
