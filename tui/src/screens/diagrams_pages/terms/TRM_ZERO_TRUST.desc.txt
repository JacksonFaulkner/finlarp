Zero trust is a security model that drops the idea of a trusted internal network and checks identity, device health and policy on every request. Access is granted per application and per session with least privilege, instead of through a flat network connection. The model became standard after remote work and cloud apps removed the network perimeter, and it drives spending on identity, endpoint and access products. Related: Zscaler (ZS), Palo Alto Networks (PANW), CrowdStrike (CRWD), Okta (OKTA).
