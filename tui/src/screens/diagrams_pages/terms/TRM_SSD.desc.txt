An SSD (solid-state drive) is a storage device made of NAND flash chips managed by a controller, usually connected over NVMe/PCIe in servers. It replaced spinning disks for anything latency-sensitive because it has no moving parts and serves random reads hundreds of times faster. In AI clusters, SSDs sit in GPU servers as local scratch and in storage arrays feeding training jobs.
