# 1. What it is: the quantum bit

   classical bit:  0 or 1
   qubit:          a|0> + b|1>   (superposition until measured)

   ┌───────────────────┐
   │ qubit             │  measure ──▶ 0 or 1
   │ two-level system  │  (probability |a|^2 and |b|^2)
   └───────────────────┘

(N qubits hold 2^N amplitudes, but a readout returns only N bits)

# 2. Why it matters

Qubit count and error rate are the two numbers that decide whether a machine can run useful algorithms. Today's systems are noisy, so most of the near-term value is in research and access, not production AI.
