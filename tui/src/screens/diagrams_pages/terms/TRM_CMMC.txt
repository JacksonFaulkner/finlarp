  (CUI = controlled unclassified information)

# 2. Why AI cares

Chip, cloud, and AI vendors that handle controlled unclassified information for the Defense Department must meet CMMC to keep eligibility for that work. The phase-in began in late 2025, so certification cost and timing matter for contractors. A failed assessment can remove a vendor from a defense contract.
