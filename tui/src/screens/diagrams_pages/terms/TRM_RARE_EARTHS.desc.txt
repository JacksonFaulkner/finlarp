Rare earths are 17 metallic elements, including neodymium, praseodymium, and dysprosium, that are used in the permanent magnets of motors, actuators, and some drives. Neodymium-iron-boron (NdFeB) magnets are the strongest common type, and they are used in cooling fans, hard drives, and robot and vehicle motors. China handles most rare earth mining and nearly all separation and magnet making, which made the supply chain a target of its export controls. Magnet supply affects the cost and lead time of motors and actuators in data center and factory equipment. Related: MP Materials (MP), Lynas Rare Earths (LYC.AX), Neo Performance Materials (NEO.TO)
