# 1. What it is: the wait between PRE and the next ACT

   PRE                    ACT (new row)
   ───┬──────────────────────┬──────────────▶
      │◀──── tRP ───────────▶│
      │ bitlines settle      │ row can open
      │ back to idle         │

  (tRP applies within one bank; other banks can keep working meanwhile)

# 2. Why AI cares

tRP is the idle gap after closing a row. It is one part of the row-cycle cost that limits random access rates on memory-bound workloads.
