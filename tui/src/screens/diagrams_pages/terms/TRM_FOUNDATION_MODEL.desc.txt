A foundation model is a large model trained once on broad data, such as web text, code, and images, and then adapted to many tasks. Developers adapt it through prompting, retrieval, or fine-tuning rather than training from scratch. Frontier training runs cost tens to hundreds of millions of dollars, which concentrates development among a few well-funded labs. Once deployed, the main recurring cost is inference, which scales with usage. Related: Microsoft (MSFT), Alphabet (GOOGL), Meta Platforms (META), Amazon (AMZN).
