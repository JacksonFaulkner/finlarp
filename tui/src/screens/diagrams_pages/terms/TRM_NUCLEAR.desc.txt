Nuclear plants split uranium to make heat, steam and carbon-free power that runs at over 90% capacity factor. Small modular reactors (SMRs) are factory-built designs under ~300 MW meant to be cheaper and faster to deploy. Hyperscalers sign long contracts with existing plants and fund SMR startups to secure firm clean power. Related: Constellation Energy (CEG), Vistra (VST), Oklo (OKLO), NuScale (SMR), Cameco (CCJ).
