# 1. What it is: fission heat to steam to power

   ┌───────────┐   ┌──────────────┐   ┌─────────┐   ┌────────────┐
   │ URANIUM   │──▶│ REACTOR      │──▶│ STEAM   │──▶│ GRID / DC  │
   │ fuel rods │   │ fission heat │   │ turbine │   │ 24x7 power │
   └───────────┘   └──────────────┘   └─────────┘   └────────────┘

  (large plant ~1 GW; SMR ~50-300 MW per module)

# 2. Why AI cares

Data centers need power every hour of the year, and nuclear is the main carbon-free source that delivers that.

# 3. Timing

Restarts and uprates of existing plants come first; most SMRs are not expected online until the 2030s.
