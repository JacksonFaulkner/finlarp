# 1. What it is: customer picks your chip for its product

   ┌───────────────┐    ┌────────────────┐    ┌────────────────────┐
   │ DESIGN WIN    │───▶│ QUALIFICATION  │───▶│ VOLUME             │
   │ chip selected │    │ samples, tests │    │ production revenue │
   └───────────────┘    └────────────────┘    └────────────────────┘

  (lag from win to revenue is often 12-36 months)

# 2. Caveat

Design wins are not orders. Their value depends on how many units the end product actually ships.
