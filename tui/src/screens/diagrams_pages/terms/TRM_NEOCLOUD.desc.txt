A neocloud is a newer cloud provider focused on renting GPU capacity for AI training and inference, rather than a full range of cloud services. They buy Nvidia systems in bulk, often financed with debt backed by customer contracts, and lease power from data center developers. Large customers include AI labs and hyperscalers needing overflow capacity, which creates customer concentration. Related: CoreWeave (CRWV), Nebius (NBIS), IREN (IREN), Applied Digital (APLD), Nvidia (NVDA).
