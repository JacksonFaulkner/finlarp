# 1. What it is: profit left after the cost of the product

 ┌───────────────┐
 │ REVENUE       │  $100
 └───────┬───────┘
         │ minus
 ┌───────┴───────┐
 │ COST OF REV.  │  $60
 └───────┬───────┘
         │ equals
 ┌───────┴───────┐
 │ GROSS PROFIT  │  $40
 └───────┬───────┘
         │ divide by revenue
 ┌───────┴───────┐
 │ GROSS MARGIN  │  40%
 └───────────────┘

# 2. Why AI cares

Accelerator and server margins depend on mix (systems versus chips), on pricing when supply is tight, and on component costs. Gross margin shows up in every earnings release, so a drop is often the first sign of pricing pressure.
