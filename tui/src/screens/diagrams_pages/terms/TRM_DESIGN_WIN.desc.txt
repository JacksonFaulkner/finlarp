A design win is when a customer picks a supplier's chip or component for a product that is still being designed. Revenue usually follows one to three years later, once the product reaches volume production. Chip companies cite design wins to show future revenue before it appears in results. Losing a socket at the next product generation can remove a large revenue stream. Related: Marvell (MRVL), Broadcom (AVGO), Astera Labs (ALAB), Credo (CRDO), Lattice (LSCC).
