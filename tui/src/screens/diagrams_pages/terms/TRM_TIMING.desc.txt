Timing chips make and distribute the clock signals that keep digital circuits in step. They include crystal and MEMS oscillators, clock generators, buffers and jitter cleaners. Fast SerDes, PCIe and Ethernet links need very low-jitter clocks, so AI servers and switches carry more and better timing parts. Related: SiTime (SITM), Renesas (RNECY), Texas Instruments (TXN), Microchip (MCHP), Skyworks (SWKS).
