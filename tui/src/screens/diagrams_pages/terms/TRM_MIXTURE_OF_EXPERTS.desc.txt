Mixture of experts is a model design where a router sends each token to a few specialist sub-networks instead of the whole model. This cuts compute per token while keeping a large total parameter count. The trade-off is more memory to hold all experts and heavy GPU-to-GPU traffic. That shifts hardware needs toward memory capacity and high-bandwidth interconnect such as NVLink. Related: NVIDIA (NVDA), Micron (MU), Broadcom (AVGO).
