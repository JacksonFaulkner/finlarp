The CHIPS and Science Act is a 2022 US law that set aside about $39 billion in manufacturing grants plus a 25% investment tax credit for building chip fabs in the US. Grants are paid in milestones and come with conditions such as limits on expanding advanced capacity in China. Recipients include Intel, TSMC Arizona, Samsung, Micron and GlobalFoundries. It lowers the net cost of US fabs, which are otherwise more expensive than Asian ones. Related: Intel (INTC), Micron (MU), TSMC (TSM), GlobalFoundries (GFS), Texas Instruments (TXN).
