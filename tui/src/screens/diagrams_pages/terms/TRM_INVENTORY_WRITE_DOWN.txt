# 1. What it is: stock marked down to what it can sell for

 ┌───────────────┐
 │ INVENTORY     │  $500 at cost
 └───────┬───────┘
         │ compare with
 ┌───────┴───────┐
 │ MARKET VALUE  │  $380 net sale price
 └───────┬───────┘
         │ cost minus value, if lower
 ┌───────┴───────┐
 │ WRITE-DOWN    │  $120 charge to COGS
 └───────────────┘

# 2. Why it matters

The charge is non-cash in the quarter it is booked, but it lowers gross margin and reduces the carrying value of the stock. Repeated write-downs point to demand or product-timing problems, not one-off accounting.
