A chiller is a refrigeration machine that cools water for a building's cooling loop, rejecting the heat outdoors through condensers or cooling towers. Data centers use chilled water to feed air handlers and, increasingly, the facility side of liquid cooling loops. Liquid-cooled AI racks can run warmer water, which allows more free cooling and fewer chiller hours, but large campuses still buy chillers in volume. Related: Trane Technologies (TT), Carrier (CARR), Johnson Controls (JCI), Vertiv (VRT).
