A gas turbine burns natural gas to spin a generator; a combined-cycle plant adds a steam turbine that uses the exhaust heat. It is the fastest firm power a data center can add, either from the grid or built on site behind the meter. Turbine order books and slot reservations now run years out because of AI load. Related: GE Vernova (GEV), Siemens Energy (ENR), Mitsubishi Heavy (MHVYF), Caterpillar (CAT).
