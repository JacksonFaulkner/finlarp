Powered land is a site that already has an interconnection agreement, a substation, and enough utility capacity to deliver megawatts of power, so a data center can be built on it without waiting years for grid upgrades. It is scarce and valued by data center developers, which is why miners with existing power rights are selling or leasing it. Acreage without power rights is worth much less. Related: IREN (IREN), TeraWulf (WULF), Cipher Mining (CIFR), Core Scientific (CORZ).
