CAS latency, written CL or tCL, is the number of memory clock cycles between a READ command on an open row and the first data word on the bus. It is one of the main timing numbers on DRAM datasheets and in server memory specs. For AI infrastructure it matters less than bandwidth, since large tensor streams hide most of the wait.
