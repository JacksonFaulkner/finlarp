A reconfigurable optical add-drop multiplexer (ROADM) is a node in a DWDM network that can drop, add or pass through individual wavelengths under software control. Its core part is a wavelength selective switch (WSS), which steers each color to a chosen port using liquid crystal or MEMS optics. ROADMs let carriers and cloud providers reroute capacity without site visits. They are a major product line for optical system and component suppliers.
