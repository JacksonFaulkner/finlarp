# 1. What it is: FERC

   ┌───────────────────┐       ┌────────────────────────┐
   │ FERC              │       │ RTO / ISO              │
   │ federal regulator │──────▶│ PJM, MISO, SPP         │
   │ approves tariffs  │       │ runs wholesale markets │
   └───────────────────┘       └────────────────────────┘

  (FERC covers interstate power and gas transmission; states regulate retail rates and siting)

# 2. Why AI cares

Large data center campuses connect to transmission systems that FERC regulates, so interconnection and co-location rules help decide how quickly a site gets power. In 2025 the Department of Energy pushed FERC toward a rulemaking on large-load interconnection. Those rules feed directly into power prices and build timelines for AI capacity.
