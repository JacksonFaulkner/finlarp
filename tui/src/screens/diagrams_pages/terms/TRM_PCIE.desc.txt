PCIe (PCI Express) is the standard serial bus that connects a server's CPU to GPUs, NICs, SSDs and other add-in devices. Each generation doubles the per-lane rate: Gen5 is 32 GT/s, Gen6 64 GT/s, Gen7 128 GT/s, usually used as x16 links. In AI servers it is the host-side fabric that feeds accelerators and network cards, and its speed upgrades drive sales of switches and retimers.
