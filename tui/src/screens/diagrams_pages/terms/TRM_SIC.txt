# 1. What it is: a high-voltage power switch material

   ┌────────────────┐    ┌─────────────┐    ┌───────────────┐
   │ battery / grid │    │ SiC MOSFETs │    │ inverter      │
   │ 400-1500V      │───▶│ low loss    │───▶│ to motor/load │
   └────────────────┘    └─────────────┘    └───────────────┘

  (substrates are hard to grow; 150mm moving to 200mm wafers)

# 2. Why it matters

SiC raises EV range and inverter efficiency. For data centers it appears in solid-state transformers and 800V DC rack power.

# 3. Supply

Substrate crystal growth is the bottleneck and the cost driver; a few firms make most boules.
