# 2. Why AI cares

Hyperscalers buy the majority of data center GPUs and design custom chips (TPU, Trainium, Maia). Their spending plans drive supplier revenue.

# 3. Concentration

Many suppliers report one or two hyperscalers above 10% of revenue, a customer concentration risk noted in 10-Ks.
