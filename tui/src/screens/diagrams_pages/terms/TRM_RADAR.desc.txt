Automotive radar sends 77 GHz radio waves and reads the echoes for range, speed and angle of nearby objects. It works in fog, rain and darkness where cameras struggle, and it measures closing speed directly. Newer 4D imaging radar adds elevation and denser points, which narrows the gap to LiDAR at lower cost. Related: Arbe (ARBE), Ambarella (AMBA), NXP Semiconductors (NXPI), Infineon (IFNNY).
