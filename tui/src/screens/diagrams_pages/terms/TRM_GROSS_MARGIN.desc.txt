Gross margin is revenue minus cost of revenue, shown as a percent of revenue. For AI hardware it moves with product mix, pricing, and the cost of memory, packaging and other inputs, so a small change can shift operating profit a lot. Investors read a high and stable gross margin as pricing power, since it leaves more of each sales dollar to pay for R&D and SG&A. Related: NVIDIA (NVDA), Broadcom (AVGO), Super Micro Computer (SMCI), Dell Technologies (DELL).
