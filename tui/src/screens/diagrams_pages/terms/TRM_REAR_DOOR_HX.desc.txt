A rear-door heat exchanger is a water-cooled coil mounted on the back door of a rack that absorbs heat from server exhaust air before it reaches the room. It lets existing air-cooled servers run at higher rack density without full direct-to-chip plumbing. It is a common bridge for retrofitting older halls to host 40-70 kW AI racks. Related: Vertiv (VRT), nVent (NVT), Schneider Electric, Motivair.
