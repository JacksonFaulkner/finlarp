Gallium nitride is a wide-bandgap semiconductor used for power transistors and RF amplifiers. GaN switches faster with lower losses than silicon, so power supplies get smaller and more efficient. In AI data centers it shows up in high-density server power supplies and 48V-to-point-of-load converters. Related: Navitas (NVTS), Infineon (IFNNY), Texas Instruments (TXN), Qorvo (QRVO), Wolfspeed (WOLF).
