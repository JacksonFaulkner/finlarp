The NRC, or Nuclear Regulatory Commission, is the US agency that licenses and inspects commercial nuclear reactors and other nuclear facilities. New reactors need a construction permit and an operating license from it, and restarts of closed plants need its approval too. For AI infrastructure it matters because nuclear is one of the few firm, carbon-free power sources a campus can contract for, and NRC timelines set when that power arrives. Related: Constellation Energy (CEG), Oklo (OKLO), NuScale Power (SMR), Centrus Energy (LEU).
