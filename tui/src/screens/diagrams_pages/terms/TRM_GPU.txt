# 1. What it is: many small cores doing math in parallel

   ┌────────────┐   ┌──────────────┐   ┌──────────────┐
   │ HOST CPU   │   │ GPU          │   │ HBM          │
   │ sends work │──▶│ ~100+ SMs    │──▶│ stacked DRAM │
   └────────────┘   │ tensor cores │   │ TB/s         │
                    └──────────────┘   └──────────────┘

  (one CPU feeds 4-8 GPUs in a typical AI server)

# 2. Why AI cares

Training and inference are dominated by matrix math that parallelizes well. GPUs plus their software stack (CUDA, ROCm) are the default platform for that work.

# 3. Business angle

Data center GPU revenue is the largest single line in AI hardware spend. Lead times, HBM supply and CoWoS packaging capacity limit how many ship.
