# 1. What it is: the gate that switches a cell

┌────────────────────────────────────────┐
│        CONTROL GATE (word line)        │  gate voltage switches the cell
├────────────────────────────────────────┤
│          interpoly dielectric          │  blocks leakage off the gate
├────────────────────────────────────────┤
│             FLOATING GATE              │  trapped electrons = stored bit
├────────────────────────────────────────┤
│          tunnel oxide (thin)           │  electrons tunnel through on write/erase
├────────────────────────────────────────┤
│    n+ source   p channel   n+ drain    │  silicon substrate
└────────────────────────────────────────┘

(in NAND and NOR, control gates across a row form a word line)

# 2. Why it matters

Its voltage, together with the drain bias, decides whether the cell reads, programs or erases. Gate spacing and stack height are central process limits in 3D flash.
