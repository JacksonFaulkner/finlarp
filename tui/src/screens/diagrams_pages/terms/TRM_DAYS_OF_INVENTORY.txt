# 1. What it is: how many days of cost are on the shelf

 ┌───────────────┐
 │ INVENTORY     │  $5.0B ending balance
 └───────┬───────┘
         │ divided by
 ┌───────┴───────┐
 │ COGS PER DAY  │  $8.0B / 365 = $22M
 └───────┬───────┘
         │ result
 ┌───────┴───────┐
 │ DAYS OF INV.  │  $5.0B / $22M = ~228
 └───────────────┘

# 2. Why it matters

The number depends on the period used. Compare the same quarter year over year, since seasonal builds and ramps distort a single quarter.
