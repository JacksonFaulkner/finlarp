A memory mat is a small rectangular array of DRAM cells inside a subarray, with its own local sense amplifiers and row drivers. Subarrays are assembled from several mats, and the mat size trades off latency, area and power. Mat design is a major reason HBM and DDR5 differ in energy per bit, which matters when AI servers are power limited. Related: Micron (MU), SK hynix (000660.KS), Samsung (005930.KS).
