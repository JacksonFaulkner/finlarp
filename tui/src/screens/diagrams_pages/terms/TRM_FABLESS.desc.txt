A fabless company designs chips but outsources manufacturing to foundries and packaging houses. NVIDIA, AMD, Broadcom, Qualcomm and Marvell are fabless. The model keeps capex low and margins high but makes the company depend on foundry capacity and pricing. Filings often name TSMC as a sole or key supplier risk. Related: NVIDIA (NVDA), AMD (AMD), Broadcom (AVGO), Marvell (MRVL), Qualcomm (QCOM).
