# 1. What it is: shares sold a little at a time

 ┌───────────────┐
 │ BOARD         │  authorizes up to $2.0B
 │ AUTHORIZATION │
 └───────┬───────┘
         │ company sells through a broker
 ┌───────┴───────┐
 │ NEW SHARES    │  sold into the open market
 └───────┬───────┘
         │ cash in, share count up
 ┌───────┴───────┐
 │ DILUTION      │  existing holders own less
 └───────────────┘

# 2. Why it matters

ATM sales are disclosed in the prospectus supplement and the quarterly filings, with the shares sold and cash raised. They are a steady funding source, so they show up as a growing share count rather than a single equity event.
