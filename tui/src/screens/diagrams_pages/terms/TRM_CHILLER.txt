# 1. What it is: makes the cold water

   ┌──────────────┐     ┌───────────────┐     ┌──────────────┐
   │ CHILLER      │     │ CHILLED WATER │     │ IT LOAD      │
   │ compressor + │ ──▶ │ to CRAHs or   │ ──▶ │ heat back to │
   │ refrigerant  │     │ CDUs          │     │ chiller      │
   └──────────────┘     └───────────────┘     └──────────────┘

  (heat leaves via cooling towers or dry coolers; free cooling skips the compressor)

# 2. Why AI cares

Cooling is the largest non-IT power draw in a data center. Chiller orders are a direct read on data center construction for HVAC suppliers.
