A SmartNIC is a network interface card with its own processing (ASIC, FPGA or cores) that handles packet work in hardware. Basic NICs just send and receive; SmartNICs add functions such as RDMA, congestion control, encryption and virtual switching. AI back-end networks use high-speed NICs, one per GPU, so NIC count scales with accelerator count. Related: NVIDIA (NVDA), Broadcom (AVGO), Advanced Micro Devices (AMD), Intel (INTC), Marvell Technology (MRVL).
