# 1. What it is: woven glass that stiffens the board

   ┌──────────────────┐
   │ glass yarn       │  E-glass or low-Dk T-glass
   └────────┬─────────┘
            ▼
   ┌──────────────────┐
   │ woven cloth      │  sets stiffness and thermal size
   └────────┬─────────┘
            ▼
   ┌──────────────────┐
   │ resin-impregnated│  becomes CCL or substrate core
   │ prepreg          │
   └──────────────────┘

# 2. Why AI cares

The specialty glass grades that AI boards need are made by few suppliers. A shortage there can cap substrate and laminate output for accelerators.
