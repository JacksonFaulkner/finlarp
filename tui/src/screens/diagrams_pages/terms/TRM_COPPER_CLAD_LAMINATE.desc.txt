Copper clad laminate (CCL) is a resin-and-glass-cloth board with copper foil bonded to one or both sides, and it is the base material that printed circuit boards are etched from. High-speed AI server boards need low-loss CCL with a low dielectric constant, which is harder to make than standard FR-4. Suppliers differ in resin chemistry and glass-cloth grade, and qualified low-loss grades are a limited pool. Demand for CCL rises with the number of AI accelerators, switches, and backplanes built per rack. Related: Rogers (ROG), Taiwan Union Technology (6274.TW), Shengyi Technology (600183.SS)
