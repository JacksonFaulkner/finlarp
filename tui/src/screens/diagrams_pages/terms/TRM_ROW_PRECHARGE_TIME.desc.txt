Row precharge time, written tRP, is the minimum delay between a PRECHARGE command and the next ACTIVATE to the same bank. It is one of the core DRAM timing values, and together with tRCD and CAS latency it sets the cost of switching rows. For AI systems, these delays limit random access rates, so controllers schedule around them. Related: Micron (MU), SK hynix (000660.KS), Rambus (RMBS).
