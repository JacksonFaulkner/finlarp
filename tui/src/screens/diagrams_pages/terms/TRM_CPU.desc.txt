A CPU is the general-purpose processor that runs the operating system and most ordinary software, with a few dozen to a few hundred powerful cores. In AI servers the CPU schedules work, moves data and feeds the accelerators, and it still handles most non-AI cloud workloads. The market splits between x86 (Intel, AMD) and Arm-based designs such as NVIDIA Grace, AWS Graviton and Google Axion. Related: Intel (INTC), Advanced Micro Devices (AMD), Arm Holdings (ARM), NVIDIA (NVDA), Amazon (AMZN).
