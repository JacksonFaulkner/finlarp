A DPU is a programmable chip on a network card that offloads networking, storage and security work from the server CPU. It typically combines fast Ethernet ports, Arm cores and hardware engines for encryption and packet processing. In AI clusters DPUs isolate tenants and move data to GPUs without burdening the host. Related: NVIDIA (NVDA), Marvell Technology (MRVL), Intel (INTC), Advanced Micro Devices (AMD), Broadcom (AVGO).
