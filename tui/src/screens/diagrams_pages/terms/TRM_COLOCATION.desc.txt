Colocation is renting space, power and cooling in a third-party data center while the customer owns and runs its own servers. Retail colo sells cages and cabinets with interconnection; wholesale colo leases whole halls or buildings, often to hyperscalers. Revenue is recurring and priced largely per kW of power, so AI demand shows up as larger, denser leases. Related: Equinix (EQIX), Digital Realty (DLR), Iron Mountain (IRM), Applied Digital (APLD).
