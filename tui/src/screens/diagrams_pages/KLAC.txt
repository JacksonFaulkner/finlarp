# 1. What happens: measuring every layer so the next one lands right

 ┌───────────┐   ┌───────────────┐   ┌────────────────┐   ┌────────────────┐   ┌────────────┐
 │ WAFER     │──▶│ INSPECT       │──▶│ MEASURE        │──▶│ ANALYSE        │──▶│ FEED BACK  │
 │ post-etch │   │ find defects  │   │ overlay, CD,   │   │ bin + classify │   │ to scanner │
 │           │   │ light, e-beam │   │ film thickness │   │ excursion?     │   │ next layer │
 └───────────┘   └───────────────┘   └────────────────┘   └────────────────┘   └────────────┘

  (KLA sells the inspection, metrology and data-analytics tools; it makes no chips, it tells fabs when
      a layer is wrong)

# 3. Three tool families and what they see

  optical inspection   broadband plasma light scans the whole wafer, fast; finds killer defects on
      patterned wafer
  e-beam inspection    scanning electron beam, much slower, sees tiny voids/opens that light cannot
      resolve
  overlay metrology    measures how well layer N+1 aligns to layer N (Archer family)
  CD / film metrology  line width and film thickness (KLA SpectraShape etc.; CD-SEM is Hitachi/AMAT turf)
  unpatterned wafer    bare/blanket wafers incoming and after CMP (Surfscan; KLA and Hitachi High-Tech
      lead)
  reticle inspection   checks the mask itself before it prints errors onto every wafer (Lasertec EUV
      blanks)

  Why feedback matters: an overlay miss of a few nm between two layers can open or short a via
      (textbook knowledge).
  The data path: tool measurements ──▶ fab host ──▶ run-to-run control ──▶ scanner/etch recipe offsets,
      lot by lot.
  Defect review ladder: optical finds candidates, review SEM images them, classifier sorts killer vs
      nuisance.
  Physics in one line: each tool trades speed against sensitivity. Optical covers 100% of the wafer
      area in
  minutes; e-beam resolves smaller defects but samples a few dies. Fabs combine them (textbook
      knowledge).

# 8. Who makes it, market structure, chokepoints

  KLA (KLAC)             the near-monopoly in optical wafer and mask-adjacent inspection and overlay
  Applied Materials (AMAT)  e-beam review + CD-SEM, with Hitachi High-Tech (Hitachi parent, 6501.T)
  Lasertec (6920.T)      EUV mask-blank and actinic mask inspection
  Onto Innovation (ONTO) packaging + 3D metrology and inspection
  Nova (NVMI)            optical CD/film metrology; ASML (ASML) sells overlay-adjacent YieldStar
      metrology

  Risks: China export controls hit KLA sales (Q2 2025 call cited export challenges); Specialty Semi
      segment fell 1%
  on lower China investment. Dependence on leading-edge capex: process control rises faster than WFE
      when nodes
  shrink because each new layer needs more measurement. 'Next layer feedback' lock-in makes switching
      costly.
