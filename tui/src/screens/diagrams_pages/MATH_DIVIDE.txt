# 1. Share 12 dots into 4 equal groups

   ●●●  ●●●  ●●●  ●●●        4 groups of 3 = 12

   12 / 4 = 3: each group gets 3

# 2. Undo multiplication

   4 x 3 = 12, so 12 / 4 = 3

  (division asks: how many 4s fit into 12? Answer: 3)

# 3. Repeated subtraction: take 4 away until none is left

   12 - 4 = 8     (1 group taken)
    8 - 4 = 4     (2 groups taken)
    4 - 4 = 0     (3 groups taken)

   3 groups were taken, so 12 / 4 = 3.
   Dividing by 0 has no answer: taking 0 away never gets you to 0.
