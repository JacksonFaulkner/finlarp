# 3. Procedure and physics

  1 interposer wafer   TSVs etched, Cu-filled; RDL on front; wafer thinned to expose TSVs.
                       Interposer is passive silicon: wires only, no transistors (CoWoS-S).
  2 chip-on-wafer      known-good GPU and HBM dies placed face-down, microbumps reflowed (CoW).
  3 underfill + mold   epoxy flows between dies (capillary), then compression mold fixes the whole stack.
  4 wafer-on-substrate CoW thinned, C4-bumped, diced, flipped onto organic substrate.
  5 second underfill   C4 joints get their own underfill; lid + TIM fitted; BGA balls attached.
  Why Si interposer: only silicon can route thousands of fine wires between GPU and HBM at um pitch.
  Why the name matters: Chip on Wafer on Substrate = steps 2 then 4 (TSMC expansion, textbook).

  Thermal stress: Si ~3 ppm/C vs organic substrate ~15-20 ppm/C (textbook) so a big package warps on
  every heat cycle; underfill, stiffener ring and lid keep bumps from cracking.

  Inside the HBM stack (memory makers, not TSMC):
    DRAM dies thinned to tens of um, each pierced by TSVs, stacked and bonded, base die at the bottom
    HBM3E 12-high = 12 DRAM dies; JEDEC raised stack height limit 720 to 775 um (Tom's)
    microbump pitch: historically ~40 um, trending to ~10 um; volume standard cited ~25 um
    bonding: TC-NCF or MR-MUF (SK hynix); hybrid bonding validated at 12-high, not yet volume
    hybrid bonding ~6 um pitch now, 4.5 and 3 um coming; TSMC wants ~5 um class bumps by 2H 2028

# 4. By the numbers

  interposer limit      3.3x reticle, ~2,700 mm2 (CoWoS-S, TSMC); 6x reticle is a development target
  B200 package          2 dies, 208B transistors, 192 GB HBM3E, ~8 TB/s memory, 10 TB/s die-to-die
  HBM per stack         1024-bit bus, ~1 TB/s (Tom's Hardware launch report)
  CoWoS-R RDL pitch     4 um minimum stated (secondary source)
  OSAT top-10 revenue   $41.56 billion in 2024 (+3% YoY, TrendForce)

  supply gap            about 20% now, may narrow to about 10% by end-2026 (TrendForce, June 2026)

# 7. Who makes it, market structure, chokepoints

  CoWoS (the advanced part)  TSM is the main CoWoS source (textbook); ASE (ASX),
                             Amkor (AMKR) take overflow oS work. SPIL is part of ASE.
  HBM                        SK hynix (000660.KS), Samsung (005930.KS), Micron (MU); see PICK for shares.
  Substrates (ABF)           Ibiden (4062.T), Unimicron (3037.TW) (textbook, no share sourced)
  Mold / underfill           epoxy-compound makers, mostly Japanese (textbook)
  Risks                      one company's packaging lines gate Nvidia + AMD + custom ASIC output
                             warpage and yield loss rise with interposer area; ABF substrate supply
                             Taiwan concentration; ~20% supply gap (TrendForce) means allocation rules
  Competition                panel-level packaging, glass cores (watch items, not sourced)
