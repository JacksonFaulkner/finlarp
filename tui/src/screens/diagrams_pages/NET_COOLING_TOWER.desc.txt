A cooling tower is where the heat finally leaves (HEAT OUT): warm condenser water is sprayed over fill while a fan pulls air through, about 1% evaporates and cools the rest by roughly 6 C. It is the cheapest way to reject heat, but 100 MW of heat evaporates about 3.6 million litres of water a day. Tickers: SPXC, JCI, TT, MOD, VRT.
