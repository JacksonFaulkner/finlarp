# 1. What happens: the organic substrate fans fine pitch out to board pitch

Under the interposer sits the package substrate, a laminate of copper wiring layers built up on a core. It takes the C4 bumps at about 150 um pitch and fans them out to the BGA balls that solder to the server board at about 1 mm. The insulating layers are ABF FILM; the stack of BUILD-UP LAYERS on each side of a CORE carries power and signals; AI packages need 12 to 20 of them and very large areas, and WARPAGE is a constant fight.

# 2. The parts

ABF FILM           Ajinomoto Build-up Film, the dielectric between substrate copper layers
BUILD-UP LAYERS    alternating ABF and copper, laminated layer by layer; 12-20 for AI GPUs
CORE               the stiff centre the build-up layers are laminated onto
WARPAGE            the large, thin laminate bends as it heats and cools
BGA                ball grid array: solder balls joining the substrate to the board
FAN-OUT            spreading fine-pitch connections out to coarse board pitch
GLASS CORE         a stiffer, flatter core material under development
SUBSTRATE MAKERS   Ibiden, Unimicron, Shinko, AT&S, Nan Ya and others

# 3. A substrate, in order

1. start from a core with plated through-holes
2. laminate a layer of ABF film
3. drill vias and plate copper wiring
4. repeat for each build-up layer, both sides
5. finish with solder resist, then the C4 pads on top and BGA pads below

# 4. ABF film: one supplier

ABF FILM is the build-up dielectric in almost every high-end CPU and GPU substrate; Ajinomoto's subsidiary is estimated to control about 95% of the CPU/GPU-grade market (analyst estimate). A single film supplier is a known chokepoint.

# 5. Layers and size

AI GPU substrates need 12 to 20 build-up layers with fine lines; H100's is estimated at 12+ layers in a package over 120 x 120 mm (research estimates). Multi-die layouts roughly double substrate area per package.

# 6. Warpage and glass cores

WARPAGE: a big, thin organic laminate bends during reflow and in use, stressing bumps and making assembly harder. A GLASS CORE is stiffer and flatter and could carry finer wiring; it is under development.

# 7. Fan-out to the board

FAN-OUT: the substrate spreads tens of thousands of fine bumps out to the BGA balls at board pitch, routing power planes and high-speed signals on the way.

# 8. Who makes it

SUBSTRATE MAKERS: one estimate puts high-end FC-BGA share at Ibiden about 35%, Shinko 18%, Unimicron 14%, AT&S 10%, Nan Ya 5%; another has Unimicron leading at about 22% of capacity (they measure different things; estimates). Ibiden has committed about JPY 500B (US$3.3B) to two fabs ramping from fiscal 2027. Ajinomoto supplies the film.

# 9. Chokepoints and risks

- One supplier for ABF film.
- Few makers can yield 14+ layer GPU-class substrates.
- Warpage grows with package size.
- Notes: share and layer figures are market-research estimates, not company filings.
