== BGA BALLS
# What it does
BGA balls are the solder balls on the package's underside, at about 1 mm pitch, that solder it to the board.
# Why it matters
They carry all of the package's power and signals; large packages warp, which stresses them.
# Source
Textbook; patent 8896118 context.

== MODULE
# What it does
A module is the board carrying the GPU package, its power stages, memory of its own if any, and connectors.
# Why it matters
Package growth makes modules bigger and denser.
# Source
Textbook.

== POWER DELIVERY
# What it does
Power delivery is the path from the board regulator through BGA, substrate, C4, interposer and microbumps to the transistors.
# Why it matters
Every stage drops voltage by I x R; at hundreds of amps small resistances matter.
# Source
See TOP METAL (Chip tab).

== VRM
# What it does
Voltage regulator modules on the board step supply down to under a volt, placed as close to the package as possible.
# Why it matters
Shorter paths mean less voltage drop and faster response to load changes.
# Source
Textbook.

== COLD PLATE
# What it does
A cold plate is a copper block with fins or channels pressed onto the package lid to carry heat away.
# Why it matters
The logic die's heat must cross the lid into it; its design sets how much power the package can take.
# Source
Textbook.

== LIQUID COOLING
# What it does
Liquid cooling pumps coolant through the cold plates instead of blowing air over heat sinks.
# Why it matters
At AI power densities air runs out; packages are expected to need thousands of watts.
# Source
Tom's Hardware, TSMC roadmap coverage.

== NVLINK
# What it does
NVLink is NVIDIA's GPU-to-GPU link; lanes run from the package through the board to switches.
# Why it matters
It lets many GPUs act as one large system.
# Source
Textbook (vendor technology).

== RACK
# What it does
A rack holds many modules with power shelves, network switches and cooling manifolds.
