== HBF spec
# What it does
8- and 16-high NAND stacks, up to 512 GB, bandwidth tiers from about 0.4 to 3.0 TB/s.
# Why it matters
Capacity per GPU at a bandwidth between flash and HBM; the spec is what lets other vendors build to it.
# Source
SanDisk and SK hynix, OCP spec (Aug 2026).

== UCIe link
# What it does
The HBF stack reportedly reaches 3 TB/s through a UCIe chiplet link to the host.
# Why it matters
A standard die-to-die link lets the stack sit on many accelerators, not one vendor's.
# Source
Guru3D (reported figure).

== Workstream and timing
# What it does
Google and Tenstorrent joined the standard; samples of first inference devices are expected in early 2027.
# Why it matters
Customer validation is the step that turns a spec into product demand.
# Source
TrendForce and TechRadar on the HBF standard.
