3D DRAM means building the memory cell vertically, so each bit takes less floor space and cells can be stacked. Samsung showed a 16 Gb chip at ISSCC 2026 that pairs vertical channel transistors (VCT) with wafer-to-wafer hybrid bonding, and press reports say it has a working 10a-node die with a 4F2 cell (mass production targeted for 2028). Stacked 3D DRAM is on Samsung's roadmap for the early 2030s. The main hurdle is the floating-body effect: charge builds up in the transistor body and changes how it behaves. Micron skips the 4F2 step, arguing its complexity outweighs the gain. SK hynix calls its version a vertical gate design. Related: Samsung (005930.KS), SK hynix (000660.KS), Micron (MU).
