HBF (high-bandwidth flash) is NAND stacked in an HBM-style package for AI inference. SanDisk's claims: a 16-die stack with 512 GB per stack, a CMOS logic die bonded directly under the NAND array (its CBA wafer bonding), a footprint, stack height and power close to HBM4, and 8-16x the capacity of HBM at a similar cost. In its own simulation, reading Llama 3.1 405B weights from HBF came within 2.2% of an unlimited-capacity HBM, but HBF has higher latency and larger page sizes. SanDisk and SK hynix signed an MOU to work on a shared spec and die requirements; SanDisk targets first HBF samples in H2 2026 and inference devices in early 2027. These are company figures, not measured results. Related: SanDisk (SNDK), SK hynix (000660.KS).
