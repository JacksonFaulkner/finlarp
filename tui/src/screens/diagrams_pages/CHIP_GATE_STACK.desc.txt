The gate stack is the thin sandwich over each channel: an interfacial oxide, hafnium oxide (HfO2) high-k dielectric and work-function metals (TiN, TiAlC) that replaced SiO2 and polysilicon. Intel introduced it at 45 nm in 2007 via gate-last (replacement gate). ALD tools from ASM International (ASM.AS), Applied Materials (AMAT) and Lam (LRCX) build it layer by layer for TSMC (TSM) and Intel (INTC).
