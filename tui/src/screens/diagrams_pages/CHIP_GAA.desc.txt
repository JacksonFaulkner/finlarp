Gate-all-around nanosheets wrap the gate around stacked silicon ribbons, grown as a Si/SiGe superlattice, then freed by a selective SiGe etch after inner spacers are formed. Samsung shipped it in 2022, TSMC N2 in late 2025, Intel 18A adds backside power. Forksheet and CFET come next. GAA lifts epi, ALD and selective etch demand: TSMC (TSM), Intel (INTC), Applied Materials (AMAT), Lam (LRCX), Tokyo Electron (8035.T), ASML (ASML).
