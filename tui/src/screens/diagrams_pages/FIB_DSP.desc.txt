The DSP is the chip in each optical module that turns the host's electrical lanes into clean symbols for the laser, and cleans up what the photodiode receives. It speaks PAM4 (four levels, two bits per symbol), equalizes away distortion, and adds error correction. It is also most of the module's power, which is why linear-drive optics (LPO) try to remove it. Tickers: MRVL, AVGO, CRDO.
