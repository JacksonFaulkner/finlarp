The CDU (coolant distribution unit) sits between the building's facility water and the servers' cold plates. Its plate heat exchanger passes heat from a small, sealed, filtered secondary loop into facility water, while N+1 pumps, a filter, a reservoir and a controller hold the supply temperature, pressure and flow. In-rack units serve one rack (~100-250 kW); in-row units feed a whole row (1-2 MW+). Tickers: VRT, SMCI, NVT, MOD.
