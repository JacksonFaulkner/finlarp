A cold plate is a copper block clamped onto the GPU with coolant running through microchannels cut into it: direct-to-chip liquid cooling. Heat crosses a thin thermal interface into the copper, the water carries it out through hoses and dripless quick disconnects to the rack manifolds, and leaves at about 45-60 C, warm enough to reject without a chiller. Tickers: VRT, NVT, SMCI, FLEX, MOD, Delta (2308.TW), AVC (3017.TW), Auras (3324.TW).
