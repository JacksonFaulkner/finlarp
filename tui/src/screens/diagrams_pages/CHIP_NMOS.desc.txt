An NMOS transistor is a voltage-controlled switch: a positive gate voltage inverts the p-type body into an electron channel between two heavily doped n+ source/drain regions. Arsenic, phosphorus and strained Si:P epitaxy set junctions and drive current; swing, DIBL and body bias set leakage. Modern GPUs hold 100-200 billion of them (B200: 208B). TSMC (TSM), Intel (INTC), NVIDIA (NVDA), AMD, Synopsys (SNPS) and Cadence (CDNS) matter.
