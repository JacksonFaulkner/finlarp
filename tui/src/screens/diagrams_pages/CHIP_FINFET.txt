# 1. What happens: stand the channel up as a fin and wrap the gate around it

A FinFET turns the flat channel on its side. Fins are patterned at a quarter of the litho pitch (SAQP), etched from the silicon (FIN FORM), then oxide fills between them and is etched back to expose their tops (FIN REVEAL). The high-k metal gate drapes over each fin, touching three sides (GATE WRAP), so it keeps control as the channel shrinks. Width now comes in whole fins (QUANTIZED W): a transistor is 1, 2 or 3 fins wide (FIN COUNT).

# 2. The fin under the gate

Cut through the gate, across the fin: the fin is silicon tens of nm tall (Hfin) and single-digit to about 10 nm wide (Wfin). High-k dielectric (HfO2-based) and the gate metal wrap its top and two sidewalls; the effective width per fin is 2 x Hfin + Wfin. The bottom is not gated, so a sub-fin leakage path remains, blocked by a punch-through-stop doping under the STI oxide. Planar had one gated face; the fin has three.

# 3. Node history: one FinFET family

INTEL 22: 2011 tri-gate, Ivy Bridge. TSMC 16: 16FF+ in 2015, against its 20 nm planar. TSMC N7: gate pitch 57 nm, fin pitch 30 nm (estimates). TSMC N5: gate pitch 51 nm, fin pitch 28 nm. TSMC N3: gate pitch 45 nm, with FinFlex. SAMSUNG: 14 nm FinFET, then the first GAA at 3 nm. The fin was stretched, not replaced, from 22 nm to N3E.

# 5. FinFlex: fins per block on N3

FINFLEX lets N3 designers pick fins per cell block on the same die: 3-2 (3-fin plus 2-fin cells, for speed), 2-2 (balanced) or 2-1 (low power, dense). Libraries are 1, 2 or 3 fins tall; all options use 2-fin cells as the base. Density ranges from 0.64x to 0.85x of N5's 2-2 cell area depending on option (TSMC claim).

# 7. What limits fins below 3 nm

FIN LIMITS: fins too thin raise variability, too tall bend and etch with tapered profiles. LEAKAGE: sub-fin leakage, GIDL and tunnelling grow. WAFER COST: N3 wafers cost more than N5 (TSMC prices are not public; only labelled rumour ranges exist). The industry's answer is nanosheets (GAA) at N2; Samsung already at 3 nm.

# 8. Who makes it

TSMC (TSM) for N16 through N3 (NVIDIA, Apple, AMD); Intel (INTC) from 22 nm to Intel 3; Samsung from 14 nm. Litho and etch tools for SAQP come from ASML, Applied Materials (AMAT), Lam Research (LRCX) and Tokyo Electron.

# 9. Chokepoints and risks

- Fins have run out of room below 3 nm; the move to GAA resets yields.
- Cost per wafer keeps rising with every FinFET node.
