# 1. What happens: print and fill one thin local wiring level (M1-M4)

The thin metal levels carry signals from cell to cell. Each is one loop: LITHO PITCH (EUV at 26-30 nm, or 193i multipatterning), TRENCH ETCH (cut the low-k dielectric through a hard mask), BARRIER+LINER (Ta/TaN with Co or Ru, 1-2 nm), FILL METAL (copper plated or reflowed, or Ru, Mo), CMP (polish flat, stopping on the dielectric), CAP + VIA (an etch-stop cap and the via to the next level). M1-M4 repeat it about four times, each at a pitch of about 20-40 nm.

# 2. Two thin levels in section: where the copper goes

COPPER LINE: only the core is copper, about 12-15 nm wide once the barrier is in. LOW-K ILD: SiOCH at k about 2.5-3 fills the spaces (spacing is half the pitch), with optional air gaps. BARRIER+LINER: TaN/Ta or Co/Ru, a fixed 1-2 nm skin on three sides of every line that doesn't scale down, so as width falls it takes a bigger share. Alternate levels run perpendicular; M1's pitch is tied to the standard-cell track pitch; tungsten contacts below land on the transistors.

# 3. Damascene versus subtractive

DAMASCENE: deposit dielectric, etch trenches, line and fill with copper, polish the excess. Copper forms no volatile compound and can't be dry-etched, so the dielectric is cut instead. SUBTRACTIVE: deposit a metal blanket, etch the lines, gap-fill dielectric, polish. Ru, Mo and W etch to volatile products, so they open a subtractive route, which also allows air gaps and no liner.

# 4. Metals for the thinnest lines

COPPER LINE: in every production node, but it needs a barrier and its resistivity rises from grain and surface scattering; a RuCo liner cuts the liner to about 2 nm and line resistance by up to 25% (Applied claim). RUTHENIUM: resistance less dependent on width; one analysis has it beating copper at 17 nm width and below. MOLYBDENUM: low bulk mean free path; imec has direct-etched Ru and Mo at 32 nm pitch and below; electromigration data are less clear. Cobalt, the traditional liner/fill, costs yield and resistance at 3 nm and below.

# 5. Self-aligned vias

A via placed by lithography overlay alone can miss: shift it and it shorts to a neighbour or hits the low-k wall. Overlay needs about a quarter of the via pitch, and the tightest via pitches fall below EUV resolution, so multiple masks raise cost. SELF-ALIGNED VIA: the hole is bounded by the metal line below, by selectively growing dielectric over the neighbours or using a metal-selective etch, so the via lands only on its own line.

# 6. Standard-cell routing: pin access

PIN ACCESS: cell pins sit on M1/M2 inside the cell, and the router must reach each from above. Shrinking from 7.5 to 6 to 5 tracks raises pin density and congestion and cuts the tracks covering each pin; blockages can leave pins unreachable. Libraries enforce a minimum number of accessible M1 tracks per pin, and designers sometimes hand-place M2 pins. Synopsys (SNPS) and Cadence (CDNS) place-and-route tools handle it.

# 7. Patterning limits (single exposure)

EUV 0.33 NA        practical single-exposure limit about 26 nm pitch (13 nm half-pitch)
High-NA 0.55       about 16 nm pitch single exposure reported; Intel uses it on selected 18A layers
Intel 18A M0       32 nm HD cells claimed; Panther Lake HP cells reported at 36 nm, copper (teardown-based)
TSMC N3E           minimum metal pitch 23 nm (IEDM 2022 via Wikipedia/WikiChip)
imec               28 nm pitch Ru lines by single-exposure 0.33 NA EUV

# 8. Who makes it

TSMC (TSM), Intel (INTC) and Samsung set pitch and metal choices. Applied Materials (AMAT) sells copper barrier/seed and Ru CVD tools; Lam Research (LRCX) and Tokyo Electron (8035.T) etch and plate; ASML (ASML) makes the EUV scanners. NVIDIA, AMD and Arm design cells that must route at these pitches; Synopsys and Cadence supply the routers.

# 9. Chokepoints and risks

- Ru and Mo cost, and CMP and etch maturity for them.
- RC delay of thin wires now dominates gate delay.
- Notes: higher levels get thicker and wider because RC falls with cross-section; N2/14A metal pitches are not public in the sources found.
