The leaf is the top-of-rack tier of an AI cluster: one 64-port 800G switch per rack row takes every server's NIC ports over short copper DAC or AOC, and sends the same bandwidth up over single-mode fibre to every spine. Flows are spread over the spines by ECMP; AI fabrics run 1:1, not oversubscribed, on RoCE / Ultra Ethernet or InfiniBand. Tickers: ANET, AVGO, NVDA, CSCO, CLS, CRDO, APH, COHR, LITE, FN.
