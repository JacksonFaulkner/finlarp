The array is the grid of DRAM cells: rows on word lines, columns on bit lines. An access opens a whole row into the sense amps (the row buffer), reads columns from there and closes the row. Open-row hits are three times faster than row conflicts, long bit lines weaken the signal so the array is tiled into mats and subarrays, and spare rows repair defects.
