PMOS carries current with holes, which move about a third as fast as electrons in silicon, so chipmakers squeeze the channel with boron-doped SiGe source and drain regions and size PMOS wider than NMOS. Paired with an NMOS in a CMOS inverter, it draws almost no static current. TSMC (TSM) and Intel (INTC) build it; Applied Materials (AMAT) epi tools, Synopsys (SNPS) and Cadence (CDNS) cell design support it.
