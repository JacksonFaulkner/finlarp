A FinFET stands the transistor channel up as a thin silicon fin and wraps the gate around three sides, so the gate keeps control as channels shrink. Intel shipped it at 22nm in 2012; TSMC runs FinFETs from 16nm through N3, where FinFlex lets designers pick 1, 2 or 3 fins per block. Width comes only in whole fins. Below 3nm fins give way to nanosheets. TSMC (TSM), Intel (INTC), Samsung, with Synopsys (SNPS) and Cadence (CDNS) tooling.
