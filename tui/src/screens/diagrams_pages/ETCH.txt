# 4. The chemistry and physics

  radicals   CF4 + e- ──▶ CF3 + F + e-             fluorine radicals made from fluorocarbon gas
  SiO2       SiO2 + 4F ──▶ SiF4(gas) + O2          slow without ion bombardment to break Si-O bonds
  Si         Si + 4F ──▶ SiF4  or  Si + 2Cl2 ──▶ SiCl4     (textbook: Cl2 / HBr for poly-Si and fins)
  3D NAND    HF gas etches the oxide-nitride stack at cold wafer temp; PF3 catalyst raises rate [2]
  ALE        cycle = self-limiting surface modification, then low-eV Ar+ removes only that layer [4]
             Cl2 + 50 eV Ar+ on Si: molecular-dynamics yield 0.172 Si per ion [4]
             C4F8/Ar+ on SiO2: 1.9 A per cycle [4]; GaN/AlGaN (not Si): 1.63 A per cycle

# 5. By the numbers

  pressure          5-100 mTorr (ICP-DRIE tool spec); RIE: few to few hundred mTorr [3]
  ICP source        100-700 W in one patent example; tool spec up to 2500 W at 2.4 MHz [3]
  plasma density    ~1e11 /cm3 for a high-density plasma [3]
  cryo wafer temp   0 to -30 C, helium back-side cooling [2]
  NAND channel hole ~10 um deep through a ~100 nm opening, <10 nm deviation (<0.1% of depth) [2]
  aspect ratio      approaching 70:1 today; 100:1 described as much harder [2]
  stack             tall two-tier stack about 30 um; word-line z-pitch about 40 nm [2]
  Lam Cryo 3.0      >2x etch rate vs conventional dielectric etch, -40% energy per wafer [1]
  installed base    nearly 1,000 Lam cryo chambers in NAND fabs, 5 million wafers (vendor claims) [1]

# 10. Key terms

HARD MASK: # Resist is too soft Litho exposes resist, but resist is too soft to survive a long, deep etch.

IGNITE: # From neutral gas to plasma A strong RF field accelerates the few free electrons present.

RADICALS: # The chemical half Radicals are neutral, highly reactive fragments made when electrons break gas molecules apart, for example CF4 + e- giving CF3 + F.

ION-ASSISTED: # Ions make it vertical Radicals sit on the surface but SiO2 etches slowly with fluorine alone, because Si-O bonds are strong.

SIDEWALL: # The protective film Sidewall passivation keeps trench walls from being eaten sideways.

STRIP: # Remove the helpers Strip and clean removes everything that was only there to enable the etch: leftover hard mask or resist (typically oxygen plasma ashing, textbook), the sidewall polymer, and residues containing halogens that would corrode metal if left.

SHOWERHEAD: # Even gas delivery The showerhead is a perforated top electrode (capacitive reactors) or a dielectric window beneath the coil (ICP) that distributes process gas evenly across the wafer.

PLASMA:: # Three species The plasma region holds three species.

ESC: # Holding and cooling the wafer The electrostatic chuck holds the wafer flat with an electrostatic force and lets helium flow behind it to carry heat between wafer and chuck.

cathode: # The bottom electrode The cathode is the bottom electrode beneath the chuck.

Lam Cryo 3.0: # What it is Lam Cryo 3.0 is Lam Research's third-generation cryogenic dielectric etch, aimed at the deepest 3D NAND channel holes.

NAND channel hole: # A very deep, very thin hole The NAND channel hole is a vertical hole punched through the whole stack of alternating oxide and nitride layers, later filled to form the string of memory cells.

aspect ratio: # Depth over width Aspect ratio is hole depth divided by hole width.

ALE: # Two self-limiting steps Atomic layer etching splits etching into two self-limiting half-steps: first a surface modification step (for example chlorine adsorbs on silicon), then a low-energy ion step (Ar+) that removes only the modified layer.

China: # Lam's biggest region China was Lam's largest region in FY2025 at 34% of its $18.44B revenue, ahead of Korea at 22% and Taiwan at 19%.

Etch+clean 22: # Share of wafer-fab equipment This row is Yole Group's estimate of etch plus clean as a share of wafer-fab equipment: 27% in 2022 and 19% in H1 2024, versus patterning (lithography) at 29% of WFE in 2024e and deposition at 25%.

Tokyo Electron: # Number two in etch Tokyo Electron (8035.T) is the number two etch player and the strongest challenger to Lam in memory.

Lam Research (LRCX): # The pure play Lam Research is the etch leader and the best pure-play on the segment.
