# 1. Pair up the slots, multiply each pair

   [ 1 ]     [ 4 ]
   [ 2 ]  ·  [ 5 ]
   [ 3 ]     [ 6 ]

   slot 1: 1 x 4 = 4
   slot 2: 2 x 5 = 10
   slot 3: 3 x 6 = 18

# 2. Then add the products

   4 + 10 + 18 = 32

   a · b = a1 b1 + a2 b2 + a3 b3

# 3. Why AI uses it: a neuron is a dot product

  A neuron multiplies each input by its weight, adds the results, then passes the total
  through a simple function. The weights are a vector, the inputs are a vector, and the raw
  score is their dot product.

  The dot product also measures alignment. It is large and positive when two vectors point
  the same way, and 0 when they are perpendicular: [1, 0] · [0, 1] = 0.
