== ABF FILM
# What it does
Ajinomoto Build-up Film is the insulating layer between the copper wiring layers of high-end CPU and GPU substrates.
# Why it matters
One company's subsidiary is estimated to control about 95% of CPU/GPU-grade ABF, a single-supplier chokepoint (analyst estimate).
# Source
wing.vc ABF bottleneck.

== BUILD-UP LAYERS
# What it does
Build-up layers alternate ABF film and copper wiring, laminated, drilled and plated one at a time on both sides of the core.
# Why it matters
AI GPU substrates need 12-20 of them with fine lines; few makers yield 14+ layers at volume (estimates).
# Source
marketsandmarkets GPU substrate (estimates).

== CORE
# What it does
The core is the stiff centre of the substrate, with plated through-holes, that the build-up layers are laminated onto.
# Why it matters
Its stiffness fights warpage; glass cores are being developed to do better.
# Source
Textbook.

== WARPAGE
# What it does
A large, thin organic laminate bends as it heats during reflow and cools, stressing the bumps above and the balls below.
# Why it matters
Warpage grows with package size and makes assembly and long-term reliability harder.
# Source
Textbook; see GLASS CORE.

== BGA
# What it does
A ball grid array of solder balls on the substrate's underside joins the package to the board at about 1 mm pitch.
# Why it matters
It is the package's connection to the outside world: power in, high-speed signals out.
# Source
Patent 8896118 context; textbook.

== FAN-OUT
# What it does
Fan-out is spreading tens of thousands of fine-pitch bumps out to the coarse pitch of the board, layer by layer.
# Why it matters
The substrate routes power planes and high-speed signals while doing it.
# Source
Textbook.

== GLASS CORE
# What it does
A glass core is a stiffer, flatter substrate core material that could carry finer wiring than organic cores.
# Why it matters
It could ease warpage in very large packages; it is still under development.
# Source
Industry development; not sourced here.
