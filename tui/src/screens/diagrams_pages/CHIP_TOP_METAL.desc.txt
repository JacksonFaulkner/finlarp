The top of the chip stack: thick copper layers (M10-M14) carry power, clock and long wires with low resistance, then pads, passivation, RDL and solder bumps connect the die to the package. IR drop and electromigration limit how thin these wires can get, and backside power (Intel PowerVia, TSMC A16) moves supply off the signal side. TSMC (TSM), Intel (INTC), Synopsys (SNPS) and Cadence (CDNS) matter most.
