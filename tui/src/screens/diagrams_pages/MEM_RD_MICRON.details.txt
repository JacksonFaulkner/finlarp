== HBM4 on 1-beta
# What it does
2048-bit interface per stack, built on Micron's 1-beta DRAM, with Micron's own base die.
# Why it matters
Micron is in the HBM4 volume ramp alongside SK hynix and Samsung.
# Source
TechRadar, Micron HBM4 and HBM4E.

== HBM4E custom base die
# What it does
Logic base die made by a foundry (TSMC), with customer-specific logic; DRAM moves to 1-gamma.
# Why it matters
Speed and power targets need more advanced CMOS than a DRAM process gives.
# Source
TrendForce, Micron custom HBM era (Aug 2026).

== vHBM
# What it does
DRAM stack placed directly on the processor rather than beside it, targeted for 2028.
# Why it matters
Removes the interposer shoreline limit on how many stacks fit around a GPU.
# Source
TrendForce and TechRadar on Micron's roadmap (roadmap, not a product).
