# 4. SDBG: stealth dicing before grinding, the order matters

  1  Stealth dicing: laser forms a modified section in the wafer
  2  Backside grinding thins the wafer; die separate at the modified section, which grinding removes
  3  DAF (die attach film) is expanded at low temperature, because cold DAF is brittle and splits cleanly
  4  Heat shrink removes tape sag so die move to die bonding without re-laminating the tape (DISCO DDS
      series)
  Why: grinding first then dicing leaves micro-cracks a few um deep; if thinned below that depth
      strength collapses

# 5. By the numbers

  Incoming 300 mm wafer thickness      725-775 um (SemiconductorX); roughly 750 um (Wikipedia)
  Backgrind target                     50-200 um; 150-200 um wire-bond/flip-chip; 75-100 um mobile;
      <=50 um 3D stacking
  Subsurface damage from grinding      a few um deep
  Blade street (sawlane)               50 to 80 um; stealth laser as narrow as about 15 um
  Saw kerf                             tens of um (no exact value in source)
  Mature-line dicing yield             well above 99% (SemiconductorX)
  Dies per wafer                       hundreds to tens of thousands
  Laser dicing adoption grows          at 7 nm and below, where small die make kerf loss matter
  DISCO DFL7363 stealth saw            about 50% productivity over its conventional model (vendor claim)
  DISCO laser saw shipments            passed 2,000 units by April 2020
