# 3. Specs and how flatness is measured

  Spec fields in a typical 300 mm p-type Cz vendor sheet (svmi.com SV029 / SV031):
    thickness 775 +/- 25 um     TTV 5 um or less     edge exclusion 3.0 mm
  TTV    total thickness variation across the wafer (max - min thickness)
  SFQR   site flatness: deviation within a small scanner-field-sized site (SEMI M1 / M67 family)
  ESFQR  same idea on the edge sites; flatness must hold on partial sites too, with tiny edge exclusion
  nano   short-wavelength surface height, reported per SEMI M43 (a separate guide from M1)
  area   pi * (150 mm)^2 = 70,686 mm^2  (my arithmetic: about 109.6 sq in per wafer face)
  (SEMI M1 numeric limits for SFQR / nanotopography are not public in what I found; buyers set them per
      node)

# 4. By the numbers

  2025 shipments    12,973 million square inches, up 5.8% (SEMI SMG)
  2025 revenue      $11.4 billion, down 1.2% (SEMI)
  2024 (derived)    about 12,262 MSI and about $11.5 billion, back-calculated from the % changes
  revenue per area  $11.4B / 12,973 MSI = about $0.88 per sq in  (my arithmetic, all diameters mixed)
  market size est.  $13.6 B in 2025 vs $11.5 B in 2024 in one market-research blog (differs from SEMI;
                    its basis is unclear, low confidence)
  300 mm share      67% of the polished-wafer market in 2023, top-5 85% (same blog, low confidence)
  demand drivers    AI: advanced epitaxial wafers for sub-3nm logic and polished wafers for HBM
  soft spots        mature nodes (auto, industrial, consumer) still waiting on price + demand

# 6. Market structure and tickers

  Five makers dominate: top five above 90% of the market (GEP 2018); the list is consistent since.
  Shin-Etsu Chemical (4063.T)  SUMCO (3436.T)  GlobalWafers (6488.TWO)  Siltronic (WAF.DE)
  SK Siltron (owned by SK Inc, 034730.KS)  Soitec (SOI.PA, SOI wafers).  (tickers are textbook, not
      sourced)
  Two Japanese firms held more than 60% of 300 mm epitaxial wafers in 2H 2020 (SEMI, via
      SemiEngineering).
  SUMCO said in 2021 that 300 mm logic supply was struggling to keep up and 200 mm was tight (auto).
  GlobalWafers' planned buy of Siltronic would have made it #2 with 26.7%; it did not complete
      (textbook).

# 7. Chokepoints and risks

  - five suppliers, mostly Japan: a quake or outage hits everyone (textbook; see crucibles in SAND)
  - long qualification: each fab qualifies wafers per process, so switching supplier takes many months
  - the market runs two-speed: AI epi and HBM wafers strong, mature-node wafers soft ('two-track', SEMI)
  - 2025 revenue fell 1.2% while area rose 5.8%: price per sq in is falling, a margin risk for makers
  - China is building domestic 300 mm wafer capacity (see the China landscape report in Sources)

  Wafer sizes in use (textbook): 200 mm and 300 mm dominate volume; 150 mm and below are legacy.
  Variant ladder: bulk polished  <  epi (adds a device-quality layer)  <  SOI (adds buried oxide,
      bonded).
  Each step up costs more and has fewer qualified suppliers (Soitec is the SOI name in the share table).

# 8. How it connects

  upstream: SLICE + POLISH makes the surface; POLYSILICON + MELT + PULL make the crystal.
  downstream: every DEPOSIT, LITHO and ETCH step starts from this flat reference. Flatness feeds focus
  budget at the scanner, so SFQR is a lithography number as much as a wafer one.
