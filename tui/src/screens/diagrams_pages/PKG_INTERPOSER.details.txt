== COWOS-S
# What it does
CoWoS-S uses one full silicon interposer with fine copper wiring and TSVs, made in a fab on a mature node.
# Why it matters
TSMC lists it up to about 3.3x reticle size (about 2,700 mm2); beyond that it recommends CoWoS-L or CoWoS-R.
# Source
3dfabric.tsmc.com CoWoS page.

== COWOS-R
# What it does
CoWoS-R uses a redistribution-layer interposer of polymer and copper instead of silicon.
# Why it matters
It is cheaper and more flexible but its wiring is coarser than silicon's.
# Source
semiconductorx.com (secondary); SemiWiki.

== COWOS-L
# What it does
CoWoS-L puts small silicon bridges only where dies meet, inside a larger organic interposer.
# Why it matters
It lets packages grow beyond one silicon slab; NVIDIA's Blackwell uses it for its two-die, ~10 TB/s link.
# Source
Tom's Hardware; TrendForce.

== LSI BRIDGE
# What it does
A local silicon interconnect bridge is a small silicon chip embedded under the joint between two dies, carrying their fine wires.
# Why it matters
Silicon only where the fine wiring is needed keeps big packages affordable.
# Source
Tom's Hardware on CoWoS-L.

== TSV
# What it does
Through-silicon vias are copper columns through the interposer that carry power and signals down to the C4 bumps and substrate.
# Why it matters
Every amp the logic die draws comes up through the TSV array.
# Source
TSMC 3DFabric; textbook.

== RDL
# What it does
Redistribution layers are copper wiring in polymer, used for CoWoS-R interposers and on the substrate side.
# Why it matters
RDL is cheaper than silicon wiring but coarser.
# Source
semiconductorx.com (secondary).

== RETICLE MULTIPLE
# What it does
Interposer size is counted in reticle fields: about 3.3x today for CoWoS-S, 9.5x planned for 2027 and over 14x by 2029.
# Why it matters
Bigger interposers carry more HBM (up to 24 HBM5E stacks planned) but fit fewer per wafer.
# Source
Tom's Hardware, TSMC CoWoS roadmap.

== WIRING DENSITY
# What it does
Silicon wiring is fine enough to route a 1024-bit HBM3 bus per stack across a few millimetres to the GPU.
# Why it matters
That density is what makes HBM bandwidth possible and why a silicon interposer or bridge is needed.
# Source
JEDEC JESD238 (1024-bit HBM3).
