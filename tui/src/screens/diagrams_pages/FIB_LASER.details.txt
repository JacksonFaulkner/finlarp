== DFB LASER
# What it does
A distributed-feedback laser has a grating etched along its cavity that reflects only one wavelength, so it emits a single, narrow line of light.
# Why it matters
A single wavelength spreads less in fiber, so the pulses stay sharp over kilometres; it is the light source inside an EML.
# Source
Textbook (Agrawal).

== EML
# What it does
An electro-absorption modulated laser puts a DFB laser and an electro-absorption modulator on one InP chip: the laser runs steadily and the modulator absorbs part of the light to set each level.
# Why it matters
EMLs drive most single-mode data-center modules; 200G-per-lane EMLs have been short, with lead times reported beyond 2027.
# Source
TrendForce, December 2025.

== VCSEL
# What it does
A vertical-cavity surface-emitting laser sends light straight up out of the chip from between two mirror stacks, into multimode fiber.
# Why it matters
VCSELs are cheap and low power and can be tested on the wafer, but only reach about 100 m in multimode fiber, so they serve in-row links.
# Source
Textbook.

== SILICON PHOTONICS
# What it does
Silicon photonics builds waveguides, splitters, modulators and germanium photodiodes in a silicon fab. Silicon can't emit light, so an external InP laser supplies it.
# Why it matters
It scales with silicon manufacturing and is the main alternative to scarce EMLs; co-packaged optics depend on it.
# Source
LEDinside / TrendForce, December 2025.

== MACH-ZEHNDER
# What it does
A Mach-Zehnder modulator splits light into two arms, delays one relative to the other with a voltage, and recombines them so the halves add or cancel.
# Why it matters
It sets PAM4 brightness levels in silicon photonics and tolerates temperature well, but is larger and needs more drive than a ring modulator.
# Source
Textbook.

== CW LASER
# What it does
A continuous-wave laser runs steadily and carries no data; it supplies light to a silicon modulator, which adds the data.
# Why it matters
CW lasers plus silicon modulators became the main alternative for cloud providers facing EML shortages, moving demand to high-power InP CW lasers.
# Source
LEDinside / TrendForce, December 2025.

== INDIUM PHOSPHIDE
# What it does
Indium phosphide is the compound semiconductor that 1310 nm and 1550 nm lasers are grown on; its band gap emits at those wavelengths.
# Why it matters
Every optical route still needs InP lasers; substrate supply is concentrated, and one report puts China at about 70% of indium production.
# Source
Report cited by KuCoin research; Reuters (via the same).

== WAVELENGTH
# What it does
Data-center single-mode links run at 1310 nm (the O-band), where standard fiber spreads pulses least; multimode VCSEL links run at 850 nm.
# Why it matters
Wavelength decides the fiber, the laser material and the reach; long-haul coherent links use 1550 nm where loss is lowest.
# Source
Corning SMF-28 Ultra; textbook.
