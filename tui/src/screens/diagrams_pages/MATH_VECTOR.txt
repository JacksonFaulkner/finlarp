# 1. A vector is an ordered list of numbers

      [ 2 ]
   v  [ 5 ]     3 slots, so v is a 3-vector
      [ 1 ]

  (written in a row: v = [2, 5, 1]. Slot 1 is 2, slot 2 is 5, slot 3 is 1.)

# 2. A pair of numbers is a point, or an arrow from the origin

   In 2D, [3, 4] means: 3 steps right, then 4 steps up.

     4 ┤                ●  [3, 4]
     3 ┤
     2 ┤
     1 ┤
     0 ┼────────┬───┬───┬───┬
                1   2   3   4

  (the arrow runs from the origin 0 to the dot)

# 3. Where vectors show up

  A position or direction in space, a row of prices, or a 3-number colour (red, green, blue).
  Every AI model stores its inputs and its learned weights as vectors.
