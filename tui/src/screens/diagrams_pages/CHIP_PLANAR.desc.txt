The planar MOSFET carried logic from the 1970s to 28nm: a flat gate over a flat channel. Dennard scaling kept power density flat until about 2005, then leakage and short-channel effects (DIBL) ended it, so the industry added strained silicon, high-k metal gates and FD-SOI before moving to FinFET. Planar at 28nm and above is still a large share of wafers. UMC, GlobalFoundries (GFS), SMIC and TSMC (TSM) serve it; Soitec makes FD-SOI wafers.
