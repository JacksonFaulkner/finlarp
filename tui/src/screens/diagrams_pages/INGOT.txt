# 3. The crystal physics behind the cuts

  Orientation   Miller index sets which atomic plane faces up. {100} for standard logic / CMOS,
                {111} for bipolar, power and MEMS (Wikipedia infobox). Set by the seed in MELT + PULL.
  Notch         wafers of 200 mm and up carry one small notch instead of flats, so no area is wasted;
                it marks <110> and does not show doping type. 450 mm was to be notchless (laser mark).
  Doping        boron, phosphorus, arsenic or antimony added to the melt: 1e13 to 1e16 atoms/cm3
                (Wikipedia). Purity still above 99.9999%, transition metals below ppb.
  Cropping      seed cone and tail cone are removed (cropping / lapping / grinding), also removing zones
                with voids or contamination (patent text). Resistivity check step: no source found.
  Grinding      a grind wheel brings the ingot to target outer diameter and length (patent text).
  Slicing       wire saw, diamond wire; the wafers are then machined for flatness, edge-ground to avoid
                cracking, chamfered, etched and polished.

# 4. By the numbers (300 mm wafer, SEMI-style)

  diameter          300 mm, tolerance +/- 0.2 mm (vendor datasheet)
  thickness         775 um (introduced 1999); vendor sheets show +/- 25 um, a drawing shows +/- 20 um
  mass              about 125 g per wafer (Wikipedia)
  notch             depth 1.0 mm (+0.25 / -0.00), angle 90 deg, orientation <110> +/- 1 deg
  die per wafer     640 dies of 100 mm2 (Wikipedia, ideal count)
  ingot size        up to 2 m long, several hundred kg (Wikipedia); 450 mm would weigh ~1 tonne
  governing spec    SEMI M1 for polished single-crystal wafers (tables not fetched)
  not found         kerf loss, wafers per ingot, saw speeds: no source gave them, so none are shown

# 8. Market structure and risks

  - Five firms make nearly all wafers (82-94% depending on source and year). Qualification takes
    chipmakers years, so new entrants rarely win share.
  - Industry swings: SEMI says 2023 wafer revenue fell 10.9% to $12.3B and area shipments fell
    14.3% to 12,602 million sq in; polished 300 mm -13%, epi 300 mm -5%.
  - Pricing: long-term agreements (LTAs). Shin-Etsu said LTA prices should rise on capex and raw
    material costs; fixed-LTA customers see no 2024 increase, step-up LTAs may.
  - Upstream chokepoints: crucible quartz (Spruce Pine, see SAND) and electronic-grade polysilicon.
  - Geographic: Japan (Shin-Etsu, SUMCO), Taiwan (GlobalWafers), Germany (Siltronic), Korea (SK Siltron).
