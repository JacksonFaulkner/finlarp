# 4. Why contact resistance became the bottleneck

 ┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
 │ Total R seen by a transistor ~ channel + S/D epi + CONTACT + local wire                         │
 │ plug width    ~15-20 nm; electron mean free path in W is 19.1 nm [3], so surfaces scatter       │
 │ liner + TiN   a few nm per side of a ~15 nm hole: much of the width is not conductor (textbook) │
 │ interface     Schottky barrier to n-Si: NiSi 0.65-0.7 eV, TiSi2 0.58 eV (patents) [4]           │
 │ area          contact area falls with pitch squared, so rho_c / A climbs fast                   │
 └─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

 ┌─────────────────────┐  ┌─────────────────────┐  ┌────────────────────┐
 │ CONTACT RESISTANCE  │  │ LINER SQUEEZE       │  │ BARRIER TUNING     │
 │ R = rho_c / A       │─▶│ TiN does not shrink │─▶│ SBH 0.7 -> 0.34 eV │
 │ + plug body + liner │  │ plug body R up      │  │ dopant segregation │
 └─────────────────────┘  └─────────────────────┘  └────────────────────┘

# 5. By the numbers

  W mean free path        19.1 nm at 293 K (PRB 2012, DFT) [3]
  Bulk resistivity        W ~5.6, Co ~6.2, Ru ~7.1, Mo ~5.3 uOhm-cm; Cu ~1.7 (textbook, approximate)
  Contact rho_c           pFET ~3e-9, nFET ~1e-8 ohm-cm2 (older patent background) [4]
  Schottky barrier        NiSi 0.65-0.7 eV; TiSi2 0.58 eV; N2+ pre-silicide implant 0.7 -> 0.34 eV [4]
  Applied Volta W CVD     "up to 90%" lower contact resistance, CD dependent (vendor claim) [5]
  Applied selective W     >40% contact-resistance drop vs conventional W in early via use (vendor) [8]
  Intel 10nm (2017)       Co local interconnect: 2x via resistance, 5-10x electromigration [6]
  Intel 10nm COAG         ~10% standard-cell height reduction (Intel claim, secondary) [7]
  TSMC N2 (IEDM 2024)     better gate-contact R and up to 20% R+C cut in MoL, as reported [9]

# 7. Who makes it, structure, risks

  Applied Materials (AMAT)  Endura PVD/CVD/ALD, Co and selective W; FY2025 $28.37B [1]
  Lam Research (LRCX)       ALTUS CVD+ALD W and Mo for contacts, vias, wordlines; FY2025 $18.44B [2]
  TSMC (TSM), Intel (INTC)  design the contact scheme; Intel first with Co + COAG on 10nm (2017) [6][7]
  Samsung, SK hynix, Micron buy the tools; Tokyo Electron (8035.T) sells CVD/ALD (textbook)
  SNPS, CDNS                extract contact/via RC in EDA; ARM, NVDA, AMD lose frequency to it (textbook)
  Risks: new metals (Mo/Ru) need new tools and qualification; barrierless fill raises adhesion and
  diffusion risk; contact RC erodes node-to-node gains (textbook).
