== zHBM roadmap
# What it does
Three phases: compute inside the memory stack first, then DRAM stacked directly on the processor.
# Why it matters
Removes the interposer shoreline limit: the memory no longer has to sit beside the GPU.
# Source
Tom's Hardware, Hot Chips 2026 coverage.

== Hybrid copper bonding
# What it does
Joins copper pads directly between dies or wafers, with no solder bump.
# Why it matters
Higher I/O density in a shorter stack; the enabling step for zHBM.
# Source
SemiEngineering, HBM4 sticks with microbumps (hybrid bonding is not yet in volume).

== HBM5 timing
# What it does
The JEDEC HBM5 spec is not final; zHBM commercialization is expected around 2028.
# Why it matters
A roadmap date, not a product: volume depends on the spec and on bonding yield.
# Source
Tom's Hardware, Hot Chips 2026 coverage.
